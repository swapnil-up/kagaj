Federal Parliament
House of Representatives, 2079
Sixth Session
A Brief Overview of Activities
The Sixth Session of the House of Representatives commenced on Friday, Baishakh 12, 2082, and continued until Friday, Bhadra 27, 2082. During this period, it convened for a total of 141 days, with 61 meetings held over 219 hours and 50 minutes of parliamentary proceedings. Additionally, both houses of the Federal Parliament held two joint meetings, totaling 3 hours and 15 minutes.

During this session, the Honorable President presented the Government of Nepal's Policy and Program for the fiscal year 2082/083. A motion of thanks for the President's address, delivered at a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Baishakh 19, 2082, was passed by the House of Representatives on the same day. Subsequently, the Government of Nepal's Policy and Program for fiscal year 2082/083, presented through the Honorable President's address, was discussed in the House of Representatives from Baishakh 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, and 28, 2082, and was passed by a majority.

The Annual Estimate of Revenue and Expenditure for fiscal year 2082/083, presented in a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Jestha 15, 2082, was discussed in the House on Asar 1 and 2, 2082. The Honorable Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister, Bishnu Prasad Paudel, provided a response on Asar 2, 2082.

Following clause-by-clause discussions on the "Appropriation Bill, 2082" presented in the House meeting on Asar 2, 2082, held on Asar 2, 5, 6, and 7, 2082, the relevant Honorable Ministers provided responses on Asar 8, 9, and 10, 2082. The bill was subsequently passed by a majority on Asar 10, 2082. Additionally, the "Economic Survey of Fiscal Year 2081/082" and the "Annual Review of the Status of Public Enterprises, 2082" were presented to the House.

Discussions were held in the House on Asar 30, 31, Jestha 1, and 2, 2082, regarding the principles and priorities (excluding tax proposals) of the Appropriation Bill for fiscal year 2082/083, which was presented on Asar 28, 2082. The Honorable Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister, Bishnu Prasad Paudel, provided a response.

In the question-and-answer session directly with the Prime Minister, held on Saun 7, 2082, in accordance with Chapter-9 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, the Honorable Prime Minister K.P. Sharma Oli responded to the questions raised by the Honorable Members.

During this session, 10 government bills were registered in the House of Representatives. Of these, 3 bills were passed by both houses of the Federal Parliament, 2 bills were passed by the House of Representatives and sent to the National Assembly, 4 bills were under consideration in committees, and the theoretical discussion on the "Veterinary Council Nepal (First Amendment) Bill" was completed. Furthermore, among the 18 government bills and 2 non-government bills received from previous sessions, 4 government bills were passed by the Federal Parliament. Messages received from the National Assembly regarding the "Federal Civil Service Bill" and the "Water Resources Bill" were tabled in the House. The report of the committee on the "School Education Bill" was presented to the House. 10 bills remained under consideration in the respective committees, and 2 non-government bills were only registered. The "Constitutional Council (Work, Duties, Rights, and Procedures) (First Amendment) Bill, 2079," returned by the Honorable President with a message, was only tabled in the House.

Similarly, the "Ship (Operation and Management) Bill, 2081," originating in the National Assembly and received by the House of Representatives, is under consideration in the relevant committee. Among the 7 bills received from the National Assembly in previous sessions, the "Shaheed Dasharath Chand Health Sciences University Bill, 2080" was passed by the House and returned. 5 bills were under consideration in the respective committees, and the "National Dignity Bill" was only presented to the House.

Out of 177 oral and 10 written questions related to various ministries, registered by Honorable Members during the current session of the House of Representatives, answers were provided for 164 oral questions and 9 written questions. Responses from the relevant Honorable Ministers to a total of 48 oral questions concerning various ministries were completed in the meetings held on Jestha 5, Asar 24 and 30, Saun 20 and 28, and Bhadra 6 and 19, 2082. Although the remaining 116 oral and 9 written questions, for which answers were received before the dissolution of the House of Representatives, were not tabled in the House, they have been published through the Secretariat's website.

The term of the seven-member "Special Committee formed to study and investigate the alleged errors in the report of the Federal Civil Service Bill," which was formed on Asar 23, 2082, with a term of 21 days, was extended by another 7 days through a proposal approved in the meeting held on Saun 9, 2082. The special committee also submitted its report to the House on Saun 20, 2082.

On Saun 19, 2082, the "Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies of the World Trade Organization" was discussed and ratified by a majority in the House.

During this session, the "Annual Report of the Government of Nepal for Fiscal Years 2079/080 and 2080/081" in accordance with Article 53 of the Constitution of Nepal and Section 41 of the Good Governance (Management and Operation) Act, 2064, the "62nd Annual Report of the Auditor General, 2082," and the "Fourth Annual Report of the National Dalit Commission, Fiscal Year 2080/081" were presented.

During this session, the "Annual Report of the International Relations and Tourism Committee, 2082," the "Annual Report of the Law, Justice, and Human Rights Committee, 2081/082," the "Annual Report of the Infrastructure Development Committee, 2081," the "Annual Report of the Agriculture, Cooperatives, and Natural Resources Committee, 2082," the "Annual Report of the Women and Social Affairs Committee (Fiscal Year 2080/081)," and the "23rd Annual Report of the Public Accounts Committee, 2082" were presented. Additionally, the report of the Public Accounts Committee, in accordance with Sub-section (2) of Section 41 of the Financial Procedures and Fiscal Responsibility Act, 2076, was approved by the House.

During this Sixth Session, the House passed condolence motions on the demise of members Pradip Nepal, Tirtharam Dangol, Ganesh Bahadur Khadka, Birendra Juwachan, Dundiraj Sharma Poudyal, Govinda Sharma Kandel, Ram Ayodhya Prasad Yadav, Khadga Bahadur Basnyat, and Khagaraj Sharma.

During this period, the Honorable Prime Minister K.P. Sharma Oli informed the House about his visit to Spain from Asar 16 to 19, 2082, to participate in the Fourth International Conference on Financing for Development of the United Nations; his visit to Spain in general; issues including SEE results; his visit to Turkmenistan from Saun 18 to 23, 2082, to participate in the Third United Nations Conference of Landlocked Developing Countries (LLDCs) and discussions related to their lack of sea access and their remoteness and isolation from the world market; the status of electricity exports; the current state of road infrastructure; the status of the under-construction parapet and floodlights at Tribhuvan University Cricket Stadium; visit visas; alleged political revenge against individuals in pre-trial detention during his visit to the East; the country's economic situation; the government's ongoing work; and contemporary issues; and his upcoming visit to China and related meetings.

Furthermore, the Honorable Home Minister Ramesh Lekhak responded to issues raised during the emergency, zero, and special hour sessions, including the visit visa ಪ್ರಕರಣ, the matter of some individuals who accompanied the Honorable Prime Minister on his recent visit to Spain not returning to the country, the loss of life and property due to floods in the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli rivers, efforts in relief and rescue, and rehabilitation work. The Minister of Agriculture and Livestock Development, Ramnath Adhikari, responded regarding the supply and distribution status of chemical fertilizers to be distributed to farmers as subsidies. The Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Ajay Kumar Chaurasia, responded regarding the draft amendments to the Muluki Criminal Code, 2074, the Muluki Criminal Procedure Code, 2074, and the Muluki Civil Procedure Code, 2074, which are under consideration in the Council of Ministers' bill committee, and amendments made by the government to issues such as polygamy, presidential pardons, and the age of marriage, as well as the ministry's work.

The House of Representatives was dissolved by the Honorable President effective from 11:00 PM on Friday, Bhadra 27, 2082, thus automatically concluding the current session.
