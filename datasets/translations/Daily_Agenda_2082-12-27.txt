Daily Agenda
Friday, Chaitra 27, 2082
4:00 PM
Letter from the Office of the President
The Honorable Speaker will read out the letter from the Office of the President regarding the reallocation of portfolios of the Council of Ministers.
1. To be Tabled
The Honorable Prime Minister, Balendra Shah, will table the copy of the address made by the Honorable President to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Chaitra 27, 2082.
2. Vote of Thanks
The Honorable Speaker will present the Vote of Thanks for the address made by the Honorable President.
3. Election of the Deputy Speaker
The Honorable Jan Bahadur Shahi will present the following proposal:-
"Honorable Member Saraswati Lama be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives."
The proposal will be supported by Honorable Tahir Ali Bhat, Honorable Khushbu Oli, and Honorable Bharat Giri.
The Honorable Aaren Rai will present the following proposal:-
"Honorable Member Ruby Kumari be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives."
The proposal will be supported by Honorable Ashok Kumar Chaudhary and Honorable Ambika Devi Sangraula.
4. Formation of Joint Committee
The Honorable Speaker will nominate members to the Parliamentary Hearing Committee and the Committee for Monitoring and Evaluating the Implementation of State Directive Principles, Policies, and Responsibilities, in accordance with the Federal Parliament Joint Meeting and Joint Committee (Operation) Rules, 2080.
5. Formation of Thematic Committees
The Honorable Speaker will propose the nomination of members to 10 thematic committees in accordance with Rule 173 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079.
6. Letter from the President
The Honorable Speaker will read out the letter received from the Honorable President.
