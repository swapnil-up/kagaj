Federal Parliament, House of Representatives, 2082 Second Session Meeting Number - 38 National Nepal Daily Agenda Parliament,
Wednesday, Asar 31, 2083, 1:00 PM
Letter from the Office of the President: The Honourable Speaker will read out the letter received from the Office of the President regarding the certification of bills. Presentation of Annual Report: On behalf of the Honourable Prime Minister Pushpa Kamal Dahal, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam will present: (a) The Eighteenth Annual Report of the National Human Rights Commission (from Shrawan 2081 to Ashadh end 2082). (b) The Annual Report of the National Information Commission for the Fiscal Year 2081/082. (c) The Seventh Annual Report of the National Inclusion Commission for the Fiscal Year 2081/082.
Management Work: Honourable Minister of Finance Dr. Swarnim Wagle will present a proposal to "Consider the Bill to Amend and Repeal Certain Nepal Laws Related to Finance, 2083".
Discussion and Ratification of Treaties: Honourable Minister of Youth, Labour, and Employment Ramji Yadav will present: (a) A proposal to "Discuss the provisions of Protocol No. 29 concerning the Forced Labour Convention, 1930 of the International Labour Organization, 2014 in the House." (b) A proposal to "Ratify Protocol No. 29 concerning the Forced Labour Convention, 1930 of the International Labour Organization, 2014." (c) A proposal to "Discuss the provisions of the Labour Inspection Convention, 1947 (No. 81) of the International Labour Organization in the House." (d) A proposal to "Ratify the Labour Inspection Convention, 1947 (No. 81) of the International Labour Organization." Padcha Prasad Pandey Secretary General
