Presentation of Special Motion
The Honorable Speaker will present the following special motion:-
Special Motion
"This meeting of the House of Representatives deeply condoles the incident of August 24, 2026 (Bhadra 10, 2083, Wednesday) where floods in the BhoteKoshi-Trishuli rivers caused many citizens, both Nepali and foreign, to go missing and a large number of deaths, resulting in extensive loss of life and property in areas along the Trishuli riverbanks in Timure, Syabrubesi, Betrawati of Rasuwa, Trishuli Bazaar and Devighat of Nuwakot, as well as in Dhading, Chitwan, and Gorkha districts. Furthermore, it wishes for the management of those who have been rendered homeless by the said incident, the speedy recovery of the injured, and eternal peace for the souls of the deceased, and expresses heartfelt condolences to the bereaved families."
- Ram Prasad Pandey
Secretary-General
