Commencement of Discussion on the Policy and Program
1. The Right Honourable Prime Minister Sher Bahadur Deuba will present a proposal to commence the discussion on the annual policy and program of the Government of Nepal for the fiscal year 2083/084, as mentioned in the address made by the Right Honourable President to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, 28th Baisakh, 2083.
2. Amendments proposed by Honourable Members on the annual policy and program of the Government of Nepal for the fiscal year 2083/084 will be presented, and a discussion will be held on the annual policy and program of the Government of Nepal for the fiscal year 2083/084, as mentioned in the address made by the Right Honourable President to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, 28th Baisakh, 2083.
3. The Right Honourable Prime Minister Sher Bahadur Deuba will respond to the questions raised during the discussion on the annual policy and program of the Government of Nepal for the fiscal year 2083/084, as mentioned in the address made by the Right Honourable President to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, 28th Baisakh, 2083.
4. The amendments received on the annual policy and program of the Government of Nepal for the fiscal year 2083/084 will be presented for decision.
5. The Right Honourable Speaker will present the annual policy and program of the Government of Nepal for the fiscal year 2083/084, as mentioned in the address made by the Right Honourable President to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, 28th Baisakh, 2083, for decision.
