Discussion on Policy and Program
1. Discussion will be held on the Government of Nepal's annual policy and program for the fiscal year 2083/084, as mentioned in the address by the Right Honorable President to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2083.
2. The Honorable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle, on behalf of the Right Honorable Prime Minister, will respond to the questions raised during the discussion on the Government of Nepal's annual policy and program for the fiscal year 2083/084, as mentioned in the address by the Right Honorable President to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2083.
3. Proposals for amendments to the Government of Nepal's annual policy and program for the fiscal year 2083/084 will be presented for decision.
4. The Right Honorable Speaker will present the Government of Nepal's annual policy and program for the fiscal year 2083/084, as mentioned in the address by the Right Honorable President to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2083, for decision.
Presentation of Principles and Priorities for the Appropriation Bill
4. The Honorable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle will present a proposal for discussion on "Principles and Priorities for the Appropriation Bill for the Fiscal Year 2083/084 (excluding tax proposals)".
Discussion on Report
6. The Honorable Chairperson of the House of Representatives Regulations Drafting Committee, Ganesh Parajuli, will present a proposal for consideration on "Report of the House of Representatives Regulations Drafting Committee, 2083".
