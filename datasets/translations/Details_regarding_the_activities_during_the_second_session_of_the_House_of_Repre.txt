Federal Parliament
House of Representatives, 2082
Second Session
Brief Information on Work Done
Honorable members, the Second Session of the House of Representatives commenced on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2083, and continued for a total of 145 days until Friday, Ashwin 16, 2083. Of these, 62 days were spent in 68 meetings, with a total of 277 hours and 30 minutes dedicated to parliamentary proceedings. Additionally, a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament was held on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2083, and Friday, Jestha 15, 2083, for a total of 2 hours and 55 minutes.
2. In the first meeting of this session on Baishakh 28, 2083, the order to remove obstacles, issued on Thursday, Baishakh 3, 2083, was approved by a majority vote in accordance with Rule 260 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, using the authority granted by Rules 260 and 261 of the said regulations.
3. Among the ordinances presented in the first meeting: "Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2083", "Special Arrangement Ordinance for the Removal from Office of Public Office-Bearers, 2083", "Constitutional Council (Work, Duties, Authority, and Procedures) (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2083", "Money Laundering Prevention (Third Amendment) Ordinance, 2083", "Ordinance to Amend Some Nepal Laws, 2083", "Cooperative (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2083", "Ordinance to Amend Some Nepal Laws Related to Health Sciences Institutions, 2083", and "Ordinance to Amend Some Nepal Laws Related to Universities, 2083", the following were approved in the meeting held on Ashar 5, 2083: "Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2083", "Money Laundering Prevention (Third Amendment) Ordinance, 2083", "Cooperative (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2083", and "Ordinance to Amend Some Nepal Laws Related to Health Sciences Institutions, 2083".
4. The House of Representatives, in its meeting on Baishakh 28, 2083, passed a motion of thanks for the address delivered by the Right Honorable President in the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on the same day, presenting the government's policies and programs for the fiscal year 2083/084. Furthermore, the government's policies and programs for the fiscal year 2083/084, presented through the Right Honorable President's address, were discussed and unanimously passed in the meeting held on Baishakh 31, 2083.

5. In the first meeting of the session on Baishakh 28, 2083, the "Report of the House of Representatives Regulations Drafting Committee, 2083", presented by Honorable Chairperson Ganesh Parajuli of the House of Representatives Regulations Drafting Committee formed in the first session, was discussed in the House and passed with amendments by a majority vote on Jestha 17, 2083.
6. Discussions on the principles and priorities for the Appropriation Bill for the fiscal year 2083/084 (excluding tax proposals) were held on Jestha 4 and 5, 2083, after which the Honorable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle provided a response. Additionally, the "Economic Survey of Fiscal Year 2082/083" and "Annual Review of the Status of Public Enterprises, 2082" were presented in the meeting on Jestha 13, 2083.
7. In the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament held on Jestha 15, 2083, the annual budget estimate for revenue and expenditure for the fiscal year 2083/084 was discussed in the meetings of Jestha 27 and 28, 2083. Following the discussions, the Honorable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle provided a response on Jestha 28, 2083.
8. The "Appropriation Bill, 2083", presented in the meeting of Jestha 28, 2083, was discussed clause by clause in the meetings of Ashar 1 and 2, 2083. After responses were provided by the concerned Honorable Ministers on Ashar 3 and 4, the bill was passed by a majority vote on Ashar 4, 2083.
9. A total of 35 government bills were registered in the House of Representatives during the Second Session. Among these, the "Bill to Amend the Election of the House of Representatives Members (First Amendment), 2082", "Voter List (First Amendment) Bill, 2082", "Finance Bill, 2083", "Appropriation Bill, 2083", "Bill to Raise Public Debt, 2083", "Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Bill, 2083", "Money Laundering Prevention (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083", "Cooperative (First Amendment) Bill, 2083", "Bill to Amend Some Nepal Laws Related to Health Sciences Institutions, 2083", and "Bill to Revive Previous Provisions of the Nepal Special Service Act, 2042, 2083" have been passed by both houses of the Federal Parliament. Twenty bills are under consideration in the relevant committees of the House of Representatives. The "Bill to Mobilize Alternative Development Finance, 2082", received from the first session of the House, has been passed by both houses of the Federal Parliament.
10. Similarly, the "Federal Civil Service Bill, 2083" is only registered, while the "Diploma Engineer Council Bill, 2083" has been presented to the House. The report of the committee related to the "Nepal Rastra Bank (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083" has been received, and the committee's report related to the "Bill Concerning Remuneration, Service Conditions, and Facilities of Office Bearers of Constitutional Bodies (First Amendment), 2083" has been presented to the House. The "National Cadet Corps Bill, 2083" has been passed only by the House of Representatives. The "National Forensic Science Laboratory (Establishment and Operation) Bill, 2082", passed by the first session of the National Assembly, has also been passed by the House of Representatives. Among the 4 bills received from the National Assembly, 4 are under consideration in their respective committees. Thus, 12 bills have been passed by both houses of the Federal Parliament in this session, and 19 bills have been authenticated by the Right Honorable President.
11. In this session, the "Treaty between the Government of Nepal and the Government of the Republic of India on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters" has been presented to the House in accordance with the Nepal Treaties Act, 2047. The "International Labour Organization Convention on Labour Inspection, 1947 No. 81", "Protocol of 2014 to the ILO Forced Labour Convention, 1930 No. 29", the proposal to join the "Council of Asian Legislative Institutions (CALI)" to be presented in the House of Representatives of the Federal Parliament, and the "Agreement on the Conservation and Sustainable Use of Marine Biological Diversity of Areas Beyond National Jurisdiction under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea" have been approved by the House.
12. In the meeting held on Jestha 17, 2083, the Right Honorable Prime Minister Balendra Shah responded to questions raised by Honorable members during the impromptu session. Similarly, in the meeting held on Bhadra 17, 2083, information was provided to the House regarding the government's actions in rescue, relief, and rehabilitation following the floods in the BhoteKoshi-Trishuli rivers; the assertion that climate change is the primary cause of the BhoteKoshi-Trishuli disaster and that its mitigation and management is a shared responsibility of the global community; the government's efforts to protect its citizens post-disaster; and the need to provide incentives to the army, armed police, and Nepal police involved in rescue operations.
13. During this period, in accordance with Article 294 of the Constitution of Nepal, the following reports were presented to the House: "Report of the Auditor General, 2083", "Annual Report of the Attorney General, 2081/082", "Thirty-fifth Annual Report of the Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority, 2081/082", "Nineteenth Annual Report of the Election Commission, 2081/082", "Eighth Annual Report of the National Security Council, 2081/082", "Eighteenth Annual Report of the National Women Commission, 2081/082", "Annual Report of the Judicial Council, Fiscal Year 2081/082", "Annual Report of the Judicial Service Commission, Fiscal Year 2081/082", "Sixty-sixth Annual Report of the Public Service Commission (Shrawan 2081 - Ashadh 2082)", "Eighteenth Annual Report of the National Human Rights Commission (Shrawan 2081 - Ashadh 2082)", "Annual Report of the National Information Commission, Fiscal Year 2081/082", "Seventh Annual Report of the National Inclusion Commission, Fiscal Year 2081/082", "Sixteenth Annual Report of the Indigenous Nationalities Commission, Fiscal Year 2081/082", "Seventh Annual Report of the Tharu Commission, Fiscal Year 2081/082", and "Eighth Annual Report of the National Natural Resources and Fiscal Commission, 2083".
14. In various meetings of the House, Honorable ministers responded to issues raised during impromptu, zero, and special times. The Honorable Minister of Communications and Information Technology, Dr. Vikram Timilsina, responded twice; the Honorable Minister of Youth, Labour, and Employment, Ramji Yadav, responded once; the Honorable Minister of Industry, Commerce, and Supplies, Gauri Kumari, responded once; the Honorable Minister of Agriculture, Forests, and Environment, Gita Chaudhary, responded once; the Honorable Minister of Foreign Affairs, Shishir Khanal, responded four times; the Honorable Minister of Energy, Water Resources, and Irrigation, Biraj Bhakta Shrestha, responded once; the Honorable Minister of Physical Infrastructure Development, Sunil Lamshal, responded once; the Honorable Home Minister, Sudhan Gurung, responded twice; the Honorable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, responded once; the Honorable Minister of Health and Population, Nisha Mehta, responded three times; the Honorable Minister of Education and Sports, Sumana Pokharel, responded twice; and the newly appointed Honorable Minister of Industry, Commerce, and Supplies, Deepak Kumar Shah, responded once.
15. Among the five urgent public importance proposals registered in the Second Session, a proposal presented by Honorable Bhimraj Aaidambe in the meeting of Saun 19, 2083, stated the necessity of identifying landless, squatters, and irregularly settled citizens, ensuring their right to safe housing, basic services, food, health, education, and livelihood, providing appropriate compensation and rehabilitation guarantees, and distributing land ownership certificates to landless and genuine squatters as soon as possible. Following discussions on this proposal, the Honorable Minister of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Poverty Alleviation, Pratibha Rawal, provided a response. Additionally, three resolution proposals were registered during this period.

16. In the meetings of Saun 25, Bhadra 2, 8, and 30, 2083, question and answer sessions related to 13 issues concerning the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Ministry of Youth, Labour and Employment, Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation, Ministry of Agriculture, Forests and Environment, Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation, Ministry of Industry, Commerce and Supplies, and Ministry of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Poverty Alleviation were completed in accordance with Chapter 8 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083. Similarly, answers to 16 oral questions and 8 written questions from ministries that were pending were tabled in the House today.
17. In various meetings of the session, the leader of the opposition party and leaders and officials of parliamentary parties represented in the House expressed their views on contemporary issues. Furthermore, during impromptu, zero, and special times in the meetings, Honorable members drew the attention of the government, the House, and concerned parties to various contemporary issues and expressed their views.
18. In the meeting held on Bhadra 17, 2083, a special proposal was passed expressing deep sorrow over the incident of the BhoteKoshi-Trishuli river floods, which resulted in the disappearance of a large number of domestic and foreign citizens, significant casualties, and extensive loss of life and property in the Trishuli riverine areas of Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Chitwan, and Gorkha districts. The proposal also conveyed condolences to the bereaved families and wished for the speedy recovery of the injured and eternal peace for the departed souls, and for the management of those who were displaced.
19. In the meeting held on Ashoj 5, 2083, a 15-member "Parliamentary Special Committee on the Impact of Climate Change in the Himalayas, Climate Justice, and BhoteKoshi-Trishuli Disaster" was formed, with a mandate to submit its report within three months.
20. During this period, the House passed condolence motions on the demise of former members Mangal Prasad Gupta, Rajendra Kharel, Ghanendra Basnet, former Member of Parliament and Constituent Assembly member Gopalman Shrestha, and former National Assembly member Shukraraj Shastri. The agenda for today's meeting also includes passing a condolence motion on the demise of former member Prakash Bahadur Gurung.
