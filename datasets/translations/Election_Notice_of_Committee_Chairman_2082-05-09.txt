Information regarding the Election of the Chairperson
This notice has been published for the information of the members of the House of Representatives as per Rule 176, Sub-rule (1) of the Rules of Procedure of the House of Representatives, 2079, regarding the vacant Chairperson's post within the Good Governance and State Affairs Committee under the House of Representatives. The Speaker has scheduled the election for Wednesday, Bhadra 22, 2082.
