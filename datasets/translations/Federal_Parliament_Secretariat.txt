Date: 2083/04/28
Notice regarding the appointment of staff for the private secretariat of the officials and honorable members of the Federal Parliament.
As per the arrangement regarding staff for the private secretariat, made by amending Schedule-2 of the Act Relating to Remuneration and Facilities of Officials and Members of the Federal Parliament, 2073, in accordance with the notice published in the Nepal Gazette on 2083/04/04, the Secretariat has published a notice on its notice board and website www.parliament.gov.np on 2083/04/05 for the officials and members of the Federal Parliament to appoint staff for their private secretariats. This is for information. Furthermore, if the documents required to be submitted as per law by the private secretaries appointed by the officials and members of the Federal Parliament before or after the publication of the aforementioned notice have not been submitted or are pending submission, you are requested to submit those documents to the Parliament Secretariat, Member Facilities Management Branch, as per the decision.
