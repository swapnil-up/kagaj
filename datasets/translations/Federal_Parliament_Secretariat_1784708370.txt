Information regarding the appointment of personal staff for the Right Honourable Speaker, Deputy Speaker, and Members of the Federal Parliament and the Secretariat's staff.
As per the notice published in the Nepal Gazette on the 4th and 5th of Shrawan, 2083, the Secretariat has made arrangements for the appointment of personal staff for the Right Honourable Speaker, Deputy Speaker, and Members of the Federal Parliament, and the Secretariat's staff, in accordance with the provisions related to remuneration and facilities for the Speaker, Deputy Speaker, and Members of the Federal Parliament Act, 2073, Schedule-2, and the Federal Parliament Secretariat Staff Regulations, 2064.
Interested Right Honourable Speaker, Deputy Speaker, and Members of the Federal Parliament are hereby informed that they can fill out the form available on the Parliament Secretariat, Member of Parliament Management Branch website or www.parliament.gov.np and submit it to the Member of Parliament Management Branch for the appointment of personal staff for their respective secretariats.
Documents and details required for the appointment of personal staff for the secretariat:-
1. Two passport-size photographs
2. Photocopy of Citizenship
3. Photocopy of Educational Certificate
4. Educational Qualification: As mentioned in Schedule-4 of the Federal Parliament Secretariat Staff Regulations, 2064, and the Administration provisions.
5. Photocopy of Citizenship and Educational Certificate to be attested by the applicant themselves. (Mention "Original is correct" "सक्लबमोजिम नक्कल ठिक छ")
6. Name, Bank Account Number, and Bank Name where remuneration is to be deposited.
7. Permanent Account Number/PAN
8. Mobile Number
9. National Identity Card Number (if available)
10. Applicant's Signature
