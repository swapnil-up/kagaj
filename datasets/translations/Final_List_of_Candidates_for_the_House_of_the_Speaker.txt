This notice is published for the information of all concerned as a notice of candidacy has been received as per the provisions. As per Rule 7 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2071, the period for submitting a notice of proposal to elect a Speaker of the House of Representatives has been set from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM on March 20, 2082 (2082/12/20). The time for submitting the proposal is 2:30 PM on 2082/12/20. At 11:05 AM, Dol Prasad Aryal of the Rastriya Swatantra Party, residing at Balaju, Ward No. 16, Kathmandu Metropolitan City, aged 51, has submitted the final list of registered candidates for the position of Speaker. The proposer is Hon. Rabi Lamichhane and the supporters are Hon. Sobita Gautam, Hon. Sunil Lamshal, and Hon. Swarnim Wagle. The notice was published by the Secretary-General (Patra Prasad Pandey).
