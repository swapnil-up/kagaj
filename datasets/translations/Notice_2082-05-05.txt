(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Thursday, Bhadra 5, 2082
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:10 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Devraj Ghimire.
At the beginning of the meeting, after the Honourable Members from Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats, the Right Honourable Speaker gave time to Hon. Shiva Nepali and Hon. Dhruv Bahadur Pradhan, who respectively stated that the Nepal Government should take prompt action to protect Nepal's territory, and that the general letter requesting the Honourable Deputy Speaker Indira Rana to schedule an interview date was not registered anywhere, hence the recommendation for a visit visa was not implemented. They further stated that it is inappropriate to repeatedly raise questions about a position like the Deputy Speaker, even when Rastriya Swatantra Party has demanded an independent investigation into the matter. They asserted that Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stand for constitutional process, rule of law, democratic dignity, and the people, and against injustice, corruption, and human trafficking. Therefore, in the visit visa case, involvement may extend beyond employees to the Ministry of Home Affairs, Ministry of Labour, Department of Immigration, and intermediaries. They declared that the movement will continue until an impartial investigation is conducted, the guilty are legally punished, and the victims receive justice.
After Hon. Dhruv Bahadur Pradhan finished speaking, the Honourable Members from Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party walked out of the meeting hall.
Subsequently, in the impromptu session, the following Honourable Members expressed their views on various issues: the agreement between India and China to operate India-China trade by using Nepal's territory of Lipulek without Nepal's consent and approval, which has come to light, and that the government should resolve such activities that harm Nepal's freedom, sovereignty, and independence through diplomatic and political discussions; a collective protest by the Parliament and all political parties against the Indian side's statement that Lipulek, Limpiyadhura, and Kalapani are not within Nepal's claim based on historical documents and evidence; the need for the federal government and relevant bodies to take necessary initiatives to clear the Rolpa-Rukum East road section blocked by landslides; and the requirement to stop the appointment of workers through contracts and outsourcing contrary to the law, and to ensure regular employment contracts for workers employed in industrial establishments as per the Labour Act.

Thereafter, Santosh Chalise, Honourable Chairperson of the Finance Committee, presented the "Report of the Committee on Mobilizing Alternative Development Finance, 2082".
Subsequently, the proposal put forth by Hon. Damodar Bhandari, Minister of Industry, Commerce and Supplies, to "send the Industrial Property Bill, 2082 to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion" was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was unanimously approved.
After that, the proposal put forth by Hon. Deven Dahal, Minister of Physical Infrastructure and Transport, to "send the Ship (Operation and Management) Bill, 2081, originating in the National Assembly, to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion" was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled to be held on Friday, Bhadra 6, 2082, at 1:00 PM, and the meeting was adjourned at 3:10 PM.
