Information Bulletin - 2
(Brief Minutes of the Meeting)
Friday, Bhadra 6, 2082
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 1:10 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Devraj Ghimire.
At the beginning of the meeting, after the Honourable Members of Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their respective seats, when the Right Honourable Speaker gave time to Hon. Nisha Dangi and Hon. Anisha Nepali, they respectively demanded an investigation by a high-level committee into the Visit Visa scam, warning the ruling party on behalf of Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party, and representing the voice of the people in the House to protect civil rights, stating that the government's characterization of the continuous movement and house boycott as a step against democratic practice is wrong, the government responsible for listening to and solving the people's problems is protecting individuals involved in crime, the agreement between India and China to open trade routes using Nepal's territory without consulting Nepal is not only disrespectful to Nepal but also contrary to international law and diplomatic norms, therefore the Nepal government must take diplomatic initiative with India and China to nullify the agreement, and until a high-level investigation commission is formed for the Visit Visa scam, they will boycott the House, among other issues, they expressed their views.
After Hon. Anisha Nepali finished speaking, the Honourable Members of Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party walked out of the meeting hall.
Subsequently, the following Honourable Members, during the impromptu session, respectively stated that initiatives should be taken for discussion and agreement between Nepal and China regarding risky lakes in the Nepal-China border region for monitoring, mitigation, and resolution, the Lumpy Skin disease infection is increasing day by day causing deaths of livestock, therefore the disease should be declared an epidemic and necessary vaccines and compensation should be provided to farmers, the issue of the government altering tax rates while the House is in session has come to light, therefore the Finance Minister must respond in the House, regarding the agreement to operate the India-China trade route via Lipulek, the government must inform the House, an impartial investigation must be conducted and the culprits brought to justice regarding the incident where a youth was stabbed and killed while bringing goods from India to Gaur, Rautahat, the Tharu language should also be included as an official language in government work in Koshi Province, the government must promptly take initiatives for the post-mortem of 2 men who died after falling from a cliff in Chumanu, Gorkha and for relief to the victim's families, the motorable road has not yet reached the extremely remote area of Chumanu Rural Municipality, therefore the foot trail should be managed immediately, among other issues, they expressed their views:-

Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the question-and-answer programs related to the Ministry of Home Affairs, Ministry of Youth and Sports, and Ministry of Forest and Environment, listed as item number 1 in today's agenda, have been removed from the list, and under the question-and-answer program as per Chapter-8 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, regarding the Ministry of Labour, Employment and Social Security, the Hon. Minister of Labour, Employment and Social Security, Sharad Singh Bhandari, responded to the questions (No. 49 and 50) and supplementary questions asked by Hon. Purna Bahadur Gharti Magar.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the question-and-answer program for today has concluded.
Subsequently, the Honourable Chairperson of the Committee on Agriculture, Cooperatives, and Natural Resources, Kusum Devi Thapa, presented the "Report of the Committee on the Bill to Amend Some Nepal Laws Related to Land, 2082".
After that, the Honourable Chairperson of the Committee on Education, Health, and Information Technology, Ammar Bahadur Thapa, presented the "Report of the Committee on the School Education Bill, 2080".
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the program related to the "Nepal Veterinary Council Bill" listed as item number 4 in today's agenda has been removed from the list and some minor changes have been made to the remaining agenda items.
After that, the proposal put forth by the Hon. Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister, Bishnu Prasad Paudel, "Let's discuss the Bill on Mobilizing Alternative Development Finance, 2082" along with the report of the Finance Committee, was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Hon. Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister, Bishnu Prasad Paudel, responded to the questions raised by the following Honourable Members in the discussion on the proposal:-

Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented amendments mentioned in serial numbers 1 to 24 of the report of the Finance Committee regarding the "Bill on Mobilizing Alternative Development Finance, 2082" for decision, and they were unanimously approved.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker presented amendments mentioned in serial numbers 1 to 24 of the report of the Finance Committee regarding the "Bill on Mobilizing Alternative Development Finance, 2082" to become part of the bill for decision, and they were unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented sections 2 to 58 of the "Bill on Mobilizing Alternative Development Finance, 2082" for decision to become part of the bill, and they were unanimously approved.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker read out the preamble and title of the bill and presented the "Bill on Mobilizing Alternative Development Finance, 2082" for decision to become part of the bill, and it was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the proposal put forth by the Hon. Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister, Bishnu Prasad Paudel, "Let the Bill on Mobilizing Alternative Development Finance, 2082 be passed" was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the proposal put forth by the Hon. Minister of Communications and Information Technology, Prithvi Subba Gurung, "Let the Information Technology and Cyber Security Bill, 2082 be sent to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion" was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the proposal put forth by the Hon. Minister of Youth and Sports, Tejulal Chaudhary, "Let the National Sports Development (First Amendment) Bill, 2082 be sent to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion" was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled to be held on Friday, Bhadra 13, 2082, at 1:00 PM, and the meeting was adjourned at 4:00 PM.
