(Brief Minutes of the Meeting)
Friday, Bhadra 13, 2082
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 1:10 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Devraj Ghimire.
At the beginning of the meeting, as the Honourable Members of the Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood up in front of their seats, the Right Honourable Speaker stated that the Honourable Members who have been in the opposition benches for a long time, demanding an investigation into the Visit Visa scam, have not been participating in regular meetings. He mentioned that he himself has adopted all possible means and dialogues for consensus and has been striving for agreement, but the necessary discussions and dialogues between the government and political parties have not taken place since the meeting began. He further stated that the tendency to "protest disagreement" and walk out as soon as the meeting starts could be detrimental in the long run from the perspective of institutional democratic systems and parliamentary practices. He emphasized that Parliament is not just a place to express disagreement but also a forum to express the voice of national consensus. He noted that currently, bills directly related to federalism are in the process of being passed to strengthen the federal democratic republic, and these bills, like those in committees, require the debate of all Honourable Members in the Parliament. He appealed to the government, the opposition, the parties in power, and all Honourable Members to create an environment for dialogue and bring the session back to its original rhythm.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker gave time to Honourable Ganesh Parajuli and Honourable Jan Bahadur Shahi, who respectively raised concerns about the reduction and non-payment of subsidies to sugarcane farmers, leading to their protest at Maitighar Mandala, and urged for immediate resolution of the farmers' demands. They also stated that the agreement between India and China to use a trade route via Lipulek, a Nepali territory, is against international law and lacks Nepal's participation and consent, and therefore, this issue should be raised at the United Nations. They further demanded that the Right Honourable Prime Minister should engage in fruitful and peaceful dialogue during his visit to China, that action should be taken against those who protect human trafficking, and that a high-level judicial inquiry committee should be formed to investigate the Visit Visa scam, and that the protest would continue until this is done. They expressed their views on these matters.
After Honourable Jan Bahadur Shahi finished speaking, the Honourable Members of the Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party walked out of the meeting hall.
Subsequently, the following Honourable Members spoke during the impromptu session, raising concerns about the government's failure to pay over 1.5 billion rupees in subsidies to sugarcane farmers, leading to their protest, and urging for the fulfillment of their just demands. They also highlighted the death of a man due to a tiger attack in Bara, the increasing attacks by wild animals, and the need to control them. They mentioned that despite a court order allowing the scrap metal business in Kathmandu, the Kathmandu Metropolitan City has been using bulldozers and causing destruction, leading to protests by scrap metal dealers, and urged for a solution through dialogue. They also pointed out the outbreak of cholera in Birgunj and the need for its control, the death of a man and a girl after falling from a cliff in Achham, the drowning of 4 people while swimming in the Khutiya River in Godawari, Kailali, and the death of 4 people in a jeep accident while en route to Dadeldhura headquarters, urging for relief to the families of the deceased. They also suggested that providing services at home to senior citizens and incapacitated citizens would be appropriate for implementing social security. The Honourable Members who spoke were:

Thereafter, the Secretary of the House of Representatives, Hariraj Rai, tabled the "Nepal Citizenship (Second Amendment) Bill, 2081" with the message received from the National Assembly.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker proposed to the House for its consent to change the name of Honourable Ram Hari Khathwada in place of Honourable Ishwori Devi Neupane in the Parliamentary Hearing Committee, in accordance with Sub-rule (2) of Rule 25 of the Rules of Procedure for Joint Meetings and Joint Committees of the Federal Parliament, 2080. The House gave its consent.
Subsequently, the proposal presented by the Honourable Minister of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Poverty Alleviation, Balram Adhikari, to "Discuss the Bill to Amend Some Nepal Acts Related to Land, 2082, along with the Report of the Agriculture, Cooperatives, and Natural Resources Committee" was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was unanimously approved.

Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the amendments mentioned in serial numbers 1 to 3 of the report of the Agriculture, Cooperatives, and Natural Resources Committee regarding the "Bill to Amend Some Nepal Acts Related to Land, 2082" for decision, and they were approved by a majority.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the amendments mentioned in serial numbers 1 to 3 of the report of the Agriculture, Cooperatives, and Natural Resources Committee regarding the "Bill to Amend Some Nepal Acts Related to Land, 2082" to become part of the bill for decision, and they were approved by a majority.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented clauses 2 to 4 of the "Bill to Amend Some Nepal Acts Related to Land, 2082" to become part of the bill for decision, and they were approved by a majority.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker read out the preamble and title of the bill and presented the "Bill to Amend Some Nepal Acts Related to Land, 2082" to become part of the bill for decision, and it was approved by a majority.
After that, the proposal presented by the Honourable Minister of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Poverty Alleviation, Balram Adhikari, to "Pass the Bill to Amend Some Nepal Acts Related to Land, 2082" was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was approved by a majority.
