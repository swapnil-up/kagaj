(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Wednesday, Bhadra 18, 2082
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:15 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Devraj Ghimire.
At the beginning of the meeting, when the Honourable Members from Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats, the Right Honourable Speaker gave time to Hon. Hari Dhakal and Hon. Jan Bahadur Shahi, who respectively spoke about the need for investigation into the Visit Visa case through a parliamentary inquiry committee or a high-level judicial commission, stating that the ruling party has been making false accusations without listening to the protests in the House for 95 days, that democracy cannot be strengthened by suppressing voices of protest, that the import of foreign apples with chemicals should be stopped and local apples from Jumla, which have come to the market, should be commercialized, that some road sections in Jumla are blocked due to landslides and arrangements including equipment should be made to open the roads quickly, and other related issues.
After Hon. Gyan Bahadur Shahi finished speaking, the Honourable Members from Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party walked out of the meeting hall.
Thereafter, the following Honourable Members, in the impromptu session, spoke about the need for immediate and easy supply of drinking water in the Terai districts including Rautahat, the immediate provision of facilities due to be provided to drought-affected areas as Madhesh Province has been declared a drought-affected region, the need to amend the integrated ambulance service procedure recently issued by the Ministry of Health, as the operation of ambulance services only by hospitals could affect the emergency services for the public, the need to immediately address the demands of sugarcane farmers who are protesting with various demands through negotiation, the immediate repair of the Kamala bridge under the Postal Highway, the need to immediately open the blocked roads and provide relief and compensation to the victims as landslides have blocked the Mid-Himalayan Lokmarg and the Saljhandi-Dhorpatan road section in Baglung causing significant physical damage, the establishment of an administrative service center in Galchot, Baglung, the need to facilitate the repatriation of the body of Dambar Bahadur Magar from Dhankuta, who is working abroad, due to his death from consuming poisonous liquor, the need for immediate filling of vacant positions for health workers at the Khotang District Hospital as it is affecting health services, and the need for the government to provide adequate budget for the development of the Karnali region, among other issues:-

Thereafter, when the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal "Consider the Bill to Amend the Nepal Veterinary Council Act (First Amendment), 2082" for decision, Hon. Hitraj Pandey raised a point regarding the lack of quorum in the House. Following this, the Right Honourable Speaker directed the Secretariat to ascertain the quorum, stating that according to Article 94 of the Constitution of Nepal, one-fourth of the members of the House of Representatives must be present to present any question or proposal for decision. After the ringing of the quorum bell for 3 minutes and counting the number of present Honourable Members, it was found that the required quorum was 69, but only 49 Honourable Members were present. Therefore, it was informed that the remaining agenda items have been removed from the proceedings.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned until Thursday, Bhadra 19, 2082, at 1:00 PM, and the current meeting was adjourned at 6:34 PM.
