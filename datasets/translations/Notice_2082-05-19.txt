(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Thursday, Bhadra 19, 2082
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced at 1:05 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Devraj Ghimire.
At the beginning of the meeting, after the Honourable Members from Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their respective seats, the Right Honourable Speaker gave time to Hon. Ashok Kumar Chaudhary and Hon. Dhawal Shumsher Jung Bahadur Rana. They respectively stated that the social networks that the government has decided to ban are linked to digital business, employment, and entrepreneurship, and it would be appropriate to regulate and manage them rather than ban them; the government should show promptness in controlling the cholera outbreak in Birgunj; the news that Nepal agreed to the Global Security Initiative during the Right Honourable Prime Minister's visit to China is contrary to Nepal's foreign policy, and the Prime Minister should provide information to the House on this matter; the government should adopt a policy of encouragement as private schools have made a significant contribution to Nepal's economy and education system; and the demand for the formation of a high-level judicial inquiry committee to investigate the visit visa scam, and the continuous protest by RSP and RPP against human trafficking and corruption, among other issues.
After Hon. Dhawal Shumsher Jung Bahadur Rana finished speaking, the Honourable Members from Rastriya Swatantra Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party walked out of the meeting hall.
Subsequently, Hon. Rajendra Prasad Pandey spoke on issues such as China distancing itself from the Lipulek-Kalapani region issue, stating it is a matter between Nepal and India, which is unacceptable as Nepal is a good neighbor and supports the 'One China' policy.

Subsequently, Hon. Dipendra Dahal, Minister for Physical Infrastructure and Transport, responded to issues raised during the impromptu session, zero hour, and special time concerning transport management and the embossed number plate to be used on vehicles.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the question-and-answer session related to the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology and the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock Development has been removed from today's agenda.
Following this, under the question-and-answer program as per Chapter-8 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, in the question-and-answer session related to the Ministry of Health and Population, Hon. Pradip Poudel, Minister for Health and Population, responded to the questions (Question Nos. 57 and 58) asked by Hon. Prem Suwal and supplementary questions related to those questions.
After that, Hon. Deepak Khadka, Minister for Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation, responded to the question (Question No. 63) asked by Hon. Surya Prasad Dhakal.
Subsequently, Mr. Hariraj Rai, Secretary of the House of Representatives, tabled the bill "Federal Civil Service Bill, 2080" with messages from the National Assembly.
Then, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal "Consideration of the Nepal Veterinary Council (First Amendment) Bill, 2082" which was pending for decision due to lack of quorum in the meeting of Bhadra 18, 2082, and it was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to present amendments to the "Nepal Veterinary Council (First Amendment) Bill, 2082" as per Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, can register their amendments at the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singha Durbar.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal by Hon. Nawal Kishor Sah Sudi, Minister for Women, Children and Senior Citizens, "Send the Children Bill (First Amendment), 2072 to the relevant committee for clause-wise discussion," which was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled for Wednesday, Bhadra 25, 2082, at 1:00 PM, and the meeting was adjourned at 2:45 PM.
