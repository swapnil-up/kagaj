First Session
Meeting Number - 9

(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Thursday, Chaitra 19, 2082
The first meeting of the first session of the House of Representatives, 2082, commenced at 2:20 PM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Senior Member/Right Honorable Speaker Arjun Narsingh K.C.
As soon as the meeting began, the Honorable Members of the NCP-UML stood up demanding time to speak. The Right Honorable Speaker requested the Honorable Members to cooperate in conducting the first meeting of the first session of Parliament in a harmonious atmosphere. However, as the Honorable Members remained standing, the Right Honorable Speaker granted time to Honorable Guru Prasad Baral, who drew the attention of the government and the House to issues such as the arrest of former Prime Minister KP Sharma Oli and former Home Minister Ramesh Lekhak under the incorrect application of the Criminal Offenses Code, and the injury of Honorable Sajida Khatun Siddiki and Honorable Bishnumaya BK due to the government's suppression of peaceful protests, and the need for an investigation into these matters.
Subsequently, the Right Honorable Speaker read out the following letter regarding the summoning of the session from the Right Honorable President:-
"In accordance with Article 93, Sub-article (1) of the Constitution of Nepal, I have summoned the session of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Thursday, Chaitra 19, 2082, at 2:00 PM at the Federal Parliament Building, Singhdarbar, upon the recommendation of the Council of Ministers of the Government of Nepal dated Chaitra 16, 2082.
(Ram Chandra Poudel)
Date: Chaitra 16, 2082
Thereafter, the House observed a 1-minute silent standing tribute to all the martyrs who sacrificed their lives for the nation, democracy, and progressive change, as well as for the Gen-Z movement.
Following this, the Right Honorable Speaker welcomed all the Honorable Members present at the first meeting of the first session of the House and delivered his address as follows:-
"I wish to congratulate all the Honorable Members of this esteemed House of Representatives, which has been formed with overwhelming majority in the historic first parliamentary elections of 2015, and the federal representative elections held on Falgun 21, in pursuit of political stability, an end to impunity, good governance, eradication of policy corruption, a corruption-free society, and the establishment of the rule of law. I extend my heartfelt congratulations to the Right Honorable Prime Minister Mr. Balendra Shah on his appointment as Prime Minister, and my best wishes for a successful tenure to him and all the Honorable Ministers of the Council of Ministers. I pay my heartfelt tribute to all the great martyrs who sacrificed their lives for the honor, dignity, and nationality of the Nepali people in various democratic movements and in the Gen-Z movement of Bhadra 23. The people-elected parliament is the main platform of democracy, representing the sovereign rights of the people. This House of Representatives, formed by the fresh mandate of the Nepali people, is being watched by the general public with full hope, trust, confidence, and anticipation. The people are not just seeking a change in government, but a fundamental change in the culture and practices of governance," he said.
"The 100-point governance reform agenda recently made public by the Council of Ministers is welcome. Its implementation requires coordination, dedication, and impartiality. Diversity in this esteemed House is natural, and unity in diversity is democracy. We are all committed from our respective positions to the spirit of inclusivity, equality, and the supremacy of the people enshrined in the Constitution of Nepal, 2072. Therefore, it is necessary to address the national aspirations by joining rather than breaking, fostering consciousness rather than excitement, and using reason rather than impulse. Addressing public trust and hope through practical policies, plans, and results, while upholding the constitution and constitutional rights and adhering to procedures, is the need of the hour," he said.
"Over time, the political system has changed, but practices have not changed in accordance with the system. The country has repeatedly faced premature dissolution of the House of Representatives, leading not only to confusion, instability, and uncertainty in the country's governance and administration but also to undesirable anomalies, distortions, and problems that have affected national life. The credibility of constitutional bodies in terms of impartiality, autonomy, and transparency has been declining," he said.
"Such impacts on parliamentary practice have also seriously affected policy-making, law formulation, and making the government accountable to the people. The clear mandate given by the people is that such activities must now come to a full stop. Bills related to development, economy, and good governance must be passed with priority. The people, tired of political instability and the same old ideas, practices, and habits, are looking for a new culture and practice along with a new government," he said.
"Parliamentary debate has its own rules and code. The language, style, and expression presented in debates require a certain standard, culture, and civilized boundary. For its dignity and effectiveness, it has been defined that unparliamentary words or expressions should not be brought into practice. I believe that the Honorable Members will enhance the dignity, effectiveness, and prestige of the Parliament by adhering to polite, dignified, and responsible behavior. Debate, counter-debate, and dialogue are the soul of democracy. The ruling party and the opposition are its structural characteristics. There are many issues where consensus and cooperation among parties are needed on common national matters and public concerns. The opposition's protest should constructively contribute to the government staying on the right track. Multi-ideological, original, and decision-oriented debates are considered a natural attraction of Parliament," he said.
"Intense friction and bitterness in ideas and debates may also lead us to bitter experiences. However, the beautiful conclusion derived from ideological friction is the heritage of democracy. It is a democratic culture to take such situations in stride while making the House of Representatives a House of Solutions. Therefore, it is necessary to combine change rather than revenge, ambition with practicality, and ideals with reality. None of us are enemies of each other. Our common enemies are poverty, illiteracy, unemployment, inflation, disease, governance weaknesses, and the distortions that directly affect the country's stability, which the people are enduring. The people have placed great trust in this Parliament to achieve freedom from these and attain prosperity. The nation's entire attention must be focused on social justice and security guarantees and income certainty for the neglected and marginalized communities below the absolute poverty line. In our situation, which is becoming complex, chaotic, and conflict-prone in a changing and adverse global environment, the need for complete unity among us Nepalis has increased," he said.
"The scope of economic development has not expanded in proportion to the expansion of the political sphere. I believe it is the responsibility of this esteemed House to broaden the scope. As Immanuel Kant said, 'Man achieves freedom when he needs to obey only the law and not the command of any person.' This is also the internal aspiration of our democracy and parliament. In the current context of the formation of the House of Representatives, tasks such as the appointment of the Speaker, Deputy Speaker, and other office bearers, and the formation of parliamentary committees are yet to be completed. I believe that all these tasks will be accomplished harmoniously through mutual dialogue, understanding, and cooperation," he said.
"Finally, I have full confidence that there will be no shortcomings in the full success and effectiveness of the newly formed government and the House of Representatives," he said.

Thereafter, the Right Honorable Speaker presented a proposal to the House for approval to conduct its business, maintain order in the meeting, and make other proceedings regular and effective in accordance with Article 104 of the Constitution of Nepal, until the House formulates its own rules of procedure, in accordance with the "House of Representatives Rules of Procedure, 2079". The proposal was unanimously approved.

Subsequently, the Right Honorable Speaker informed that the date for the election of the Speaker of the House of Representatives has been set for Sunday, Chaitra 22, 2082, in accordance with Rule 7, Sub-rule (1) of the House of Representatives Rules of Procedure, 2079, and directed the Secretary-General of the Federal Parliament, Padma Prasad Pandey, to publish the notice regarding the election program for the Speaker's post today.
Following this, the Honorable Home Minister Sudhan Gurung presented the "Voter List (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2082", the "House of Representatives Member Election (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2082", and the "Nepal Special Service (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2082" respectively.
Subsequently, the Right Honorable Speaker presented the following condolence motions on the demise of former members and Constituent Assembly members, which were unanimously approved:-
(a) "This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses sorrow over the untimely demise of Mr. Rishikesh Gautam, who was elected from Bara District Constituency No. 3 in the parliamentary elections of 2048 and 2056 BS and played an active role in democratic movements, at the age of 84 on Ashoj 31, 2082, and offers condolences to the bereaved family."
(b) "This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses sorrow over the untimely demise of Mr. Mohammad Aftab Alam, who was elected as a member of the House of Representatives in 2053, 2056, and 2074 BS, a member of the Constituent Assembly from Rautahat District Constituency No. 2 in 2064 BS, a former minister, and played an active role in democratic movements, at the age of 63 on Kartik 22, 2082, and offers condolences to the bereaved family."
(c) "This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses sorrow over the untimely demise of Mrs. Krishna Kumari Shrestha, who was elected as a member of the National Assembly, a former member, and played an active role in democratic movements, at the age of 84 on Mangsir 10, 2082, and offers condolences to the bereaved family."
(d) "This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses sorrow over the untimely demise of Mr. Amod Prasad Upadhyay, who was nominated as a member of the National Assembly in 2048 BS, elected as a member from Morang District Constituency No. 1 in the House of Representatives member election of 2056 BS, a member of the Constituent Assembly in 2064 BS, a former minister, and played an active role in democratic movements, at the age of 91 on Mangsir 27, 2082, and offers condolences to the bereaved family."
(e) "This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses sorrow over the untimely demise of Mr. Homnath Dahal, who was elected as a member from Okhaldhunga District Constituency No. 1 in the House of Representatives member election of 2056 BS, a former minister, and played an active role in democratic movements, at the age of 84 on Poush 25, 2082, and offers condolences to the bereaved family."
(f) "This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses sorrow over the untimely demise of Mr. Baldev Bohara, who was elected as a member of the Constituent Assembly in 2070 BS and a member of the National Assembly in 2080 BS, and played an active role in democratic movements, at the age of 74 on Poush 30, 2082, and offers condolences to the bereaved family."
Thereafter, the House observed a 1-minute silent standing tribute for the eternal peace of the souls of the late Rishikesh Gautam, Mohammad Aftab Alam, Krishna Kumari Shrestha, Amod Prasad Upadhyay, Homnath Dahal, and Baldev Bohara.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned until Sunday, Chaitra 22, 2082, at 11:00 AM, and the current meeting was adjourned at 4:10 PM.
