(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Sunday, Chaitra 22, 2082
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Member/Right Honorable Speaker Arjun Narsingh KC.
First, the Right Honorable Speaker informed that as per the program for the election of the Speaker of the House of Representatives, a proposal to elect Honorable Member Dol Prasad Aryal to the post of Speaker, as it was the only proposal received within the stipulated time, was presented.
After that, the proposer of the said proposal, Honorable Ravi Lamichhane, presented the proposal stating, "Let Honorable Member Dol Prasad Aryal be elected as the Speaker of the House of Representatives."
Subsequently, the proposer of the said proposal, Honorable Finance Minister Swarnim Wagle, Honorable Minister for Physical Infrastructure and Transport and Minister for Urban Development Sunil Lamshal, and Honorable Minister for Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam supported the proposal.
Thereafter, the Right Honorable Speaker, in accordance with Rule 7, Sub-rule (3) of the Rules of Procedure of the House of Representatives, 2079, declared that Honorable Dol Prasad Aryal has been unanimously elected as the Speaker of the House of Representatives, as it was the only proposal presented to elect Honorable Dol Prasad Aryal as the Speaker of the House of Representatives.
Subsequently, the Right Honorable Speaker extended heartfelt congratulations and best wishes for a successful tenure to Honorable Dol Prasad Aryal on his unanimous election to the esteemed position of Speaker of the Federal Parliament, House of Representatives.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 11:35 AM, scheduled to convene on Monday, Chaitra 23, 2082, at 1:00 PM.
