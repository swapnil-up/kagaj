Thereafter, the Right Honorable Speaker made the following statement before proceeding with the agenda items:-
"I extend a warm welcome to all of you to today's meeting of the House of Representatives. Before commencing today's daily agenda, I wish to share a few words. I express my sincere gratitude to all the Honorable Members and the Federal Parliament Secretariat for providing me the opportunity to address this esteemed assembly. I pay my heartfelt respects to the respected voters of my constituency, Kathmandu 9, who elected me and sent me as a Member of Parliament, and to all those who voted across the country. Similarly, I express my heartfelt gratitude to all the sisters and brothers living abroad who have shown immense love and provided direct/indirect support in the voting, and to all the Honorable Members for electing me to the esteemed position of Speaker. I express my sincere gratitude to the Rastriya Swatantra Party, which nominated me as a candidate for the Speaker, to the proposer Honorable Ravi Lamichhane, and to the supporters Honorable Members Swarnim Wagle, Sobita Gautam, and Sunil Lamshal. I also express my thanks to the members representing all parties for their cooperation in creating an environment where I can represent everyone equally by electing me unopposed.
On this special occasion, I pay my heartfelt tribute to all the great martyrs who sacrificed their lives in the course of the democratic movement and the Janjati movement to establish good governance, control corruption, and achieve the prosperity of the country. Their sacrifice, dedication, and struggle have brought us this far, and we must fulfill our responsibilities with respect to them.
I extend my heartfelt congratulations and best wishes for a successful tenure to the Right Honorable Prime Minister Mr. Balendra Shah, who has assumed the responsibility of Prime Minister with a historic majority close to two-thirds, and the members of the Council of Ministers.
The people have entrusted us with the responsibility of representation for five years through their invaluable votes. This is not just a right, but also a profound responsibility. Many young individuals have sacrificed their lives to bring us to this parliament with expectations of prosperity in the country. Many mothers have been widowed, many have lost their husbands, many have become disabled, maimed, and many are still shedding tears. Enduring these tragic events, the people have entrusted us through the ballot to establish good governance, control corruption, and achieve economic development.
Our primary duty is to deliver results by translating the trust, expectations, and hopes of the people into action. This trust should not be weakened under any pretext. It is our shared responsibility to make parliament effective, accountable, and people-oriented.
The proposals we presented through our promises and commitments during the election process, which were approved and endorsed through the electoral process, have now transformed from mere promises and commitments into a formal and official political agreement between the voters and us, the representatives. Therefore, many important laws still need to be drafted, amended, and revised for result-oriented work. Thus, the active participation of all Honorable Members is essential to complete these tasks by making a time-bound plan. It is necessary to make parliament a center of performance, not inactivity.
We are all responsible for ensuring that parliamentary practice is necessary to make the country prosperous by embracing the fundamental principles of the Constitution of Nepal, democracy, and the parliamentary system. We have all gathered in this esteemed parliament to find the right solutions to every problem of the citizens, carrying the mandate of every citizen who has the zeal to advance the nation on the path of development and prosperity in accordance with the demands of the Janjati movement. In a multi-party democratic system, the dignity and effectiveness of parliament increase through the coordination of all parties, as the will of the people is expressed. It is expected that good constitutional practices will be followed by the political parties and all members present in the parliament.
You are aware that parliament must function with utmost discipline and decorum. This meeting will proceed based on the laws, rules of procedure, and prevailing practices we have. With the arrival of the new House of Representatives, we will immediately form a Rules Drafting Committee. All political parties represented here will participate in this. I look forward to your suggestions to conduct the meeting effectively and smoothly. Furthermore, it will be the duty of all of us to fully comply with and ensure compliance with the Rules of Procedure. I will play an impartial role in resolving any difficulties that arise in this process. I am confident that you will also play an important role in adhering to the procedures you have established.
While there are some old and experienced members in this house, there are also many new and young members. I am confident that this esteemed parliament will be run as a platform that blends the creative thinking, energy of the new with the experience of the old. I also request you that as this house, which is an administrative organ of the state, has been entrusted with the responsibility of enacting federal laws for the country, some laws are old, some are inactive, and some are contradictory. Therefore, before enacting new laws, I urge you to seriously review and revise the existing laws and laws, study and review which laws should be repealed, and which laws should be consolidated, and then proceed to enact new laws as needed.
Every decision and activity we undertake is closely observed by the public. Our primary responsibility after being elected and sent here is to them. Every action of the House of Representatives of this representative federal parliament, which is a common representative of all geographies, ethnicities, languages, and religions, should be a matter of public concern. We must work in such a way that every Nepali citizen feels their participation in the discussions and activities of the parliament. It is our shared duty to make parliament an effective forum for policy debate, dialogue, and resolution.
I pledge to you that the position of Speaker will be neutral, impartial, and coordinating. The role of all Honorable Members in the parliament is and should be equal. Since you have come representing the people, I clarify that there will be no discrimination against any member based on their party affiliation. I will play a significant role in further enhancing the dignity of the Speaker's position in the parliamentary system. The beauty of the parliamentary system lies in policy debate, discussion, and conclusion; I express that parliament will be like an open university for this purpose. As the opposition plays its role based on the merits and demerits of the ruling party, all Honorable Members will have an equal role in establishing good governance and building a developed nation. All parliamentary activities will be conducted under the guidance of the Constitution of Nepal, the Rules of Procedure of the House of Representatives, and prevailing laws. I also pledge to move forward based on mutual cooperation and coordination with political parties within the framework of the constitution and prevailing laws.
As parliament is the place for enacting necessary laws to establish good governance, control corruption, promote development, and create employment, I heartily urge all political parties to provide constructive cooperation and participation.
I have come to the responsibility of the Speaker of this esteemed assembly as an elected member of the House of Representatives, having been affiliated with a political party. I assure you that I will not allow any कमी in the dignity and honor of this esteemed assembly due to my past participation in a political party. All members are equal to me, and it will be my utmost duty to implement all suggestions received from this esteemed assembly impartially. I assure you that the dignity of this esteemed assembly and its members will increase only if the Speaker remains impartial and independent. There will be no discrimination based on numbers, and there will not be any. I expect active participation, constructive cooperation, and coordination from all of you for the conduct of the assembly. I express my heartfelt thanks to the Honorable Members for their active participation and to the Honorable Arjun Narsingh KC, who presided over the meeting until now.
Finally, I express my gratitude on behalf of myself and the entire assembly to the senior Honorable Member Arjun Narsingh KC for presiding over the meetings of the previous House of Representatives and accomplishing important tasks such as the election of the Speaker, he said.

Thereafter, the Right Honorable Speaker presented the proposal "Let the House of Representatives Rules Drafting Committee be formed" for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the Right Honorable Speaker informed the Honorable Members that no notice has been received to reject the "Voter Roll (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2082" in accordance with Rule 94, Sub-rule (1) of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079.
Then, the proposal presented by the Honorable Home Minister Sudhan Gurung, "Let the Voter Roll (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2082 be approved," was presented by the Right Honorable Speaker for decision and was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the Right Honorable Speaker informed the Honorable Members that no notice has been received to reject the "House of Representatives Member Election (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2082" in accordance with Rule 94, Sub-rule (1) of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079.
Then, the proposal presented by the Honorable Home Minister Sudhan Gurung, "Let the House of Representatives Member Election (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2082 be approved," was presented by the Right Honorable Speaker for decision and was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the Right Honorable Speaker informed the Honorable Members that no notice has been received to reject the "Nepal Special Service (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2082" in accordance with Rule 94, Sub-rule (1) of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079.
Then, the proposal presented by the Honorable Home Minister Sudhan Gurung, "Let the Nepal Special Service (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2082 be approved," was presented by the Right Honorable Speaker for decision and was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the Right Honorable Speaker presented the following condolence motion on the demise of former member Ramchandra Bhattarai, which was unanimously passed by the House:-
"This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses sorrow over the untimely demise of Ramchandra Bhattarai, who was elected as a member of the National Assembly in the year 2056 BS from the Communist Party of Nepal, a litterateur, editor, and active participant in democratic movements, at the age of 84 on Chaitra 13, 2082, pays tribute, and expresses condolences to the bereaved family."
Thereafter, the House stood for one minute of silence for the eternal peace of the soul of the late Ramchandra Bhattarai.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned until Tuesday, Chaitra 24, 2082, at 1:00 PM, and the meeting was adjourned at 2:44 PM.
