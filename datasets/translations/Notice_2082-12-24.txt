Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed about the correction made to the House of Representatives Business Advisory Committee, formed as per Rule 13 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, with Honourable Ninu Kumari Karna being included instead of the previously mentioned name.
Subsequently, the Secretary of the House of Representatives, Hakraj Rai, tabled the bills received from the National Assembly: "Forensic Science Laboratory (Establishment and Operation) Bill, 2081", "Tourism Bill, 2081", and "Film Bill, 2081", along with the messages from the National Assembly.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 2:20 PM, scheduled to convene on Wednesday, Chaitra 24, 2082, at 1:00 PM.
