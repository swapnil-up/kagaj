Wednesday, Chaitra 24, 2082
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:05 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, Honourable Samina Mia took emergency time to draw the government's attention to the loss of life of three students who were returning home after taking the SEE examination, in a motorcycle accident on the Jaleshwor-Bardibas road section. She prayed for the eternal peace of the departed souls and urged for appropriate compensation for the families of the deceased and free treatment for those injured in the accident.

Subsequently, Honourable Guru Prasad Baral raised a point of order, stating that during the Zero Hour, a Honourable Member from the ruling party had used objectionable words, calling the party chairman K.P. He demanded that these words be expunged from the parliamentary record, and the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the matter had been noted.

Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the date for the election of the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives has been set for Friday, Chaitra 27, 2082, in accordance with Rule 8, Sub-rule (2) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079. He directed the Secretary of the House of Representatives, Mr. Harkaraj Rai, to publish the notice regarding the election program for the Deputy Speaker today itself, in accordance with the said regulations.
2
Subsequently, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, Honourable Gita Chaudhary, Minister of Agriculture and Livestock Development and Minister of Forests and Environment, presented the "Seventh Annual Report of the National Natural Resources and Fiscal Commission, 2082".
Thereafter, Honourable Dr. Swarnim Wagle, Minister of Finance, presented a proposal seeking permission to present the "Alternative Development Finance Mobilization Bill, 2082".
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker announced that the proposal seeking permission to present the bill was approved, as no Honourable Member had submitted a notice of objection in accordance with Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079.
Thereafter, Honourable Dr. Swarnim Wagle, Minister of Finance, presented the "Alternative Development Finance Mobilization Bill, 2082".
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned until Friday, Chaitra 27, 2082, at 4:00 PM, with the adjournment taking place at 3:40 PM.
