Thereafter, the Right Honorable Prime Minister Balendra Shah tabled the copy of the address made by the Right Honorable President to the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament.
Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker, in accordance with Rule 37, Sub-rule (2) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, presented the motion of thanks for the address made by the Right Honorable President for decision, which the House unanimously passed.
Motion of Thanks
"This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses its gratitude to the Right Honorable President for the address delivered to the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Chaitra 27, 2082, in accordance with Article 94 of the Constitution of Nepal, following the commencement of the first session after the general election of the House of Representatives."
Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker informed the House about the receipt of notices for two proposals regarding the election of the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives: that Honorable Member Sarita Lama be elected as the Deputy Speaker and that Honorable Member Ruby Kumari be elected as the Deputy Speaker.
Then, the proposer of the first proposal, Honorable Gyan Bahadur Shahi, presented the proposal: "Let Honorable Member Sarita Lama be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives."
Subsequently, Honorable Tahir Ali Bhat, Honorable Khushbu Oli, and Honorable Bharat Giri supported the said proposal.
Thereafter, the proposer of the second proposal, Honorable Aaren Rai, presented the proposal: "Let Honorable Member Ruby Kumari be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives."
Subsequently, Honorable Ashok Kumar Chaudhary and Honorable Ambika Devi Sangraula supported the said proposal.
Thereafter, the following Honorable Members participated in the discussion on both proposals regarding the election to the post of Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives:
1. Honorable Ramesh Kumar Malla
2. Honorable Dr. Amaresh Kumar Singh
3. Honorable Harka Raj Rai
4. Honorable Basana Thapa
5. Honorable Mohammad Istiyak Rai
Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker gave time to the proposer of the first proposal, Honorable Gyan Bahadur Shahi, and the proposer of the second proposal, Honorable Aaren Rai, to withdraw their respective proposals. As they did not wish to withdraw, the Honorable Speaker informed the members that both remaining proposals regarding the election to the post of Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives would be presented for decision in order, in accordance with Rule 8 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079.
Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker informed the members that since the proposal "Let Honorable Member Sarita Lama be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives" requires a majority of the total members present in the House of Representatives to be passed, a division of votes would be conducted by signing, in accordance with Rule 8 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079.
Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker provided information regarding the procedure to be followed for the vote division. While presenting the proposal "Let Honorable Member Sarita Lama be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives" for decision, it was informed that out of the 257 (two hundred and seventy-seven) Honorable Members present in today's voting, a total of 5 (five) votes were cast in favor, i.e., in favor of "Yes", 215 (two hundred and fifteen) votes were cast against, i.e., in favor of "No", and 37 (thirty-seven) votes were cast in the "Abstain" category. Therefore, the proposal "Let Honorable Member Sarita Lama be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives" was declared rejected by a majority.

Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker informed the House about the scheduling of the election for the chairpersons of the thematic committees under the House of Representatives, in accordance with Rule 176, Sub-rule (1) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, for Friday, Baishakh 4, 2083, at 1:00 PM.
Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker informed the House about the brief summary of the proceedings of the first session of the House of Representatives as follows:-
Honorable Members, the first session of the House of Representatives commenced on Thursday, Chaitra 19, 2082, and continued for a total of 9 days until Friday, Chaitra 27, 2082. During this period, 6 meetings were held on 6 days, with a total of 10 hours and 50 minutes spent on parliamentary proceedings. Additionally, on Friday, Chaitra 27, 2082, a joint meeting of the Federal Parliament was held, lasting for 15 minutes.
1. On Chaitra 27, 2082, the Right Honorable President addressed the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament, commencing the first session after the general election, in accordance with Article 95 of the Constitution of Nepal. The House unanimously passed a motion of thanks for the address made by the Right Honorable President.
2. In accordance with Article 104 of the Constitution of Nepal, the House of Representatives, until it formulates its own rules of procedure, will conduct its proceedings in accordance with the "House of Representatives Regulations, 2079" to regulate its functioning, maintain order, and ensure effective proceedings. This proposal was unanimously approved by the House on Chaitra 19, 2082.
3. The "Voter List (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2082", "House of Representatives Member Election (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2082", and "Nepal Special Service (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2082", which were presented in the meeting on Chaitra 19, 2082, were approved by the meeting held on Chaitra 23, 2082.
4. In the election for the Speaker held on Chaitra 22, 2082, Honorable Ravi Lamichhane was the proposer, and Honorable Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Minister Sobita Gautam, Honorable Finance Minister Swarnim Wagle, and Honorable Physical Infrastructure and Transport Minister and Urban Development Minister Sunil Lamsal were the supporters for the sole proposal: "Let Honorable Member Dol Prasad Aryal be elected as the Speaker of the House of Representatives." Consequently, Honorable Dol Prasad Aryal was unanimously elected as the Speaker.
5. The task of presenting the "Alternative Development Finance Mobilization Bill, 2082", which was introduced in the House of Representatives during this session, was completed.
6. Similarly, messages received from the National Assembly regarding the "National Forensic Science Laboratory (Establishment and Operation) Bill, 2081", "Tourism Bill, 2081", and "Film Bill, 2081", which originated in the National Assembly, have been presented in the House.
7. In the meeting held on Chaitra 23, 2082, in accordance with Rule 13 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, the names of 21 Honorable Members were nominated to the House of Representatives Business Advisory Committee. Also on the same day, Honorable Kavindra Bullakoti was the proposer, and Honorable Sita Thapaliya, Honorable Ganesh Singh Thagutra, Honorable Yuvraj Dulal, Honorable Dhurbaraj Rai, Honorable Bharat Giri, and Honorable Pushpa Kumari Chaudhary were supporters for the proposal to form a 15-member "House of Representatives Regulations Drafting Committee", which was unanimously approved by the House.
8. In the election for the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives held on Chaitra 27, 2082, out of the two proposals, "Let Honorable Member Sarita Lama be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives" and "Let Honorable Member Ruby Kumari be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives", the proposal "Let Honorable Member Ruby Kumari be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives" received two hundred and twenty-nine (229) votes in favor and was passed by a majority of the total members present in the House of Representatives.
9. The "Seventh Annual Report of the National Natural Resources and Fiscal Commission, 2082" was presented during this session.
10. On Chaitra 27, 2082, the task of forming the Parliamentary Hearing Committee and the Committee for Monitoring and Evaluation of the Implementation of State Directive Principles, Policies, and Responsibilities was completed in accordance with Rules 24 and 28 of the Federal Parliament Joint Meeting and Joint Committee (Operation) Regulations, 2080. Furthermore, the task of forming 10 different thematic committees under the House of Representatives in accordance with Rule 175 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, was completed.
11. During this period, condolence motions were passed by the House on the demise of former members of the Constituent Assembly and Parliament: Rishikesh Gautam, Mohammad Aftab Alam, Krishna Kumari Shrestha, Amod Prasad Upadhyay, Homanath Dahal, Baldev Bohara, and Ramchandra Bhattarai.
12. During this session, various Honorable Members raised contemporary issues during Zero Hour, Special Time, and Adjournment Time, drawing the government's attention.
Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker read out the following letter from the Right Honorable President:-
In accordance with Article 93, Sub-article (2) of the Constitution of Nepal, upon the recommendation of the Government of Nepal, Council of Ministers, dated 2082/12/24, I hereby prorogue the ongoing session of the Federal Parliament, effective from 12:00 AM on Friday, Chaitra 27, 2082.
(Ram Chandra Paudel)
President
