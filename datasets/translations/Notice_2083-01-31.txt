Thursday, Baisakh 31, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, during the impromptu session, the following Honourable Members raised their concerns on contemporary issues including: the need for the relevant ministry to make preparations for preventing fire, floods, and risks; the increase in heat and snake bites in the Terai region; the inability to operate the ICU and ventilators in hospitals due to a lack of skilled manpower; the Prime Minister's disregard for the House of Representatives meetings; the Prime Minister's failure to answer important questions; the need for the Speaker to take initiative to make the government accountable and responsible to the House; that no one is above democracy; the Prime Minister must be accountable and responsible to the government and the parliament; the Prime Minister must be present in the House; the Prime Minister should not only respond via social media but also be accountable and responsible to the House; the need to set standards for fees charged by various schools and colleges; and the necessity of taking action to prevent the menace of monkeys on tigers and other wild animals and the crops planted by farmers.

Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker granted permission to the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, to respond to the questions raised during the discussion on the Government of Nepal's Annual Policy and Program for the fiscal year 2083/084. However, the opposition party's Honourable Members stood up in front of their seats, reiterating their demand for the Prime Minister's presence, and continued their obstruction. Meanwhile, as the opposition party's Honourable Members continued their obstruction, the Right Honourable Speaker requested the Honourable Members to cooperate in conducting the session, stating that the Prime Minister could not be present, and a letter had been sent based on law, principles, and legal provisions, and the agenda was issued accordingly, hence the Honourable Minister would respond to the questions raised in the discussion. However, as all the Honourable Members of the opposition party remained standing, the Right Honourable Speaker adjourned the meeting for fifteen minutes.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned until 11:00 AM today, fifteen minutes later.
The meeting of the House of Representatives, which was adjourned until 11:00 AM today, fifteen minutes later, commenced at 9:00 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
First, as the Honourable Members of the opposition party remained standing in front of their seats, raising their demands, and amidst the obstruction, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, responded to the questions raised during the discussion on the Government of Nepal's Annual Policy and Program for the fiscal year 2083/084, which was mentioned in the address by the Right Honourable President to a joint session of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Baisakh 28, 2083, Monday.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle's response was on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, and only those Honourable Members who had presented amendments to the Government of Nepal's Annual Policy and Program for the fiscal year 2083/084 could seek clarification, while those who participated in the discussion without presenting amendments could not. He further informed that no Honourable Member sought clarification.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker gave time to the aforementioned 35 amendment-proposing Honourable Members to withdraw their amendment proposals on the Government of Nepal's Annual Policy and Program for the fiscal year 2083/084. As they did not wish to withdraw them, the amendment proposals were presented for decision one by one and were rejected by a majority.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the Government of Nepal's Annual Policy and Program for the fiscal year 2083/084, which was mentioned in the address by the Right Honourable President to a joint session of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Baisakh 28, 2083, Monday, for decision, and it was passed by a unanimous vote.
Subsequently, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented the proposal: "Let's discuss the principles and priorities of the Appropriation Bill for the fiscal year 2083/084 (excluding tax proposals)."
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the discussion on the principles and priorities of the Appropriation Bill for the fiscal year 2083/084 (excluding tax proposals) has been scheduled for Baisakh 4, 5, and 6, 2083, and directed the Ministry of Finance as well as the Secretariat to take notes of the issues raised by Honourable Members during the discussion on the principles and priorities of the Appropriation Bill for the fiscal year 2083/084 (excluding tax proposals).
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the program item "Report of the House of Representatives Rules Drafting Committee, 2083" listed as serial number 6 in today's agenda has been removed from the agenda.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned until Monday, Baisakh 4, 2083, at 11:00 AM, at 10:32 PM.
