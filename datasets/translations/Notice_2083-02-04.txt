(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Monday, Jestha 4, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, when the Honourable Speaker called the name of Honourable Thakur Singh Tharu to speak during the impromptu session, all Honourable Members of the Labour Culture Party came in front of the Speaker's dais and protested for some time with placards before taking their seats. During the impromptu session, the following Honourable Members raised their voices on various contemporary issues: demanding action against the forest officer who ordered the bulldozing of the shelter for mourners on the banks of the Babai River in Ward No. 3, Bardibadiya Municipality, Bardiya; the disappearance of the 'Rato Machhindranath's left and right 'hiti' (water channel); Sharmila Darji being stranded in Kuwait due to a human trafficking network, with her mother seeking help from the police administration; the government investigating the alternative network used by human traffickers; the speedy construction of roads left incomplete by contractors; swift investigation into the rape of a 5-year-old minor, providing free treatment and justice to the victim; information on the government's policy regarding monkey and wild boar menace; resolution of issues concerning tenant farmers; efforts to expedite the return of the mortal remains of Santosh Rai, who died while returning from foreign employment; and other contemporary issues.

Thereafter, the Honourable Speaker requested the Honourable Members to submit their names in writing within the allotted 4 minutes for speaking and drew their attention to the significance of the yellow and red lights in the House.
Meanwhile, Honourable Yagyamani Neupane, during the impromptu session, spoke about the need for an independent judiciary, decisions being made arbitrarily in judges' chambers contrary to the conflict of interest, administrative processes and lack thereof, the unclear bench mentioned in media reports as per the order of the Acting Chief Justice, and the need to focus on resolving pending cases. Similarly, Honourable Sumiksha Baskota questioned whether the judiciary had forgotten the Theory of Separation of Power, stating that if the judiciary leans towards power seizure, it will lead to problems in justice delivery, and that the Acting Chief Justice's order to register rejected writs was wrong, calling for a House discussion on this matter and challenging them to come out in the open if they intend to engage in politics.
Subsequently, when Honourable Ain Bahadur Mahar requested time to make a point of order, the Honourable Speaker informed that the Speaker decides whether to grant time for Honourable Members to make points of order as per the regulations.
Thereafter, when Honourable Ain Bahadur Mahar repeatedly requested time to make a point of order, the Honourable Speaker drew his attention to the fact that matters prohibited from discussion under Article 105 of the Constitution and Rule 29 of the House of Representatives Regulations are frequently discussed in the House.
Subsequently, the Honourable Chairman of the House of Representatives Regulations Drafting Committee, Ganesh Parajuli, responded to the questions raised during the discussion.
Following this, the proposal presented by the Honourable Chairman of the House of Representatives Regulations Drafting Committee, Ganesh Parajuli, "Be it considered on the Report of the House of Representatives Regulations Drafting Committee, 2083," was presented for decision by the Honourable Speaker and was approved by a majority.
Thereafter, the Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to present amendments to the "Report of the House of Representatives Regulations Drafting Committee, 2083" can submit them to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singh Durbar.

Meanwhile, Honourable Eshuda Kumari Baral requested impromptu time and stated that a teacher from Sompal Secondary School in Dadeldhura had committed suicide after writing a long status on Facebook. She paid tribute to the deceased teacher and demanded a thorough investigation into the incident and action against the guilty. Similarly, as Honourable Pramila Kuluu was about to speak, Honourable Som Sharma requested impromptu time and stated that some locals in Ward No. 5 of Badigad Rural Municipality, Baglung district, had become seriously ill after consuming wild mushrooms, with one person dying. He wished for the speedy recovery of the sick and paid tribute to the deceased, urging the government to conduct public awareness programs on such issues in the future.
Thereafter, the Honourable Speaker informed that the discussion on the principles and priorities of the Appropriation Bill for the fiscal year 2083/084 (excluding tax proposals) would continue in the next meeting.
Subsequently, the House of Representatives was adjourned at 6:10 PM, with the next meeting scheduled for Tuesday, Jestha 5, 2083, at 11:00 AM.
