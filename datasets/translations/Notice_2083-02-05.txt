(Brief Minutes of the Meeting)
Tuesday, Jestha 4, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:05 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
First, during the impromptu session, the following Honourable Members expressed their views on contemporary issues such as the disruption of studies for Plus Two students residing in the Holid Center for the displaced in Banepa; the government's policy being not about poverty but about discouraging poverty; the suicide of one employee working at a bank in Biratnagar, along with four senior employees and a colleague; the demand for a dowry of 1 million rupees by the groom's side in a marriage about to take place between a youth employed in foreign employment and a young woman from Kankai, Jhapa, leading to the young woman's suicide, necessitating an impartial investigation; the 39th day of the protest at Maitighar against the ongoing prohibition, discrimination, and boycott against the Dalit community since ancient times, requiring the state to provide opportunities, respect, and equal access to the Dalit community; and regarding the information that the construction of the cable car project in the religious site Pathibhara/Mukumlung area has been halted, questioning whether the work has indeed stopped and what the government's plan is to maintain a balance between religious faith, cultural issues, and development.

Meanwhile, after Honourable Bipin Kumar Acharya finished speaking, Honourable Bishnu Bahadur Khadka sought impromptu time and expressed condolences for the soul of Dhaka Bir Bhandari, aged 40, from Salyan, who died during treatment after his motorcycle was hit by a truck on the road towards Sall Bazaar on the Chinchu-Jajarkot road on Jestha 5, 2083, and extended deep sympathy to the bereaved family.
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister for Finance, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, responded to the questions raised during the discussion on the principles and priorities of the Appropriation Bill for the fiscal year 2083/084 (excluding tax proposals).
After that, Honourable Sajida Khatun Siddiki sought impromptu time and drew attention to the conduct as per Chapter 26 of the Regulations, stating that the news of Honourable Krishna Karki of Morang Constituency No. 2 impregnating and abandoning a 26-year-old Nigerian woman is being reported in national and international media, which has embarrassed the entire House, and demanded a swift investigation. Following this, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the matter has been noted.
Additionally, Honourable Khushbu Oli sought impromptu time and drew the government's attention through the House to take action against the human trafficking syndicate, as an activist raising her voice against human trafficking in Kuwait has been threatened with death and false imprisonment.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker announced the conclusion of the discussion on the principles and priorities of the Appropriation Bill for the fiscal year 2083/084 (excluding tax proposals) and informed the Honourable Members that the report, compiled with the points raised in the discussion on the principles and priorities of the Appropriation Bill for the fiscal year 2083/084, would be sent to the Council of Ministers, the Ministry of Finance, and the National Planning Commission.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned to convene on Wednesday, Jestha 6, 2083, at 11:00 AM, and the meeting adjourned at 5:00 PM.
