(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Wednesday, Jestha 6, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:14 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
As soon as the meeting began, the Honourable Members of the Shram Sanskriti Party stood in front of their seats with placards, chanted various slogans one after another, and then took their seats.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker urged all Honourable Members to maintain the decorum of the House of Representatives, stating that it is the duty of all Honourable Members to maintain the decorum of the House of Representatives. He also drew attention to the provision in Rule 30 of the Rules of Procedure, 2079, and warned the Honourable Members, including Honourable Herkaraj Rai and other Honourable Members of the Shram Sanskriti Party, to correct their activities and behavior as per Rule 30 of the Rules of Procedure, 2079, for regularly displaying placards and behaving indecently in the parliament in violation of the decorum of the House.
Subsequently, when the Right Honourable Speaker gave Honourable Herkaraj Rai time to speak, he stated that the provision in Rule 15 (2) of the Rules of Procedure of the House of Representatives mandates that the government ministers should answer the questions raised by the Honourable Members in the House, and therefore, this provision should be implemented. He added that the government should be accountable to the public and the House, that question-and-answer programs should be conducted in the House, and that an environment should be created for the government to be present in the House with dignity.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that there have been 7 meetings so far in the session that began on Jestha 28, and that some Honourable Ministers are scheduled to answer questions raised during the Urgent, Zero, and Special Time today, and drew the attention of the Honourable Members to the importance of Urgent Time in the House.
Thereafter, during the Urgent Time that commenced, the following Honourable Members raised their concerns on contemporary issues including: the provision in Article 35 of the Constitution that every Nepali citizen has the right to health, but it is not being realized; the need to maintain Real-time Monitoring and adequate stock of life-saving Anti Rabies Vaccine and Anti Snake Venom medicines at Bhim Hospital in Rupandehi; the accusation that the government is trying to displace the flood and landslide affected Dhanasara Damai in Junichanda Rural Municipality, Jajarkot, by demolishing her house, which was already damaged by the earthquake, and her land, which was also damaged by floods and landslides, forcing her to leave; the need to exercise caution as the World Health Organization has declared Ebola a public health emergency, and the Ebola epidemic could spread in Nepal; the government's removal of squatters without arranging for their proper settlement; the government's decision to provide Rs. 25,000 per month to each family for temporary settlement should be accompanied by arrangements for permanent settlement; the deaths of many citizens due to road accidents caused by the haphazard condition of roads during construction; the suggestion that instead of rehabilitation centers, it would be appropriate for the government to arrange for home-based care for severely disabled and incapacitated individuals; the need to reconsider the examination schedule set by Mid-Western University and Tribhuvan University on Jestha 14, the day of Eid al-Adha, a festival for the Muslim community, and to implement the provisions related to courtesy as per Rule 29 (d) of the Rules of Procedure; and the urgent need for the government to provide relief, rehabilitation, and rescue to the victims of the severe flood in Chankheli Rural Municipality Ward No. 5, Humla, which has caused humanitarian crisis and significant physical damage.

Thereafter, Honourable Jayapati Rokaya sought Urgent Time and drew the government's attention to the need for immediate relief and rescue due to the death of a large number of livestock and significant physical damage caused by floods and landslides in Humla district.
Subsequently, Honourable Minister of Communications and Information Technology, Dr. Vikram Timilsina, responded to issues raised during the Urgent, Zero, and Special Time, including efforts to promote press freedom, enhance the capacity of the Press Council, and encourage the film, art, and mass communication sectors; the commencement of work to make government websites accessible according to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG); expansion of data center capacity; and the initiation of 4G service expansion in rural areas.
Meanwhile, Honourable Herkaraj Rai requested time to ask questions to the Honourable Minister. The Right Honourable Speaker informed that there is no provision for clarification in response to questions raised during Urgent, Zero, and Special Time, and that time will be granted as needed if time is requested based on the rules of procedure. He directed the Honourable Members to maintain the decorum of the House as the rules of procedure have been made to uphold its decorum.
Thereafter, Honourable Minister of Youth, Labour and Employment, Ramji Yadav, responded to issues raised during the Urgent, Zero, and Special Time, including the government's work to make foreign employment safe and dignified, the ministry's efforts to create employment opportunities within the country, the policy to resolve past complaints by setting a timeline, the establishment of a Labour Help Desk at Tribhuvan International Airport, and the plan to update the laws to be time-appropriate.
After that, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister and Home Minister, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "House of Representatives Member Election (First Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, as no notice of objection was received from any Honourable Member as per Rule 105 of the Rules of Procedure of the House of Representatives on the proposal seeking permission for the said bill, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister and Home Minister, introduced the "House of Representatives Member Election (First Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister and Home Minister, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "Voter List (First Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, as no notice of objection was received from any Honourable Member as per Rule 105 of the Rules of Procedure of the House of Representatives on the proposal seeking permission for the said bill, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister and Home Minister, introduced the "Voter List (First Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 1:24 PM until Thursday, Jestha 7, 2083, at 11:00 AM.
