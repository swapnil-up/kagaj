Tuesday, Jestha 12, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:30 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
First, during the impromptu session, the following Honourable Members raised issues including: damage to 34 houses due to hailstorms and heavy rains in Baglung, disruption of educational activities due to damage to the roofs of 4 schools, the need for appropriate compensation for crop damage; the death of Bini Maya Tamu Magar, Balkumari Khamcha from Rambha Rural Municipality, Palpa, and Anup Rana from Tansen due to consumption of poisonous mushrooms, offering heartfelt condolences to them and arranging free treatment for the 22 others undergoing treatment; significant damage to houses, sheds, cardamom cultivation worth millions, bridges, micro-hydro projects, irrigation projects, and other structures in Wards No. 1 and 2 of Yadabark Rural Municipality, Panchthar, due to floods on Jestha 8, leading to disruption of daily life due to food shortages caused by the obstruction of the Mid-Hilly Highway, and the need to declare the affected area a disaster zone; the need for the government to collect details of damage, arrange relief, preparedness, and appropriate compensation for significant loss of life and property due to natural disasters in various parts of the country, and to develop an early warning system for disaster events; the death of minors Safal Pulami and Yukesh B.K. by drowning in a river in Sindhuli on Jestha 10, offering condolences to the deceased, and the increasing number of drowning incidents annually, urging the government to launch awareness programs on water-related risks; the need to assess the damage to life, property, crops, and physical structures caused by floods and landslides in Taplejung and Panchthar on Jestha 8 and provide relief and compensation; the disruption of transportation and hardship in daily life due to the Indrawati bridge being washed away, necessitating its prompt repair and operation; expressing condolences for the death of Rajita Yadav of Aurahi Rural Municipality Ward No. 2, Dhanusha, due to negligence, and demanding strict action against the involved medical personnel after an investigation; the disruption of electricity in parts of Pyuthan, Rolpa, and Arghakhanchi districts due to a malfunction in the Jhimruk Power Plant's transformer, affecting daily life, and urging the Nepal Electricity Authority to promptly resolve the issue; the spread of fear and suffering among the public due to the municipality's actions of demolishing settlements and using bulldozers indiscriminately in the market area of Gaushala Municipality Ward No. 4, Mahottari, contrary to a court order, and the death of Binod Saha due to a heart attack; the death of 100 out of 240 goats raised by farmer Kisan Mune Buda of Ramaroshan Rural Municipality Ward No. 6, Achham, for livelihood due to rain and landslides on Jestha 11, with others missing, and the need to provide appropriate compensation to the farmer; and other contemporary issues were discussed by the Honourable Members.

Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker granted permission to the Honourable Minister of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation, Khadak Raj Poudel, to present the "Tourism Bill, 2082 for Consideration". Following this, all Honourable Members of the opposition, including the main opposition, stood in front of their seats. The Right Honourable Speaker then gave time to Honourable Basana Thapa, who stated that the House is not just a game of numbers but a democratic forum that operates on discussion, deliberation, accountability, and consensus. She further stated that the government, driven by the arrogance of majority, is weakening the dignity of the House and the role of the opposition. Democracy thrives on consensus and cooperation, not majority, and she drew the government's attention to listen to the opposition's voice, respect the House, and act in accordance with the spirit of democracy.
Thereafter, Honourable Ain Bahadur Mahar stated that the government, with a two-thirds majority, is trying to evade the House, and is violating Articles 76(10) and 81 of the Constitution. He demanded that the Prime Minister should immediately respond to the various questions raised by the Honourable Members of the ruling and opposition parties. He further demanded that the Right Honourable Speaker should summon the Right Honourable Prime Minister to the House, even if through a written order, to ensure the implementation of the Constitution and rules, respect public mandate, and ensure accountability to the people. He stated that the proceedings of the House cannot proceed until the Prime Minister is present.
Subsequently, Honourable Yuvraj Dulal questioned why the rules of procedure should be followed for passing bills of far-reaching importance if the government is to proceed based on majority, especially amidst opposition obstruction. He demanded that the opposition's voice should be heard in the House, the Constitution, laws, and rules should be fully followed, and the Prime Minister should be present in the House to provide answers.
Following this, Honourable Aaren Rai stated that the Prime Minister should be present in the House to address the demands and issues of the people, the government should make efforts for this, and his party will express a dissenting opinion until the Prime Minister is present in the House.
Thereafter, Honourable Khushbu Oli stated that the ruling party should also make efforts for the Prime Minister's presence in the House, the public should have the opportunity to question the Prime Minister on public demands and issues, and the Nepali people are seeking the presence and accountability of the Prime Minister and the Home Minister.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker acknowledged that he was aware of the issues raised by the Honourable Members, that they had drawn his attention, that he was in continuous dialogue with the party leaders/chief whips/whips, and that the Prime Minister's response to the questions asked by the Honourable Members was constitutional, in accordance with the rules, and justified. He informed the House that continuous dialogue is ongoing with the Right Honourable Prime Minister and that a date for the Prime Minister's response has been set, and he would inform the House once that date is confirmed. Despite repeated requests to take their seats, the Honourable Members of the opposition continued to stand and chant slogans.
Following this, amidst the chanting of slogans, the Right Honourable Speaker granted permission to the Honourable Minister of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation, Khadak Raj Poudel, to present the "Tourism Bill, 2082 for Consideration". Honourable Minister of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation, Khadak Raj Poudel, presented the "Tourism Bill, 2082 for Consideration".
Thereafter, amidst the obstruction, the following Honourable Members expressed their views on the discussion of the bill:-

Meanwhile, after Honourable Jagdish Kharel (Lalitpur) finished speaking, the Honourable Members of the opposition began standing in front of their seats and chanting slogans. The Right Honourable Speaker adjourned the meeting for 15 minutes.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned for 15 minutes and scheduled to reconvene at 3:15 PM.
The meeting of the House of Representatives, which was adjourned for 15 minutes earlier today, commenced at 4:50 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, Honourable Ramesh Kumar Malla, citing the rules, stated that Honourable Jagdish Kharel (Lalitpur) had called the opposition's protest a "tamasha" (spectacle), and questioned whether it was a spectacle for the Right Honourable Prime Minister, who also holds significant ministerial portfolios, to be present in the House and answer the questions raised by Honourable Members. He demanded that the word "tamasha" be expunged from the record as it is insulting, unparliamentary, and against public etiquette.
Meanwhile, as Honourable Nishkal Rai attempted to cite the rules, the Right Honourable Speaker acknowledged his attention and stated that he would inquire with the Secretariat and provide information. Similarly, Honourable Jagdish Kharel (Lalitpur), citing the rules, objected to the previous Honourable Member calling the word "tamasha" obscene and unparliamentary. He stated that according to the Nepali dictionary, "tamasha" means commotion or unnecessary noise, and that he used the word keeping in mind the commotion and noise during the discussion of the bill, therefore the word is not unparliamentary.
Following this, the Honourable Members of the opposition began standing in front of their seats and chanting slogans. The Right Honourable Speaker stated that he had provided time to all Honourable Members present in the House as needed, had previously provided time to the opposition for speaking, and had provided time to the ruling party members when they sought to cite rules. He urged them to cooperate in running the House without arguments and counter-arguments, and expressed his serious concern that despite his requests to take their seats, Honourable Members were persistently trying to speak.
Subsequently, Honourable Nishkal Rai, citing the rules, stated that he felt the dignity of the House was being further enhanced, but the action of the marshals removing Honourable Member Gita Kumari Sendang from the meeting hall without the Speaker's order, after she was cautioned under Rule 30 and ordered under Rule 31 of the Rules of Procedure, was unacceptable. He drew attention to the fact that the Speaker should inform everyone before such orders are given.
The Right Honourable Speaker acknowledged the issue raised by the Honourable Member and stated that Honourable Gita Kumari Sendang had attempted to take video and photos with her mobile phone clearly visible towards the Speaker, in violation of Rule and the dignity of the House while the House was in session. He had directed the marshals to check whether photos and videos were taken, but she was not removed from the meeting hall, and cautioned the Honourable Members not to distort the issue.
Immediately thereafter, amidst the obstruction, Honourable Dhanendra Karki expressed his views during the discussion of the bill.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker gave the floor to Honourable Kabindra Buletoti, who stated that as per the discussions held with the chief whips, whips, and whips of all parties, and based on the conversation held at the Prime Minister's Secretariat, a way out should be found through discussion rather than debate, and results should be delivered. Therefore, the Prime Minister would come to the House, and he informed the House that the Prime Minister would likely come within a week.
Thereafter, Honourable Padma Kumari Aryal stated that there was a lack of sincerity in implementing the consensus reached in the meeting and the decision made in the Business Advisory Committee meeting held in the morning. She stated that the Honourable Members of the opposition have been continuously raising their voices, emphasizing that the House should run on the basis of consensus and cooperation, and demanded that the commitment made by the Chief Whip of the ruling party be "ruled" from the Speaker's chair.
Subsequently, Honourable Paresh Kumar Hamal demanded that democracy should be protected, the House should be run in accordance with the Constitution, laws, and rules, the dignity of the sovereign House should be maintained, and the values and principles of democracy and parliament should be kept intact. He sought accountability of the government to the House and demanded that the commitment made by the Chief Whip of the ruling party be "ruled" from the Speaker's chair.
Thereafter, Honourable Member Khushbu Oli stated that it is inappropriate for the current ruling party, which neglected the opposition when it was in opposition, to neglect the opposition now. She demanded that the meetings of the Business Advisory Committee and the ruling/opposition parties should be held and the decisions of those meetings should be implemented verbatim in the House.
Following this, Honourable Dhurabraj Rai stated that all Honourable Members should follow the system, and the voice of the Honourable Prime Minister should be conveyed to the public. He urged the Right Honourable Speaker to "rule" to make the Prime Minister accountable to the House.
Thereafter, Honourable Abhishek Pratap Shah questioned who was preventing the Right Honourable Speaker from exercising his authority to "rule" the Prime Minister, Ministers, and Members, and demanded that the executive be made accountable to the legislature through "ruling".
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker stated that continuous dialogue is being held with the Right Honourable Prime Minister, and the Prime Minister is ready to answer the questions raised by the Honourable Members. He informed the House that once a date for the government's response is confirmed, he would provide the information and requested cooperation in running the House and taking seats.
Thereafter, the discussion on the "Tourism Bill, 2082" resumed, and the following Honourable Members expressed their views:-

Thereafter, the Honourable Minister of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation, Khadak Raj Poudel, responded to the questions raised during the discussion.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker put the proposal "Tourism Bill, 2082 for Consideration" to a vote, and it was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to present amendments to the "Tourism Bill, 2082" in accordance with Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, could submit them to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singhdarbar.
Subsequently, the proposal presented by the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, Home Minister, and Minister for Science, Technology and Innovation, Balendra Shah, that "The National Forensic Science Laboratory (Establishment and Operation) Bill, 2082 be sent to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion" was put to a vote by the Right Honourable Speaker and unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the proposal presented by the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, Home Minister, and Minister for Science, Technology and Innovation, Balendra Shah, that "Clause-by-clause discussion on the House of Representatives Member Election (First Amendment) Bill, 2083 be held in the House" was put to a vote by the Right Honourable Speaker and unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that no amendment notices had been received for the said bill.
Thereafter, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, Home Minister, and Minister for Science, Technology and Innovation, Balendra Shah, responded to the question raised by Honourable Prem Bahadur BK during the discussion of the bill.
Subsequently, the clauses 2 and 3, preamble, and title of the bill, "House of Representatives Member Election (First Amendment) Bill, 2083" were put to a vote by the Right Honourable Speaker and unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the proposal presented by the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, Home Minister, and Minister for Science, Technology and Innovation, Balendra Shah, that "The House of Representatives Member Election (First Amendment) Bill, 2083 be passed" was put to a vote by the Right Honourable Speaker and unanimously passed.
Subsequently, the proposal presented by the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, Home Minister, and Minister for Science, Technology and Innovation, Balendra Shah, that "Clause-by-clause discussion on the Voter List (First Amendment) Bill, 2083 be held in the House" was put to a vote by the Right Honourable Speaker and unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that no amendment notices had been received for the said bill.
Thereafter, as no Honourable Members' names were received to participate in the discussion of the bill, the Right Honourable Speaker put the clauses 2 and 3, preamble, and title of the bill, "Voter List (First Amendment) Bill, 2083" to a vote and they were unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the proposal presented by the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, Home Minister, and Minister for Science, Technology and Innovation, Balendra Shah, that "The Voter List (First Amendment) Bill, 2083 be passed" was put to a vote by the Right Honourable Speaker and unanimously passed.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned to be held on Wednesday, Jestha 13, 2083, at 11:00 AM, and the meeting adjourned at 7:24 PM.
