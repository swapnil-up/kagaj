(Brief Details of Meeting Proceedings)
Wednesday, Jestha 13, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
As soon as the meeting began, Honourable Hariraj Rai stood up demanding time to speak with a placard. When the Right Honourable Speaker granted him time, he demanded that the Right Honourable Prime Minister be summoned to respond to the question raised as per Rule 14(2) of the Rules of Procedure, or that he be expelled from the House as per Rule 31(1) of the Rules of Procedure. The Right Honourable Speaker stated that drawing attention to this matter was the Speaker's prerogative and that it was not appropriate for a Honourable Member to demand the Speaker to rule contrary to the constitution and laws. Following this, Honourable Hariraj Rai left the hall.
Subsequently, during the impromptu session that commenced, the following Honourable Members raised issues: the lack of dialysis services at the Provincial Hospital in the Koshi Province, forcing patients to travel to Kathmandu for treatment; the death of Pema Lama, aged 29, from altitude sickness while collecting Yarsagumba in Mugu, and the need for compensation for the deceased's family; the unconscious state of Phutik Lama and the need for free treatment by bringing him to the district hospital via air transport; the serious injuries sustained by Birendra Thaguna, his wife, son, and niece in a tiger attack while riding a motorcycle on a road section within Shuklaphanta National Park in Kanchanpur, and the need for free treatment for the injured, as well as necessary initiatives to address human-wildlife conflict; accidents occurring due to speeding heavy load pickup vehicles hitting other vehicles in the morning on the Kankre Vinaik-Markhu road, and the need to control speeding vehicles; the damage to crops caused by hailstorms in Meluali, Baitadi district, and the need for appropriate compensation for farmers; the risk of floods and landslides in various locations in Dilasaini, Pauchodi, Patan, and Dogadakedar of the same district during the monsoon, and the need for the government to pay attention to its prevention; the need for a monkey management policy rather than killing monkeys, for their long-term and scientific management to protect crops; the inability to bring back the bodies of Umesh Kumar Pango from Ilam and Dev Gurung from Nuwakot, who died in a road accident in Croatia on May 6, even after 23 days, and the need for the government to make necessary efforts to repatriate the bodies; the need for disaster response for potential landslides in Ilam, which has been affected by disasters; the need for quick repair and restoration of electricity poles that have fallen due to windstorms in the Karnali Province, which have disrupted roads and electricity; and the need to make the hall of the House of Representatives, which is under construction, dignified by using quality chairs and tables, similar to those in a cinema hall and classroom, respectively. Thereafter, Honourable Dr. Bikram Timilsina, Minister of Communications and Information Technology, proposed that the "Film Bill, 2082" originating in the National Assembly be considered.

Subsequently, Honourable Dr. Bikram Timilsina, Minister of Communications and Information Technology, responded to the questions raised during the discussion.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal "Consideration of the Film Bill, 2082" originating in the National Assembly for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to present amendments to the "Film Bill, 2082" originating in the National Assembly, as per Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079, can submit them to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singh Durbar.
Following this, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that as per the provision in Article 119 of the Constitution of Nepal, the annual estimate of revenue and expenditure for the fiscal year 2083/084 will be presented by Honourable Dr. Swarnim Wagle, Minister of Finance, in a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Jestha 15, 2083, at 4:00 PM. Therefore, all Honourable Members of the House of Representatives are informed to be present in the House of Representatives meeting hall for the joint meeting by 3:45 PM on the said day and take their seats.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 3:00 PM, to convene on Friday, Jestha 15, 2083, at 5:30 PM.
