(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Monday, Jestha 18, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 2:15 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
As soon as the meeting began, the Honourable Members of the Opposition Party stood in front of their seats and started chanting slogans. Subsequently, when the Right Honourable Speaker gave time to the following Honourable Members, the Honourable Prime Minister, in the course of responding to the House, stated that the facts, evidence, and realities presented must be based on truth. Due to diplomatic ignorance and lack of knowledge, the Honourable Prime Minister should appear in person, withdraw the statement from the rostrum, and apologize. Regarding the statement "Nepal has also encroached upon India's land," it was demanded that if encroachment has occurred, information on which places should be provided to the House. The Prime Minister's statement has caused the House and the entire Nepali people to hang their heads in shame, and the Prime Minister's statement was immature and baseless, causing embarrassment. The expression given by the Prime Minister during the question-and-answer session with the Prime Minister in the House meeting on Jestha 17 was against national interest. Nepal's non-aligned foreign policy means it does not encroach on the land of others. The Indian Border Security Force is obstructing the construction of a canal in Susta, Nepali land. The Prime Minister attended the program organized by the Nepali Army on the occasion of Republic Day in national attire, but his presence in the House without wearing the national attire is undermining the dignity of the House. The response given by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in defense of the Honourable Prime Minister's statement was erroneous, and therefore, the Prime Minister must withdraw his statement and apologize in the House. Meanwhile, after Honourable Ain Bahadur Mahar finished speaking, Honourable Indira Rana Magar demanded that the words "cutting babies" and "guerrilla" used by the previous speaker Honourable Member during his speech be removed from the parliamentary record, citing a violation of the rules.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker stated that after the Honourable Members of the Opposition Party had presented their views, time was given to Honourable Members of both the ruling and opposition parties during the impromptu session. He acknowledged their concerns and urged them to cooperate in conducting the session according to the agenda. However, the opposition Honourable Members continuously demanded that the House would not function unless the Prime Minister's statement was corrected. The Right Honourable Speaker then stated that Honourable Members can present their issues in the House concerning people's livelihoods, lawmaking, and against corruption. The Speaker only presents any proposal and issue to the House for decision; the authority to approve or reject it lies with the House itself. Despite this, as the opposition Honourable Members continued to stand in front of their seats and chant slogans, the Right Honourable Speaker adjourned the meeting for fifteen minutes.

Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 2:50 PM, to reconvene after fifteen minutes.
The meeting of the House of Representatives, adjourned today to reconvene after 15 minutes, commenced at 4:35 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
As the Honourable Members of the Opposition Party remained standing in front of their seats at the beginning of the meeting, the Right Honourable Speaker informed them that their concerns had been noted by him and the government. He also mentioned that Honourable Members had requested time to speak during the impromptu session and urged them to cooperate in conducting the meeting for lawmaking. However, as the opposition Honourable Members continued to stand in front of their seats, the Right Honourable Speaker announced that all programs on today's agenda had been removed from the list. He further informed that some Honourable Members of the House had created disorder, exhibited undignified and objectionable behavior, and caused physical damage and destruction during the meetings of the House of Representatives on Jestha 17, 2083. This information was received by the Speaker after the meeting concluded, in accordance with Rule 33 (4) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083. As a record of this matter was prepared today, Jestha 18, 2083, a committee has been formed to conduct further investigation into this matter and submit a report to the Speaker within seven (7) days. The committee will be chaired by Prakash Adhikari, Secretary of the House of Representatives, with the Chief of the Federal Parliament Secretariat's Internal Service and Planning Management Division and the Chief of the Technology Management Division as members. He also informed that after the report is received, necessary further action can be taken in accordance with Rule 33 (4) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 4:50 PM, to be held on Tuesday, Jestha 19, 2083, at 1:00 PM.
