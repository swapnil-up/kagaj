(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Tuesday, Jestha 19, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 3:00 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
First, at the outset, Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepal Communist Party, and Shram Sanskriti Party stood in front of their seats and started chanting slogans. The Right Honourable Speaker urged them to cooperate in conducting the meeting and to proceed with the regular agenda. However, as the Honourable Members of the opposition parties remained standing and chanting slogans, the Right Honourable Speaker gave time to the following Honourable Members, who expressed their views on the following issues: The statement made by the Prime Minister on Jestha 17 during the question-and-answer session was objectionable, that the statement severely undermined Nepal's sovereignty, territorial integrity, and national security, that the Prime Minister should appear in the House of Representatives, that the Prime Minister's attendance in the House should be verified as per the rules, that the Prime Minister's statement made in the House is a serious matter from a diplomatic, legal, and national security perspective, and therefore the Prime Minister should appear in the House and withdraw the statement with an apology from the rostrum, that an official stance should come from the ruling party regarding the Prime Minister's statement, that the issue of state borders is more important than budget and law-making, and therefore the House should form a clear opinion on this matter, that the Prime Minister's statement weakens claims regarding encroached borders, etc.

Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker gave the floor to the Leader of the Opposition in the House of Representatives. When the Leader of the Opposition declined to speak, the Right Honourable Speaker read out the following letter received from the Office of the President:-
It is requested that the original copies (1 each) of the following bills, which have been authenticated by the Right Honourable President, Shri Ram Chandra Poudel, in accordance with Article 113, Sub-section (2) of the Constitution of Nepal, are enclosed with this letter.

Meanwhile, while the Right Honourable Speaker was reading out the letter received from the Office of the President, Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), and Nepal Communist Party came to the Speaker's podium and started chanting various slogans, while Shram Sanskriti Party and Rastriya Prajatantra Party continued their protest by standing in their places. The Right Honourable Speaker then informed that the items numbered 2 and 3 on today's agenda have been removed from the schedule.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled for Monday, Jestha 25, 2083, at 11:00 AM, and the meeting was adjourned at 3:30 PM.
