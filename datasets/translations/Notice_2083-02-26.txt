(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Tuesday, Jestha 26, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 1:05 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, as Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Communist Party of Nepal, Labour Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats demanding time to speak, the Right Honourable Speaker stated that the agenda could not be entered for the past few days and that he would provide time to everyone to speak.
Informing that some Honourable Members had requested time to speak during the impromptu session, he urged for cooperation in conducting the meeting.
Subsequently, when time was given to the following Honourable Members to speak, they expressed their views on various issues including:
That the statement made by the Right Honourable Prime Minister in the House on issues such as borders and national security was objectionable and that the Prime Minister should proceed with the nation in mind, apologize in the House, and correct the statement.
That the alteration of tax rates after the financial bill has been presented in Parliament is a serious matter, and the Finance Minister should pave the way for an investigation into it.
That the Speaker's role is decisive in maintaining the dignity of Parliament.
That the manipulation of the financial bill is not a minor issue, and the public has sought an answer from the Finance Minister regarding this matter.
That the self-respect of the martyrs who played a role in establishing the border from Chiwa Bhanjyang to Limpiyadhura has been hurt, and the statement made by the Right Honourable Prime Minister in the House has adversely affected the nation's dignity. As a guardian, the Speaker should order its removal.
That the Right Honourable Prime Minister should respond from the rostrum regarding issues spoken against national interest, and the Speaker should rule on this.
That since the House has been continuously obstructed since Jestha 19, two options have been presented for the smooth conduct of the meeting: either the House should accept with signatures that Nepal has encroached upon India's territory, or the Prime Minister's statement should be corrected by the House.
That the 'anti-national' statement made by the Right Honourable Prime Minister on Jestha 17 during the process of responding to questions raised in the House, stating "Nepal has also encroached upon India's border in many places," should be immediately removed from the House's record and a resolution should be presented and passed.

Meanwhile, during his speech, Honourable Nishkal Rai urged for a moment of silence to honour the martyrs of various movements and revolutions for democracy and civil consciousness, and the martyrs of the Janji rebellion for good governance and accountability. Accordingly, all Honourable Members in the opposition stood for 5 minutes of silence.
Similarly, after Honourable Harkaraj Rai finished speaking, the Right Honourable Speaker stated that continuous dialogue is ongoing between the leaders of the ruling and opposition parties, and that the Honourable Prime Minister has requested time to respond to various issues raised in the House. He urged to proceed with the agenda and cooperate in conducting the meeting.
Thereafter, Honourable Janu Poudyal, citing the rules, stated that the continuous obstruction in the House has made it very frustrating and that the public's right to raise issues in the House has been deprived.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker, conducting the meeting, stated that the act of Honourable Members interfering in the meeting proceedings by setting their own time and acting on their own discretion is an insult to the House. While Honourable Members protested, he drew attention to the language used and also to Rule 21(b) of the Rules of Procedure.
He further stated that continuous dialogue is ongoing regarding the conduct of the meeting and urged to proceed with the agenda and cooperate in conducting the meeting. However, as the Honourable Members of the opposition remained standing, the Right Honourable Speaker adjourned the meeting until 11:00 AM on Jestha 27, 2083.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 1:50 PM until 11:00 AM on Jestha 27, 2083.
