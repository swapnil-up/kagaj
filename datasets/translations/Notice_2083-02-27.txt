(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Wednesday, Jestha 27, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives, scheduled to convene at 11:00 AM, was postponed to 2:00 PM after the publication of Notice-1, and commenced at 3:10 PM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the commencement of the meeting, Honorable Members from the Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepali Communist Party, Shram Sanskriti Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and requested time to speak. The Honorable Speaker granted time to Honorable Harkaraj Rai, who stated that the Foreign Minister's correction of the Prime Minister's statement in the House would not be constitutional or just, and that the Prime Minister himself should correct it. He also drew attention to the violation of Rule 15(2) and 4(1) of the Regulations and Articles 77 and 76(10) of the Constitution.
Following this, the following Honorable Members spoke during the impromptu session, raising issues such as: the successful rescue of approximately 453 Nepalis from about 40 districts who were held captive, forced to pay money, and subjected to physical and mental torture by network marketing businessmen during a Chhapa Marga program in Kushinagar, Uttar Pradesh, India, and their subsequent return to the Rupandehi District Police Office; the serious question raised about women's security by the incident of the death of INRI Bhujel, an employee of the Nepali Army, due to an attack by her own husband, and the fact that the victim's complaint to the police was not heard and the perpetrator was protected, demanding a fair investigation and strict punishment for the guilty; the incident where workers who paid Rs. 4000 to a consultancy named CIN to go to Israel were forced to reapply and pay again due to the cancellation of exams under the pretext of not having enough demand, and that the affected workers had to be detained at the Kalimati Police Circle when they protested; the demand to declare Nepal a "Sanatan" state based on the fact that 90% of the population are followers of Sanatan Dharma, and the threat of rape and murder received by the speaker, as well as attempts to convert to religion through temptation, drawing the government's attention to these matters.

Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker informed that there was no Zero Hour or Special Time scheduled for today.
Thereafter, the Honorable Foreign Minister, Shishir Khanal, provided information regarding the work being carried out by his ministry, stating that the Nepali government has already made its official stance public on the issue of India operating the Mansarovar Yatra via Nepali territory of Lipulek, diplomatic notes have been sent to neighboring countries, and border disputes will be resolved through diplomatic means. He also informed that technical teams related to Nepal-India border management are working on demarcation and mapping, and clarified that the statement made by the Honorable Prime Minister on Jestha 17th was related to the technical realities of the border and border occupancy. He further explained that the Prime Minister was raising the issue of historical documents and facts related to the border, not seeking third-party mediation.
After this, the Leader of the Opposition, Honorable Bhimraj Adhikari, and the following Honorable Members, expressed their views regarding the statement of the Honorable Foreign Minister, Shishir Khanal, and the resumption of the meeting's stalemate:-

Meanwhile, after Honorable Bhimraj Adhikari spoke, Honorable Samiksha Baskota drew attention to Rule 21(d) of the Regulations, stating that the voices of the citizens should be raised by the Honorable Members in the House, that issues are not being brought to the House in a proper manner, and that the House's dignity is being compromised, urging everyone to be restrained. In response, the Honorable Speaker instructed the Secretariat to study the matter raised by the Honorable Members from the ruling party.
Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker announced that the contents of a letter from the Office of the President would be read out. Honorable Harkaraj Rai stood up requesting time to speak regarding the response given by the Honorable Foreign Minister. The Honorable Speaker stated that the Honorable Member had not requested time to speak and their name had not been called.
Meanwhile, Honorable Rima Bishwakarma drew attention to Rule 21(a) and (d) of the Regulations.
Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker drew the attention of the Honorable Members of the Shram Sanskriti Party, informing them that the word used by Honorable Harkaraj Rai was disrespectful, and that time was not being provided to the Honorable Member because they were continuously protesting during the Foreign Minister's response.
Following this, the Honorable Speaker read out the contents of the following letter received from the Office of the President, and the Honorable Members of the Shram Sanskriti Party began chanting slogans by coming to the well in front of the Speaker's seat:-

Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker informed the members that the "Alternative Development Finance Mobilization Bill, 2082", passed by the House of Representatives on Jestha 7, 2083, and sent to the National Assembly, was tabled in the National Assembly with a message on Jestha 8, 2083. As it was not returned to the House of Representatives within the period mentioned in Article 111, Sub-article (4) of the Constitution of Nepal, the procedure under Rule 130, Sub-rule (3) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, would be proceeded with.
Subsequently, the Honorable Finance Minister, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, presented a proposal to "Commence general discussion on the annual estimates of revenue and expenditure for the fiscal year 2083/084."
Following this, the Honorable Speaker drew the attention of the Honorable Members of the Shram Sanskriti Party, who were in protest, to Rule 21 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, stating that the vocabulary and language being used by the Honorable Members in the House were indecent and unparliamentary, and that their attention had been drawn to this. He informed that the Honorable Members had not received permission to speak and would be given time after they resumed their seats. Upon which, the Honorable Members resumed their seats.
Thereafter, Honorable Harkaraj Rai expressed his views regarding the statement of the Honorable Foreign Minister, Shishir Khanal, and the resumption of the meeting's stalemate.
Following this, the following Honorable Members expressed their views in the discussion on the annual estimates of revenue and expenditure for the fiscal year 2083/084, which commenced:-
1. Honorable Lima Adhikari (Acharya)
4. Honorable Renuka Kaucha
2. Honorable Narendra Kumar Kerung
6. Honorable Arjun Kumar Karki
3. Honorable Pushparaj Kandel
7. Honorable Aaren Rai
4. Honorable Bipin Kumar Acharya
Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker informed that the discussion on the annual estimates of revenue and expenditure for the fiscal year 2083/084 would continue in the next meeting, and that agenda items 8 and 9 (a) to (d) of today's agenda have been removed from the list.
Thereafter, the Honorable Finance Minister, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, presented a proposal seeking permission to present the "Nepal Rastra Bank (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083."
Following this, as no opposition notice was received from any Honorable Member regarding the proposal to seek permission for the bill, in accordance with Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honorable Finance Minister, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, presented the "Nepal Rastra Bank (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083."
Subsequently, the Honorable Minister for Information and Communications, Dr. Bikram Timilsina, presented a proposal to send the "Film Bill, 2082", which originated in the National Assembly, to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion, and the proposal was approved by a majority.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned until 11:00 AM on Thursday, Jestha 28, 2083, at 6:54 PM.
