Thursday, Jestha 28, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:15 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, when Honourable Members from the Labour Culture Party and the Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and requested time to speak, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members had requested time during the impromptu session. Honourable Harka Raj Rai was given time, during which he drew attention to the need for the Prime Minister's statements made in the House on Jestha 17 to be removed from the parliamentary record, for the Prime Minister to correct his statements in the House, and for the proceedings to be conducted according to the rules.
Thereafter, during the impromptu session that commenced, the following Honourable Members raised their concerns regarding the Mechi Highway, a historic lifeline connecting the hills and the Terai, from Charali in Jhapa to Suketar in Taplejung. Residents living in the border areas of the highway are being forced to demolish physical structures built within the road boundary as per the notice from the Road Division, which will result in the loss of livelihood for millions of people, thus urging the government to reconsider and take necessary steps. The issue of the "Is the room empty?" protest by Deepa Nepali at Maitighar, which highlights the discrimination and exclusion faced by the Dalit community for years. Dalit students across the country are being forced to return to their districts, leaving their dreams unfulfilled. The import of 3,000 electric vehicles between Jestha 1 to 14, prior to the budget speech, is suspicious and requires a parliamentary inquiry committee. Due to landslides in various places along the Karnali Highway, the road is being obstructed, causing hardship and suffering to travellers, and the concerned bodies need to pay attention to clearing the road. The increasing daily incidents of violence against women require the government and concerned bodies to focus on their reduction, among other issues.

Thereafter, when Honourable Members requested time to speak, the Right Honourable Speaker gave time to the following Honourable Members, who respectively stated that the Prime Minister had said things that no sovereign nation's Prime Minister has ever said, asking "What are you people watching?" when our Prime Minister said it. They also stated that a parliamentary inquiry committee is necessary regarding the changes in tax rates, that the response given by the former minister was unsatisfactory, that the Prime Minister's statements and activities against the nation have made it impossible to trust him. They demanded that the Prime Minister, who kept a map of Greater Nepal in his office when he was the Mayor of the Metropolitan City, should immediately retract his statements made in the House today. They also demanded an investigation into the unusual import of electric vehicles in bulk through customs, stating that actions are being taken to gather evidence against national unity, integrity, and identity, and that it is a tragedy that the chief executive of the nation is acting against national identity. Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the speaking time for the Honourable Members on the discussion of the Annual Estimates of Revenue and Expenditure for FY 2083/084 had concluded.
Subsequently, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle responded to the questions raised by the Honourable Members during the discussion on the Annual Estimates of Revenue and Expenditure for FY 2083/084.
Following the Finance Minister's response, Honourable Sajida Khatun Siddiki raised a point of order, stating that the Finance Minister's remark that the presented budget was not communist was objectionable, and that under previous communist governments, the right to peaceful existence for the country and its people was ensured. Similarly, Honourable Ruku Neupane raised a point of order, stating that the previous speaker had violated Rule 21 (d) and (e) of the Rules of Procedure, and that the Finance Minister's statement was not objectionable but philosophical.
Thereafter, the Honourable Finance Minister Swarnim Wagle clarified the questions raised by the following Honourable Members who sought clarification regarding the response given by the Honourable Finance Minister:-

Subsequently, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented a proposal seeking permission to present the "Appropriation Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, as no objection notice was received from any Honourable Member regarding the proposal to seek permission to present the "Appropriation Bill, 2083" in accordance with Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Rules of Procedure, 2083, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented the "Appropriation Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented a proposal seeking permission to present the "Public Debt Raising Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, as no objection notice was received from any Honourable Member regarding the proposal to seek permission to present the "Public Debt Raising Bill, 2083" in accordance with Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Rules of Procedure, 2083, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented the "Public Debt Raising Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented a proposal seeking permission to present the motion "Consideration of the Nepal Rastra Bank (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, during the general discussion on the bill, the following Honourable Members expressed their views:-
1. Honourable Parshuram Tamang
5. Honourable Aren Rai
6. Honourable Lima Adhikari (Acharya)
2. Honourable Bidushi Rana

8. Honourable Krishna Hari Budhathoki
4. Honourable Guru Prasad Baral
Thereafter, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle responded to the questions raised during the discussion.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the motion "Consideration of the Nepal Rastra Bank (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083" for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
Following this, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to present amendments to the "Nepal Rastra Bank (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083" in accordance with Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Rules of Procedure, 2083, could submit them to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singh Durbar.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled for Friday, Jestha 29, 2083, at 11:00 AM, and the meeting was adjourned at 6:00 PM.
