Notice Paper - 2
(Brief Record of Meeting Proceedings)
Friday, Jestha 29, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, when Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepali Communist Party, Shram Sanskriti Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party sought time to speak by standing in front of their seats, the Right Honourable Speaker granted time to the following Honourable Members, who raised issues including: the Prime Minister's statement in the House on Jestha 17 should be removed from the parliamentary record; the Prime Minister must correct the statement in the House; the Finance Minister threatened Honourable Members with investigating old files; while dozens of Prime Ministers have changed in India, none have claimed that Nepal has encroached upon Indian land, making the Prime Minister's claim shameful and hurting the sentiments of 30 million Nepalis and national dignity; evidence related to disputed areas like Limpiyadhura, Kalapani, and Susta needs to be studied; a video has been released showing Indian security personnel intimidating Nepalis in Susta, and the Defence Minister of the Government of Nepal must respond regarding this; a parliamentary committee should be formed to investigate the Finance Minister's manipulation of the budget rate based on past events; the Foreign Minister's statement in the House regarding border management and disputes is erroneous; the press release issued by the Nepali side does not mention border disputes; during the Foreign Minister's visit to China, it is imperative to strongly state in the press note and diplomatic talks that Lipulek, Limpiyadhura, and Kalapani belong to Nepal; and other related matters.

Following this, during the impromptu session that commenced, the following Honourable Members spoke: the government must send fertilizer as needed to every district; large farmers investing 2 crore rupees receive 80 lakh rupees in grants, while small farmers receive nothing, so arrangements should be made for all farmers to receive them; Rebant Bisht, a resident of Mangalsen Municipality-5, Achham, who was residing in Kirtipur for studies, has been missing since Jestha 25, and despite relatives filing a report at the Kirtipur Police Station, his whereabouts remain unknown, so the search operation must be intensified; residents living in the border areas along the Mechi Highway from Charali, Jhapa to Suketar, Taplejung, have constructed physical structures within the road boundary as per the notice from the Road Division, which will lead to the closure of employment and livelihood for millions of people, so these structures must be demolished and reconsideration is needed; children who have completed their jail terms and are kept in children's homes should be immediately sent home after completing legal procedures; on Jestha 28, Dipnarayan Raut Kurmi's wife, a resident of Paroha Municipality-7, Rautahat, was murdered brutally and robbed of jewelry by unknown individuals while she was in the field, and the culprits must be brought to justice; the public is facing extreme difficulties due to the non-disbursement of budget under the government's Safe Citizen Housing Program; the grant amount promised to ordinary people for building houses to replace thatched and straw roofs with tin sheets, as per the plan of the previous government, has not yet been provided; strict action must be taken against those who commit fraud by misusing technology, and the money must be recovered, and the government must take necessary steps for financial literacy by strengthening the Cyber Bureau; the commercial tea cultivation that started in Eastern Nepal in 2041/42 BS is currently facing severe crisis, so the concerned parties must pay attention to solve the problem, and policy obstacles in exporting Nepali tea to neighboring countries must be removed and attention should be paid to market management in those countries; 496 mobile phones and watches confiscated during the SEE examination in Siraha were destroyed, so an investigation and action must be taken against the individuals involved in this incident; the "Nritasingh Dham" in Dhading, a sacred confluence of the Budhi Gandaki and Netravati rivers, important as a spiritual and cultural pilgrimage site, is being submerged due to the construction of the Budhi Gandaki Hydropower Project, so efforts should be made to develop the dham as an "Underwater Temple" and make it an international religious-tourist center; and other related matters were raised.

Thereafter, Honourable Ain Bahadur Mahar took impromptu time and stated that the entry of Indian SAB forces with weapons into the Susta area of Nawalparasi created panic among the locals, who resisted and forced them to retreat, and therefore, he saluted the locals and stated that problems have arisen on the Nepal-India border due to the statement made by the Prime Minister in the House that "Nepal has also encroached upon India's land".
Subsequently, the proposal "Be it considered on the Appropriation Bill, 2083" presented by the Right Honourable Speaker was unanimously approved.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the days from Jestha 1 to Asar 1, 2083, have been designated for discussing various headings under different ministries and secretariats of the Appropriation Bill, 2083, and for the concerned ministers to answer questions related to their ministries and secretariats, and also informed that the names of the Honourable Members who will participate in the discussion under various headings of different ministries and secretariats, based on party representation, should be submitted through the parliamentary party officials to the working section, House of Representatives, Federal Parliament Secretariat, one hour before the meeting on the day of the discussion on the appropriation headings of the respective ministry, and Honourable Members who wish to submit a notice of a proposal for reduction of expenditure under Rule 148 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, regarding various headings under different ministries and secretariats of the Appropriation Bill, 2083, can submit it to the Bill Section (House of Representatives) by 4:00 PM on Jestha 30, 2083.

Subsequently, the proposal "Be it considered on the Intangible Cultural Heritage (Conservation) Bill, 2083", which originated in the National Assembly, presented by the Right Honourable Speaker, was unanimously approved.
After that, Honourable Badan Kumar Bhandari took impromptu time and drew attention to the bus accident on route Ba 3 Kha 8398 going from Banepa to Mahabharat, which resulted in seven deaths and 15 injuries, and stated that despite the Supreme Court's ban on operating large vehicles in 2075 BS, large vehicles are still operating freely on the said road, leading to daily accidents, and urged for measures to reduce them.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members who wish to present amendments to the "Intangible Cultural Heritage (Conservation) Bill, 2083", which originated in the National Assembly, in accordance with Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, can submit them to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singh Durbar.
After that, the Honourable Minister of Industry, Commerce and Supplies, Gauri Kumari, who is also holding the portfolio of the Honourable Minister of Youth, Labour and Employment, presented the "International Labour Organization Convention on Labour Inspection, 1947 (No. 81)" and the "Protocol of 2014 to the Forced Labour Convention, 1930 of the International Labour Organization (No. 29)" respectively.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned to Monday, Asar 1, 2083, at 11:00 AM, and the meeting was adjourned at 4:05 PM.
