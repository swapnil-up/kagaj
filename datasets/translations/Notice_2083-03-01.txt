Meeting Number - 23
Federal Parliament of Nepal
Notice Paper - 2
(Brief Record of Meeting Proceedings)
Monday, Ashadh 1, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:15 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Shram Sanskriti Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and sought time to speak. The Right Honourable Speaker granted time to the following Honourable Members, who raised concerns regarding:
- The Minister of Foreign Affairs not clearly stating Nepal's claim regarding Kalapani and Lipulek during bilateral talks in India.
- Clarification on the Finance Minister's threat in the House to "open old files" while responding to the budget.
- The demand to remove the Right Honourable Prime Minister's statement from the parliamentary record made on Jestha 17.
- Wrongdoings such as students in Siraha submerging their mobile phones and watches in water after taking the SEE exam, forest employees pushing a tempo off a cliff, and damaging private property by throwing stones.
- The government's response to the incident of an Indian helicopter flying over Darcula without Nepal's consent.
- The government's seriousness regarding the continuous harassment of Nepali citizens by the Indian SSB in Susta, Nawalparasi, while the government remains silent.
- The public release of the report submitted by the National Human Rights Commission regarding the movement of Bhadra 23 and 24, and the report of the investigation committee formed regarding Home Minister Sudhan Gurung.
Honourable Members who spoke on these issues included:
1. Honourable Padama Kumari Aryal
2. Honourable Harka Raj Rai
3. Honourable Yuvraj Dulal
4. Honourable Renuka Kaucha
5. Honourable Bharat Giri
Following this, during the impromptu session, the following Honourable Members spoke:
- The urgent need for the government to control elephant attacks in Mechinagar, Jhapa, which have been ongoing for 35-40 years and have caused significant damage to crops like paddy, maize, and betel nut, and to provide appropriate compensation to the victims.
- The immediate dismissal of false cases filed to entrap youths involved in various political movements.
- The government's meaningful initiative for the protection of national sovereignty and borders.
- The need for awareness programs and for the Cyber Bureau to be made capable and technology-friendly, as a girl attempted suicide due to cyberbullying.
- Action against those involved in the suspicious death and recovery of the body of Manisha Saud, Ward Chairperson of Dhikari Rural Municipality-4, Achham.
- The government's initiative to reduce human-wildlife conflict, following the death of Padama Poudel due to an elephant attack in Mechinagar, Jhapa.
- The need for the Ministry of Energy and the Ministry of Home Affairs to take necessary steps to resolve issues related to Likhu Khola hydropower projects, where local crops are being submerged, water sources are drying up, and social responsibility commitments mentioned in the environmental impact assessment are not being implemented, causing hardship to the public.
- Action against forest employees who pushed off a cliff the business operated by Dhanadevi Dhami in a tempo in Godawari, Kailali.
- The government's response to the incident of an Indian helicopter flying over Darcula without Nepal's consent.
- The government's need to manage the production and market of Yarsagumba, which is a source of annual income for thousands of families in 12 Himalayan districts including Dolpa, as its production is declining due to various reasons, leading to food insecurity.
- The government's immediate provision of rabies vaccine to the district hospital, as the entire village in Jajarkot, Vheri Municipality-9, is in fear due to the suspected death of a child from rabies.
- The government's attention to resolving the problem of exporting Nepali tea to India.
- Action against those involved in the attacks on Maheshwor Dhamala and Kalpana Saud, who were swept away by floods in Godawari, Kailali on Jestha 19, and Netra Bahadur Shahi and Surendra Bahadur Shahi, who were injured in attacks by tigers and an unknown group respectively in Kalikot.
- The government's attention to ensuring a comfortable journey for passengers on the Karnali Highway.
- The immediate rescue of 8 youths from Chitwan, including Sujan Subedi, who are held captive in Ukraine, and Amrit Jha from Udaypur, who is under the control of the Iranian government.
- The prompt search for Pranjal Kunwar of Safebagar-8, Achham.
- The release of land seized from citizens of Dhunibeshi Municipality Wards 6-9, Dhading, for road expansion, and the assurance of property rights, as the road route has now been changed.
- The government's long-term plan to control fraud in the name of foreign employment, take action against those involved, and provide justice to victims.
- A thorough investigation into the incident where Anisha Dhami, working as an ANM, was found dead in Barahpokhari Rural Municipality, Khotang, and strict action against the guilty.
- An impartial investigation into the death of Sabar Bakas, a resident of LatiKoili, Surkhet, while in police custody during interrogation.

Meanwhile, after Honourable Pramila Kumari Gachchhadar finished speaking, Honourable Krishna Hari Budhathoki sought impromptu time and stated that a farmer in Ramechhap was injured while trying to protect his crops from monkeys, and that monkeys are destroying crops nationwide. He urged the government to take necessary initiatives to ensure the safety of farmers, protect crops, and prevent wildlife attacks. He said that the discussion on appropriation titles could not proceed until the concerned ministers were present in the House. Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker adjourned the meeting for 14 minutes.
Subsequently, the meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 5:05 PM to reconvene after 15 minutes.
