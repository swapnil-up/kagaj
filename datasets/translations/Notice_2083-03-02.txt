(Brief Record of Meeting Proceedings)
Tuesday, Ashadh 2, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:20 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, when the Honorable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Labor Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and sought time to speak, the Right Honorable Speaker granted them time, and they raised issues such as the Prime Minister needing to retract his statement on border issues and remove it from the record, the arrangement for free broadcast of the ongoing World Cup Football, the need for information on discussions held during the former minister's visit to China regarding Lipulek, the Indian army's helicopter flying in the skies of Darchula without permission, the need for the government to diplomatically resolve issues seen in Nepali tea exports, the recent statements by the Finance Minister and Infrastructure Minister being against the law, the Finance Minister needing to open files and take action according to laws instead of issuing threats, the need for the report of the committee formed by the Home Minister to investigate to be made public, the need for controversial statements by ministers to be removed from the parliamentary record, the need for the government to make public the facts regarding the Indian side building a high embankment in Rautahat and obstructing the flow of the Lalbakaiya and Bagmati rivers, and the need to provide land ownership certificates to locals residing on government land, among other issues. They expressed their views on these matters: Honorable Herka Raj Rai, Honorable Parmesh Kumar Hamal, Honorable Rekha Kumari Yadav, Honorable Saraswati Lama, Honorable Tukabhadra Hamal.
Meanwhile, while Honorable Rekha Kumari Yadav was speaking, Honorable Herkaraj Rai stood in front of his seat, and the Right Honorable Speaker informed that time would be given to the Honorable Member, after which he took his seat.
After that, the Right Honorable Speaker informed that there was no Zero Hour or Special Time scheduled for today.
Subsequently, during the impromptu session that commenced, the following Honorable Members stated that the Finance Minister's threat of opening files and the Infrastructure Minister's statement of breaking a contractor's legs were unfortunate; the need to develop the Hilsa border point in Humla as an international dry port; that exporting Karnali's produce to China, the world's largest market, would greatly support the country's overall economy; that the proposed budget completely overlooked Karnali's potential; that there was no clear government policy regarding the commercialization of products that Karnali can export through the quarantine established by the government at the Hilsa border point in Humla; the need to blacktop the road in Humla and the Karnali corridor; that the government's decisions and activities have put the very existence of private media at risk; that neighboring countries have released weather forecasts predicting flash floods during the monsoon and dry and hot winds in the Terai districts due to continuous heat, hence the government needs to make arrangements for disaster response, relief, and rescue; that police investigations show youth are falling into drug addiction and engaging in organized crime, and Nepal is becoming a transit point for the drug trade, so the state needs to formulate and implement a concrete action plan; the need to diplomatically resolve the problems arising in Susta, Nepal's territory; the need for petroleum product price adjustment; that Man Bahadur Rawal was injured due to lightning in Bajura and 16 of his livestock died, so the government should provide compensation; the need to include the road from Lubhu Lamatar to Dakhal-Panauti in the national highway standard; that in Ward No. 3 of Morang district, land that has been occupied and cultivated for a long time is being bought and sold without ownership certificates, so to prevent problems arising from this, the Land Revenue, local levels, and courts should coordinate; that Ujjwal Kunwar from Dhading, who went to Cyprus under various inducements, has been held hostage, beaten, and tortured by Pakistani individuals, and his family has been threatened with death if they do not pay money, so the government must rescue him; that the Ministry of Foreign Affairs should take initiative to bring the body of Deepak Dhami from Bajhang, who passed away tragically in Osaka, Japan, to Nepal; that action should be taken against those who attacked and robbed Shailesh Singh from Bajhang in Dhangadhi; that the Indian side has unilaterally raised the embankment in the Gaur-Baginiya border area of Rautahat, causing problems, and the government needs to resolve these issues diplomatically; among other matters. They expressed their views on these issues: Honorable Jagdish Kharel (Lalitpur), Honorable Jayapati Rokaya, Honorable Ashish Gajurel, Honorable Ganesh Karki, Honorable Sajida Khatun Siddiqui, Honorable Ashika Tamang, Honorable Narendra Kumar Gupta, Honorable Ain Bahadur Mahar, Honorable Bhumika Limbu Subba, Honorable Shahjahan Khatun, Honorable Janakraj Giri.
Subsequently, the Right Honorable Speaker read out the following letter from the Office of the Prime Minister and Council of Ministers: "Regarding the formal visit to the People's Republic of China by the Honorable Minister for Foreign Affairs, Shishir Khanal, who departed for the destination on Jestha 31, 2083, it is requested that during his absence, the responsibilities of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which he has been handling, be assigned to the Honorable Finance Minister, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, by the Right Honorable Prime Minister. (Suman Raj Aryal) Date: 2083/03/02 Chief Secretary."
Thereafter, the Right Honorable Speaker informed that the discussion on various headings of the Appropriation Bill, 2083, would commence collectively under the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, Ministry of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Ministry of Physical Infrastructure Development, Ministry of Home Affairs, Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Defense, Office of the Prime Minister and Council of Ministers, National Planning Commission Secretariat, Office of the Vice President, and Office of the President, and that proposals for expenditure reduction under the appropriation headings related to the mentioned ministries have been submitted by 11 Honorable Members in accordance with clauses (a), (b), and (c) of sub-rule (1) of rule 146 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083.
Subsequently, the following proposing Honorable Members of the expenditure reduction proposals presented their proposals for expenditure reduction and participated in the discussion on the appropriation headings scheduled for today: Honorable Guru Prasad Baral, Honorable Parvati B.K., Honorable Ain Bahadur Mahar, Honorable Ramesh Kumar Malla, Honorable Balawati Sharma, Honorable Pushpa Raj Kandel, Honorable Bishnumaya B.K., Honorable Parshuram Tamang, Honorable Yagya Bahadur Bogati.
After that, Honorable Vikram Thapa participated in the discussion on the appropriation headings scheduled for today.
Subsequently, as the attendance of ministers was low for the discussion on the appropriation headings scheduled for today, and the meeting could not proceed without the presence of all concerned ministers, the Right Honorable Speaker adjourned the meeting for 15 minutes.
Thereafter, the meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 1:45 PM, to reconvene after 15 minutes.
The meeting of the House of Representatives, which was adjourned for 15 minutes today, commenced at 2:55 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
Subsequently, the following Honorable Members participated in the discussion on the appropriation headings scheduled for today under the Appropriation Bill, 2083: Honorable Khadga Shahi, Honorable Bharat Kumar Swar, Honorable Hari Prasad Bhusal, Honorable Rajendra Kumar Rai, Honorable Ninu Kumari Karna, Honorable Yuvaraj Dulal, Honorable Herkaraj Rai, Honorable Dharma Raj K.C., Honorable Khushbu Oli, Honorable Madan Krishna Shrestha, Honorable Punam Kumari Agarwal, Honorable Deepak Kumar Sah (Sunsari), Honorable Mohan Acharya, Honorable Manmaya B.K., Honorable Mahendra Bahadur Shahi, Honorable Tara Bishwakarma, Honorable Shahjahan Khatun, Honorable Radhika Ramtel, Honorable Rahbar Ansari, Honorable Rajani Shrestha, Honorable Gita Kumari Sendad, Honorable Prakash Singh Kaki, Honorable Rachu Neupane, Honorable Gopal Sharma, Honorable Arvind Sah, Honorable Harina Devi Kami, Honorable Kamal Subedi, Honorable Pabitra B.K., Honorable Sunil K.C., Honorable Pradip Bisht, Honorable Nitima Bhandari (Karki), Honorable Sandip Rana, Honorable Prakashchandra Pariyar, Honorable Pukar Bam, Honorable Rina Upreti, Honorable Ram Lama, Honorable Badan Kumar Bhandari, Honorable Bharat Prasad Parajuli, Honorable Sushant Vaidik, Honorable Ankita Thakur, Honorable Sulabh Kharel, Honorable Buddhaprasad Pant, Honorable Dhanendra Karki, Honorable Smriti Century.
Meanwhile, after Honorable Bharat Kumar Swar finished speaking, Honorable Matu Bhusal took impromptu time to draw the government's attention to the need to investigate the incident of Sarita Bhandari of Banganga Municipality-7, Kapilvastu, committing suicide by jumping from the Jholujhe bridge at Supa Deurali, Arghakhanchi, and to conduct public awareness programs to reduce such incidents.
Thereafter, after Honorable Rajendra Kumar Rai spoke, Honorable Ain Bahadur Mahar, citing Rule 20 (a) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, requested that when the Right Honorable Speaker enters the chamber, everyone should stand to show respect, and that today, two ministers did not stand to show respect, and requested that the video record be checked and action be taken.
Subsequently, the Right Honorable Speaker informed that the concerned ministers would provide answers to the questions raised in the discussion on the appropriation headings related to the Ministry of Youth, Employment and Entrepreneurship; Ministry of Industry, Commerce and Supplies; Ministry of Agriculture, Forests and Environment; Ministry of Information and Communication; Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation; Ministry of Health and Population; Ministry of Land Management, Cooperatives and Poverty Alleviation; and Ministry of Women, Children, Senior Citizens and Social Security, on Ashadh 3, 2083.
After that, the Right Honorable Speaker presented a condolence motion on the demise of a former member of the House of Representatives, which was unanimously passed: "Condolence Motion: This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses sorrow over the untimely demise of Mangal Prasad Gupta, who was elected from Kapilvastu District Constituency No. 3 in the 2079 House of Representatives election from the Communist Party of Nepal (UML), was active in democratic movements, and contributed to the strengthening of the parliamentary system, at the age of 77 on Jestha 24, 2083, and offers tribute and condolences to the bereaved family."
Thereafter, the House stood in silence for 1 minute for the eternal peace of the soul of the late Mangal Prasad Gupta.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 7:05 PM to be held on Wednesday, Ashadh 3, 2083, at 11:00 AM.
Padma Prasad Pandey
