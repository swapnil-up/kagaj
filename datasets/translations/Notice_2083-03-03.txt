(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Wednesday, Ashadh 3, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, when the Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Shram Sanskriti Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood up in front of their seats and requested time to speak, the Right Honourable Speaker granted them time. The Honourable Members expressed their views on issues such as the government immediately providing relief and resettlement to the landless squatters displaced by the "Dozar" terror, the protest will continue until the Prime Minister corrects his statement on border issues made in Parliament, the Speaker, who is supposed to be the guardian of the House, has not been able to sever ties with Rastriya Prajatantra Party, and the Prime Minister and Ministers are accountable to the House as per Article 76, Sub-article 10 of the Constitution.

Meanwhile, after Honourable Gopal Sharma finished speaking, Honourable Lal Vikram Thapa raised a point of order, stating that the word "arojak" (anarchic) used by the speaker Honourable Member towards the Prime Minister and the words used by Honourable Ganesh Karki after Honourable Chandra Mohan Yadav finished speaking, were in violation of Rule 21(b) of the Regulations, and should be removed from the parliamentary records. The Right Honourable Speaker drew the attention of the Honourable Members to Rule 21, Clause (b) of the Regulations.
During the impromptu session that followed, the following Honourable Members expressed their views: State investment should be made only after determining the age of the current fiscal year; the allocated budget is not balanced; scientific surveys of the country's hydropower production capacity and potential should be conducted; the instruction given to facilitate the supply of medicines for cancer patients has not been effective, and the government should intensify its monitoring; the problems of tea industrialists and farmers should be addressed; the issues of the women protesting at Maitighar should be heard; the Nepali women who went for foreign employment in Kuwait but are in Kuwaiti jails and stranded should be immediately rescued; although the project under construction by a private entity in Sankhuwasabha benefits electricity and irrigation, it has directly impacted the local economy and damaged natural resources; the government's courageous step to remove squatters has turned into recklessness due to the death of Indrabahadur Rai, Rabin Tamang and Ravi Tamang's wife, and Bhojraj Darji during treatment; the driver driving recklessly in Morang, causing the death of Indrabahadur Shrestha, should be punished; 90 percent of women MPs are also affected by cyberbullying, and cyberbullying incidents should be controlled; the bridge under construction over the Beni River in Gurmakot, Surkhet, should be completed on time; the Chaite paddy planted in Tripurasundari Rural Municipality, Dadeldhura, is affected by a disease called Neck Blight, and it should be controlled; Dharmabahadur Rai and Ral Bahadur Rai, residents of Manahari Rural Municipality, Makwanpur, were injured in a bear attack while grazing buffaloes, and budget should be allocated for the management of human-wildlife conflict and control of monkeys and wild boars; the government's attention should be drawn to the provision of proper health treatment at Okhaldhunga District Hospital; citizens in various districts have to face great risk daily due to the lack of roads, and the government should allocate the necessary budget for physical infrastructure; the supply of chemical fertilizers for farmers in various districts should be ensured; hospitals operating without renewing their registration should be regulated, and labour exploitation of health workers should be investigated and the guilty punished; the Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation should pay attention to the incident where the "Work for Peace" team, traveling with a message of world peace, was denied flight permission by Air India while en route to Delhi; the participation of the Nepali women's national football team in the 20th Asian Games should be ensured; an environment should be created to watch the World Cup Football competition for free on national television; the government should address the issue of land possession by farmers in Parshuram, Dadeldhura, which has been restricted by the District Forest Office, leaving the future of 400 farmers uncertain; the Prime Minister's statement on border issues should be removed from parliamentary records; the government's stance on past political movements should be clarified; the National Medical Education Commission meeting chaired by the Prime Minister passed related procedures, and individuals not recognized by law should not be present as Vice-Chairperson; seven girl students fell ill at Manpur Secondary School in Bardiya, forcing the school to close, and the government should address this; Aryan Singh, a grade 12 student at DAV College, Lalitpur, recently committed suicide, and the government should implement mental health awareness programs.
