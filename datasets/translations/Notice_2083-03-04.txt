(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Thursday, Ashar 4, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:00 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the commencement of the meeting, upon Honourable Members from the Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Labour Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party standing up in front of their seats and requesting time to speak, the Right Honourable Speaker granted time to the following Honourable Members, who raised concerns regarding the objectionable act of the Prime Minister's Office and Council of Ministers forcing the issuance of arrest warrants by detaining the Chief and Officials of the Commission; the need for a parliamentary inquiry committee to ascertain the facts of the matter; the public disclosure of the report of the investigation commission against the Home Minister; the need for a parliamentary investigation into tax evasion in the import of electric vehicles (EVs); the removal of the Prime Minister's statement on national borders from parliamentary records as it was anti-national; the presence of the Minister for Defence in the House as the agenda included discussion on the title related to the Ministry of Defence under the Appropriation Bill; and the need to reduce the price of petroleum products, among other issues:-
1. Honourable Narendra Kumar Kerung
2. Honourable Harka Raj Rai
3. Honourable Laxmi Prasad Pokharel
4. Honourable Tahir Ali Bhat
5. Honourable Ramesh Kumar Mall
Meanwhile, after Honourable Ramesh Kumar Mall finished speaking, Honourable Ain Bahadur Mahar raised a point of order, drawing attention to the need to maintain parliamentary decorum, as ministers have been observed walking between the Speaker and the speaking member, which is contrary to Rule 20(ch) of the Rules of Procedure.

Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented Sections 2 to 11 of the Bill and related Schedules 1 and 2 for decision as the Appropriation Bill, 2083, which was approved by a majority.
Then, the Right Honourable Speaker read out the preamble and title of the Bill and presented it for decision as the Appropriation Bill, 2083, which was approved by a majority.
Subsequently, the proposal moved by the Honourable Minister for Finance, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, 'That the Appropriation Bill, 2083 be passed,' was presented by the Right Honourable Speaker for decision and was approved by a majority.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled to be held on Friday, Ashar 5, 2083, at 11:00 AM, and the House adjourned at 7:40 PM.
