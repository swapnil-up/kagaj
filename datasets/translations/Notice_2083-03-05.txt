(Brief Record of Meeting Proceedings)
Friday, Ashar 5, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Shram Sanskriti Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and requested time to speak. The Right Honourable Speaker granted time to the following Honourable Members, who raised issues including:
- The Prime Minister's Office compelling the issuance of arrest warrants by detaining officials of the Commission for Investigation of Abuse of Authority;
- Misbehavior with the German Ambassador;
- The recommendation committee formed to appoint university officials being formed on a party basis, thus making the recommended officials not impartial and undermining university autonomy;
- The government's secret reports regarding the Suguali Treaty and border encroachment being contradicted by the Prime Minister's statement, and the response from the Minister of Foreign Affairs being unsatisfactory;
- The Minister of Physical Infrastructure and Transport being required to respond in the meeting regarding the statement made about breaking a contractor's leg.
1. Honourable Khadak Bahadur Buda
2. Honourable Harka Raj Rai
3. Honourable Khusbu Oli
4. Honourable Guru Prasad Baral
5. Honourable Ganesh Bahadur Bishwakarma
Subsequently, the following Honourable Members participated in the impromptu session, raising issues such as:
- The placement of Bhagavad Gita in the pigeonhole and the playing of bhajans before the meeting starting being contrary to the spirit of secularism;
- The need for an investigation into the incident where police conducted a search at night in the absence of a Law Minister in Koshi Province;
- The unallocated budget kept in the Ministry of Physical Infrastructure and Transport being allocated for various roads in Surkhet;
- The gold allegedly missing from the Rastriya Banijya Bank, Baneshwor branch during the 'Genji' movement being stolen, and the victims of gold confiscation being subjected to the bank's unilateral decision.
1. Honourable Vishnu Bahadur Khadka
2. Honourable Parvati BK
3. Honourable Rajendra Kumar Rai
4. Honourable Aaren Rai
5. Honourable Indra Mani Parajuli
Meanwhile, Honourable Som Sharma, by making a point of order, drew attention to the fact that Honourable Members were taking impromptu time without informing the Right Honourable Speaker, which is against the provisions of the rules. In this regard, the Right Honourable Speaker drew the attention of the Honourable Members to the provision in Rule 14 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2083.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that there was no Zero Hour or Special Time scheduled for today.
Subsequently, the Honourable Jan Bahadur Shahi, the Parliamentary Party Leader of Rastriya Prajatantra Party, presented his views.
Thereafter, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented a proposal for consideration of the "Economic Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle responded to the questions raised by the following Honourable Members in the discussion on the proposal:
1. Honourable Khusbu Oli
2. Honourable Gangalakshmi Awal
3. Honourable Ganesh Parajuli
4. Honourable Indira Rana Magar
5. Honourable Pushpa Raj Kandel
6. Honourable Krishna Hari Budhathoki
7. Honourable Ganesh Bahadur Bishwakarma
8. Honourable Gopal Sharma
9. Honourable Harka Raj Rai
10. Honourable Guru Prasad Baral
Thereafter, the proposal presented by the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle, "Let the Economic Bill, 2083 be passed," was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was passed unanimously.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to submit amendments to the "Economic Bill, 2083" as per Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2083, can register their amendment proposals at the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singhdurbar, and that the time for discussion on the bill will be scheduled after the management work begins on Ashar 11, 2083, as per Rule 142(3) of the House of Representatives Rules, 2083.
After that, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented a proposal for consideration of the "National Loan Raising Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle responded to the questions raised by the following Honourable Members in the discussion on the proposal:
1. Honourable Aaren Rai
2. Honourable Badan Kumar Bhandari
3. Honourable Deepak Kumar Sah (Sunsari)
4. Honourable Parshuram Tamang

8. Honourable Harka Raj Rai
9. Honourable Guru Prasad Baral
10. Honourable Surya Bahadur Tamang
11. Honourable Parasmani Gelal
Thereafter, the proposal presented by the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle, "Let the National Loan Raising Bill, 2083 be passed," was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was passed unanimously.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to submit amendments to the "National Loan Raising Bill, 2083" as per Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2083, can register their amendment proposals at the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singhdurbar.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that notices to "Reject the Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2083" were received from Honourable Ganesh Singh Thaguna and Honourable Khadak Bahadur Buda as per Rule 94(1) of the House of Representatives Rules, 2083, and that as per Rule 94(3) of the same rules, the notice from Honourable Khadak Bahadur Buda was selected by mutual agreement.
Subsequently, Honourable Khadak Bahadur Buda presented his proposal to reject the ordinance.
Subsequently, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balen Shah, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam responded to the questions raised during the presentation of the proposal.
After that, as Honourable Khadak Bahadur Buda did not wish to withdraw his proposal to "Reject the Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2083," the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal for decision, and it was rejected by a majority.
Subsequently, as the proposal to "Reject the Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2083" was rejected, the proposal presented by the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balen Shah, "Approve the Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2083," was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was approved unanimously.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that a notice to "Reject the Prevention of Money Laundering (Third Amendment) Ordinance, 2083" was received from Honourable Neeta Ghataani as per Rule 95(1) of the House of Representatives Rules, 2083.
Subsequently, as the proponent Honourable Neeta Ghataani was absent, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal to "Reject the Prevention of Money Laundering (Third Amendment) Ordinance, 2083" for decision, and it was rejected unanimously.
Subsequently, as the proposal to "Reject the Prevention of Money Laundering (Third Amendment) Ordinance, 2083" was rejected, the proposal presented by the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle, "Approve the Prevention of Money Laundering (Third Amendment) Ordinance, 2083," was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was approved unanimously.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that notices to "Reject the Cooperatives (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2083" were received from Honourable Aaren Rai, Honourable Khusbu Oli, and Honourable Sajida Khatun Siddiqui, and that as per Rule 95(3) of the rules, the notice from Honourable Aaren Rai was selected by mutual agreement.
After that, Honourable Aaren Rai presented his proposal to reject the ordinance.
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Poverty Alleviation Pratibha Rawal responded to the questions raised during the presentation of the proposal.
Subsequently, the following Honourable Members requested impromptu time and presented their views on issues such as:
- The destruction of bridges due to floods and landslides in Panchthar, leading to road blockages and severed communication;
- The death of Thet Bahadur Dudel, who was injured during efforts to drive away monkeys in Manthali, Ramechhap, and the need for government attention to this incident;
- The fire in Butwal due to electrical leakage causing significant property damage, and the need for government attention to this.
1. Honourable Narendra Kumar Kerung
2. Honourable Dhurba Raj Rai
3. Honourable Manju Bhusal
After that, as Honourable Aaren Rai did not wish to withdraw his proposal to "Reject the Cooperatives (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2083," the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal for decision, and it was rejected by a majority.
Subsequently, as the proposal to "Reject the Cooperatives (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2083" was rejected, the proposal presented by the Honourable Minister of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Poverty Alleviation Pratibha Rawal, "Approve the Cooperatives (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2083," was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was approved unanimously.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that a notice to "Reject the Health Sciences University Related Some Nepal Acts Amendment Ordinance, 2083" was received from Honourable Kripa Ram Rana as per Rule 94(1) of the House of Representatives Rules, 2083.
Subsequently, as the proponent Honourable Kripa Ram Rana was absent, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal to "Reject the Health Sciences University Related Some Nepal Acts Amendment Ordinance, 2083" for decision, and it was rejected unanimously.
After that, as the proposal to "Reject the Health Sciences University Related Some Nepal Acts Amendment Ordinance, 2083" was rejected, the proposal presented by the Honourable Minister of Health and Population Nisha Mehta, "Approve the Health Sciences University Related Some Nepal Acts Amendment Ordinance, 2083," was presented for decision by the Right Honourable Speaker and was approved unanimously.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled to be held on Thursday, Ashar 11, 2083, at 11:00 AM, and the meeting was adjourned at 2:45 PM.
