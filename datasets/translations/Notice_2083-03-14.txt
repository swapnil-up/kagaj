Sunday, Ashad 14, 2083
The first meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:05 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, when Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepali Communist Party, and Shram Sanskriti Party stood up in front of their seats seeking time to speak, the Right Honourable Speaker granted time to the following Honourable Members, who expressed dissatisfaction that the Right Honourable Prime Minister, instead of responding in Parliament, was giving reactions based on the importance of the party's general convention.
"The Prime Minister's every statement should be considered an official political message of Nepal, and care must be taken to maintain the dignity of the office and diplomatic courtesy. The statement made by the Right Honourable Prime Minister on Jestha 17, stating that Nepal has encroached upon India's land, should be removed from the parliamentary records. The Vice-Chairman of CPN (UML), Bishnu Prasad Paudel, has been arrested based on political vendetta and charged with money laundering; he must be immediately released. It is alleged that the document passed by the Raswapa's general convention seeks to weaken the foundations of democracy and republicanism. Some ideologies that have emerged under the guise of popularity will push the country back into conflict. Promising to empower provincial and local levels while seeking votes and then adopting a policy to abolish those very structures after winning is a betrayal of the people. Was the statement made by Prime Minister Balendra Shah regarding the border in the national interest or against it? Raising this question, it was demanded that if the statement is in the national interest, it should not be removed from the record, and if it is against the national interest, it should be immediately removed. FIFA's suspension of ANFA is affecting the future of players, so the government must immediately take steps to lift the suspension."
Among other issues, they expressed their views.

Subsequently, during the impromptu session that began, the following Honourable Members expressed their views:
Regarding the tragic demise of 10-year-old girl Anukriti Chapagain at the bridge of Adhuba Khola in Birtabazar, Birtamod Municipality-3, Jhapa, heartfelt tribute and deep condolences were expressed to the bereaved family, and immediate action was demanded against the gang that threatened the victim's family and protected the perpetrator. The government must immediately take steps to lift FIFA's suspension of ANFA. The access road to the Arun-3 Hydropower Project in Sankhuwasabha has deteriorated due to rain and traffic, so the road needs to be upgraded. Just as citizens pay fines for violating traffic rules, the state should also make a law requiring compensation and fines to citizens for its shortcomings. Furthermore, action should be taken against the concerned bodies and construction entrepreneurs for accidents caused by the poor condition of the road. The potholes on the sides of the Butwal-Gorusinge road section of the East-West Highway are affecting daily life, so the concerned bodies should pay attention to the construction of this road. There is a shortage of anti-snake venom and anti-rabies vaccine in hospitals and health institutions in Kapilvastu, so these vaccines must be provided. The construction of roads including the BP Highway and the road from Ilam to Taplejung must be repaired and maintained as per the agreement as soon as possible. Although the Government of Nepal has approved the operation of Lamkichuha Hospital in Kailali as a provincial hospital for a long time, it has not been able to operate due to internal reasons of the municipality and the province, so immediate steps should be taken for its operation. The government has ordered the eviction of the hold center where the landless are housed, so an alternative arrangement must be made. Narayan Prasad Parajuli of Thulung Dudhkoshi, Solukhumbu, was seriously injured by an electric shock, so free treatment and compensation must be arranged. The National Examination Board hastily published the results of Grade 12, causing hardship to thousands of students and parents, so an impartial investigation into the errors in the results must be conducted and the guilty punished.
Among other issues, they expressed their views.
