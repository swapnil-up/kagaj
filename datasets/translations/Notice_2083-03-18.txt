(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Thursday, Ashar 18, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, as the Honorable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Communist Party of Nepal, and Labour Culture Party stood in front of their seats seeking time to speak, the Honorable Speaker granted time to the following Honorable Members. They alleged that the government had trampled upon the rule of law and good governance, demanding an impartial and lawful investigation into the financial irregularities in the passport printing procurement process. They stated that the current government was not accountable to the people and had resorted to politics of revenge. They urged the Honorable Prime Minister to be the Prime Minister of the country, not of the streets. They also raised concerns that the problems of economic conditions, inflation, taxes, and the stock market had caused distress to the general public. They called for immediate government action to control bird flu and Dengue fever. They argued that the draft of the proposed Criminal Code (Amendment) Bill, 2083, attacked fundamental rights such as freedom of thought and expression guaranteed by the constitution. They demanded that the Prime Minister retract his statement about India encroaching on Nepal's borders and that foreign policy should be based on principles. They also stated that the government had violated the fundamental rights guaranteed by the Constitution of Nepal and urged the government not to treat squatters like criminals, but to ensure their proper management and respect for human dignity. Subsequently, during the impromptu session that commenced, the following Honorable Members spoke on various contemporary issues:
It was requested that immediate search be conducted for Rewanta Bisht from Mangalsen Municipality, Achham, who has been missing for the past month from Kirtipur where he resides. It was requested that the body of Rakesh Kumar Patel from Pasang Lhamu Municipality, who died in Australia during his employment, be brought to Nepal immediately and handed over to his family. Due to continuous rainfall in Silichong Rural Municipality, Sankhuwasabha, floods have disrupted physical structures, roads, and communication, affecting the lives of local residents. The government was urged to provide immediate relief, rescue, and rehabilitation. In Baglung, Dadeldhura, and Gulmi, it was demanded that proper compensation be provided to those who died in incidents related to electricity supply and maintenance, and that the injured be provided free treatment, and that an investigation be conducted and action taken against the guilty. It was reported that Babu Ram Tamata died in Raskot Municipality, Kalikot, while digging with a dozer, and Ravindra Patel died in Humla due to lightning strikes. The government was urged to seriously address the issue of squatters. In Chaudandigadhi Municipality, Udayapur, the increasing elephant menace has caused significant loss of life and property, and proper compensation should be provided, and the government should seriously address the elephant menace. It was requested that Anti-Rabies Vaccine be made available in all hospitals as Mohan Hamal of Kamalamai Municipality, Sindhuli, died of rabies after being bitten by a fox. A girl died by drowning in a dam in Ghorahi, Dang; it was requested that the dam be fenced and local authorities be held responsible. Thereafter, the Honorable Minister of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Poverty Alleviation, Pratibha Rawal, responded to the questions raised by the Honorable Members during the discussion and informed that the following amendments were accepted on behalf of the Government of Nepal:
"Amendment to Sub-section (2) of Section 9 of the Bill as follows:
(2) After Sub-section (8), the following Sub-sections (9) and (10) have been added:-
(9) Notwithstanding anything else written in this section, cooperative societies shall not conduct savings and loan transactions.
However, cooperative societies at the district, provincial, and central levels that are conducting savings and loan transactions at the time of the commencement of this sub-section shall have a period of three years from the date of commencement of this sub-section to form a cooperative bank in accordance with Section 12 and Chapter-8 of the Act, or to merge with a cooperative bank in operation in accordance with Section 87, without any hindrance.
(10) The Authority shall monitor and facilitate the process as per Sub-section (9)."
