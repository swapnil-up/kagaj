(Brief Record of Meeting Proceedings)
Wednesday, Asar 24, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:15 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, when Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Labour Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and sought time to speak, the Right Honourable Speaker granted time to Honourable Pramila Kumari Gachchhadar. She stated that due to the expression given by the Right Honourable Prime Minister in the House, the people at the Nepal-India border crossings are facing problems, and the government needs to be serious about resolving the issue of squatters.
After that, when Honourable Rajendra Kumar Rai was speaking, he asked the Right Honourable Speaker, "Can you hear or not?" The Right Honourable Speaker stated that the words used by the Honourable Member during his speech constituted contempt of the House and the Speaker, and urged him to present his views within the bounds of decorum.
Meanwhile, Honourable Tika Sangraula, citing the rules of procedure, expressed optimism that the young Honourable Members of the ruling party would learn parliamentary procedures from the experienced and veteran leaders in the opposition. However, she drew the attention of the House to the violation of Rule 21(b) of the Regulations by the Honourable Members of the opposition, and demanded that the words "Can you hear or not?" used by Honourable Rajendra Kumar Rai be expunged from the House records.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker granted time to Honourable Rajendra Kumar Rai to speak after correcting his language and seeking an apology. Upon his further statement that the Speaker's behavior would prevent the House from functioning for five years, the Right Honourable Speaker adjourned the meeting to reconvene 15 minutes later today.
Thereafter, the meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 11:30 AM to reconvene 15 minutes later today.
The meeting of the House of Representatives, which was adjourned to reconvene 15 minutes later today, commenced at 12:35 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.

Thereafter, Honourable Ganga Laxmi Awal took emergency time to draw the government's attention to the suicide attempt by a mother and son in Kamalpokhari, Bhaktapur, and the problems faced by employees working at the Juddha Barun Yantra in Bhaktapur.
Subsequently, Honourable Padama Kumari Aryal, leader of the Communist Party of Nepal (UML), expressed her views on contemporary issues.
Thereafter, Prakash Adhikari, Secretary of the House of Representatives, informed about the message received from the National Assembly regarding the "Appropriation Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed about a minor adjustment made to the day's agenda.
Thereafter, the proposal presented by Honourable Khadga Raj Poudel, Minister for Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation, that the "Tourism Bill, 2082", originating from the National Assembly, be sent to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion, was presented by the Right Honourable Speaker for decision and was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the proposal presented by Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle that the "Nepal Rastra Bank (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083" be sent to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion, was presented by the Right Honourable Speaker for decision and was unanimously approved.

The meeting of the House of Representatives, which was adjourned at 2:40 PM today to reconvene 15 minutes later, was adjourned to meet on Thursday, Asar 24, 2083, at 11:00 AM, as published in Notice Paper-9.
