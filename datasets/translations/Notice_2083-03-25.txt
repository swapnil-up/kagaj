Information Bulletin - 2
(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Thursday, Ashadh 25, 2083
The first meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:04 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, CPN (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Shram Sanskriti Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and requested time to speak. Consequently, time was granted to the following Honourable Members, who expressed their views on various issues including: objection to the statement made by the Right Honourable Prime Minister in the House meeting on Jestha 17; demanding a clear response from the Prime Minister and removal of the statement from parliamentary records; the government's immediate formulation and implementation of an action plan to resolve the problems of squatters residing in hold centers; the need to change the tracks of Sajha Yatayat buses instead of just painting Facebook walls; the enduring relevance of the ideas and contributions of leaders of old parties; and the fact that tampering with federalism, achieved through the blood of thousands of martyrs, and the executive's disregard for parliament, which the constitution makes accountable to parliament, raise questions about the very relevance of parliamentary democracy.

Meanwhile, while the Right Honourable Speaker was granting time to Honourable Mahendra Bahadur Shahi to speak, Honourable Rekha Kumari Yadav, by raising a point of order, drew the Speaker's attention to Rule 20 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, regarding Honourable Members walking between the speaker and the Honourable Member addressing the house. Similarly, after Honourable Ghyanu Poudyal finished speaking, Honourable Yuvaraj Dulal took impromptu time to inform about the incident where a man from Mugu attempted self-immolation by setting himself on fire in front of the Department of Passports, was sent to Bir Hospital for treatment, and his name and address were unknown.
Thereafter, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam presented the "Annual Report of the Attorney General 2081/082" and the "Thirty-fifth Annual Report of the Commission for the Investigation of Abuse of Authority 2081/082" to the House, respectively.
Subsequently, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "National Service Corps Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, as no notice of objection was received from any Honourable Member regarding the proposal to seek permission to introduce the bill, in accordance with Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, introduced the "National Service Corps Bill, 2083".

Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal "Consider the Bill to Revive the Previous Provisions of the Nepal Special Service Act, 2042, 2083" for decision, and it was approved by a majority.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to register amendments to the "Bill to Revive the Previous Provisions of the Nepal Special Service Act, 2042, 2083" in accordance with Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, can submit them to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singhdarbar.
Thereafter, the House of Representatives was adjourned until 5:20 PM, with the next meeting to be held 15 minutes later today.
The second meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 4:04 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, Honourable Kh. Shahi, taking impromptu time, drew attention to the fact that a youth from Mugu, who was fined ten thousand rupees by the municipal police for locking the passport department, attempted suicide due to mental distress, is undergoing treatment at Bir Hospital, and requested that his treatment be free of charge and that action be taken against the culprits.
Thereafter, the Secretary of the House of Representatives, Prakash Adhikari, informed about the messages received from the National Assembly regarding the "Economic Bill, 2083" and the "Public Debt Raising Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, the Secretary of the House of Representatives, Prakash Adhikari, tabled the bills along with messages received from the National Assembly regarding the "Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Bill, 2083", "Prevention of Money Laundering (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083", "Cooperative (First Amendment) Bill, 2083", and "Bill to Amend Some Nepal Laws Related to Health Science Institute, 2083".
Thereafter, the meeting was adjourned until 6:05 PM, with the next meeting of the House of Representatives to be held 15 minutes later today.

The third meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 6:20 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam presented the proposal "Consider the Amendments Made by the National Assembly to the Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, as no Honourable Members registered to participate in the discussion, the proposal "Consider the Amendments Made by the National Assembly to the Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Bill, 2083" was presented by the Right Honourable Speaker for decision and was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, Honourable Minister of Finance Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented the proposal "Consider the Amendments Made by the National Assembly to the Prevention of Money Laundering (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, as no Honourable Members registered to participate in the discussion, the proposal "Consider the Amendments Made by the National Assembly to the Prevention of Money Laundering (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083" was presented by the Right Honourable Speaker for decision and was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, Honourable Minister of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Poverty Alleviation Pratibha Rawal presented the proposal "Consider the Amendments Made by the National Assembly to the Cooperative (First Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, Honourable Minister of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Poverty Alleviation Pratibha Rawal responded to the question raised by Honourable Parvati B.K. during the discussion.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal "Consider the Amendments Made by the National Assembly to the Cooperative (First Amendment) Bill, 2083" for decision, and it was approved by a majority.
Subsequently, Honourable Minister of Health and Population Nisha Mehta presented the proposal "Consider the Amendments Made by the National Assembly to the Bill to Amend Some Nepal Laws Related to Health Science Institute, 2083".
Thereafter, as no Honourable Members registered to participate in the discussion, the proposal "Consider the Amendments Made by the National Assembly to the Bill to Amend Some Nepal Laws Related to Health Science Institute, 2083" was presented by the Right Honourable Speaker for decision and was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned until 6:45 PM, with the next meeting to be held on Friday, Ashadh 26, 2083, at 1:00 PM.
