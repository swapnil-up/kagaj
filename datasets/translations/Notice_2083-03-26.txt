Friday, Ashad 26, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:14 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, Communist Party of Nepal (UML), Communist Party of Nepal, Labour Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood before their seats and requested time to speak. When the Right Honourable Speaker granted them time, they raised issues including: Ganesh Nepali of Mugu attempting self-immolation due to mental distress caused by the atrocities of the municipal police, necessitating free treatment and action against the culprits; the government being focused on increasing likes on social media, disregarding humanitarian sensitivity; Indian border security forces and Indian military helicopters entering Nepali territory in Susta and Daula without permission; Nepal not issuing any reaction to the Dalai Lama's birthday celebration; and questioning whether the state's policy is only for the rich and powerful or also for the poor.

Subsequently, during the impromptu session that commenced, Honourable Members raised issues including: the self-immolation incident of Ganesh Nepali of Mugu, raising questions in parliament about the past statements of the former Mayor of Kathmandu Metropolitan City, Balen Shah; Ganesh Nepali attempting self-immolation after the Kathmandu Metropolitan Police put a lock on his motorcycle; the situation where citizens are forced to set themselves on fire being a failure of the state; citizens feeling secure only through justice, employment, and good governance, not by changing green buses to blue; traffic rules and fines being for road discipline, and the state needing to maintain efficient road infrastructure; the state needing to regulate ride-sharing services with proper signals or signs; the government needing to be serious about resolving the landless problem; the problem of transportation due to the delay in the construction of the bridge over the Ratuwa River in Jhapa, necessitating its prompt construction; the compulsion to go to India for treatment of complex diseases like jaundice, and the need to establish a jaundice treatment hospital within the country; expressing heartfelt condolences on the discovery of the body of Sirjana Khadka, who went missing in a vehicle accident in Dailekh; the Prime Minister's activities being contrary to democratic values and principles, and contemptuous of parliament, the President's office, and the court; the severe damage caused to structures including bridges on the trekking route by the massive landslide in Annapurna Rural Municipality, Kaski, necessitating government initiative for transportation and trail maintenance; the state taking the incident of Ganesh Nepali seriously and formulating a mental health policy; the Kathmandu Metropolitan City needing to focus on management rather than just revenue collection from parking; and the need for the government to be led by someone who understands the characteristics of Nepali society.

Meanwhile, after Honourable Badan Kumar Bhandari finished speaking, Honourable Bodh Narayan Khadka took impromptu time and drew attention to the fact that Ramesh Bahadur Pandey and Naveen Hussain Mansuri, working at the Gurkha Hydropower in Ruby Valley Rural Municipality, Dhading, went missing due to a flood in a local river, urging for intensified search operations and effective implementation of workers' welfare and insurance. Subsequently, Honourable Khushbu Oli, on behalf of the Rastriya Prajatantra Party Parliamentary Party, expressed her views on contemporary issues. Following this, Honourable Home Minister Sudhan Gurung stated that the government had made all efforts for the life of Ganesh Nepali in his self-immolation attempt, that the federal, provincial, and local governments have their respective jurisdictions, that the traffic police had no role in the incident's location, that the government was merely implementing the law and urged against politicizing the incident, and that the Home Ministry had formed a committee to investigate the incident. Meanwhile, after the response of Honourable Home Minister Sudhan Gurung, Honourable Gopal Sharma, while raising a point of order, stated that the Home Minister's statement was an insult to the MPs and the parliament, and demanded that the phrase "until my throat dries" used by the Honourable Minister in parliament be removed from the record. Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal "Consider the National Service Corps Bill, 2083" for decision, and it was unanimously approved. Following this, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to register amendments to the "National Service Corps Bill, 2083" in accordance with Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, could submit them to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singhdarbar. Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the program listed as item number 4 in today's agenda had been removed. Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled for Tuesday, Ashad 30, 2083, at 11:00 AM, and the meeting was adjourned at 5:50 PM.
