Federal Parliament, House of Representatives, 2082 Second Session Meeting No. 37, Federal Parliament of Nepal, Notice - 2 (Brief Minutes of Meeting Proceedings) The meeting of the House of Representatives today, Tuesday, Ashar 30, 2083, commenced at 11:05 AM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker, Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, when Honorable members from the Nepali Congress, Communist Party of Nepal (UML), Communist Party of Nepal, Labor Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood before their seats and requested time to speak, the Honorable Speaker granted time to the following Honorable members. They then voiced their opinions on issues such as the Honorable Prime Minister needing to retract his statement in the House that Nepal has encroached upon India's border and to remove the statement from parliamentary records; individuals should be punished for parking vehicles with the intention of causing mental distress in front of government offices and residences/offices of political leaders; the practice of hiring employees on contract and daily wages through contractors, contrary to the Supreme Court's order, must be immediately reconsidered; traffic rules must be applied equally to everyone, not just ordinary citizens; Ganesh Nepali's treatment, which should have been at Kirtipur Burns Hospital, was taken to Bir Hospital; the presence of unauthorized individuals loitering within security perimeters has increased; the government formed on the strength of the Genji movement is persecuting youth; the government is seeking revenge against those asking questions; and other related matters.

Khushbu Oli was speaking, the Honorable Prime Minister and the ruling party's chairman, Hon. Thereafter, Hon. Ain Bahadur Mahar took impromptu time to draw the House's attention to death threats he received by being shot by Parajuli Ram and Purna Vikram Kshetri while he was protesting the government in the House. Subsequently, the Honorable Minister for Physical Planning and Infrastructure, Sunil Lampsal, spoke about the issues raised during the House's impromptu, zero, and special sessions, stating that about 90 percent of grievances related to infrastructure have been addressed by Hello Sarkar, laws have been amended for economic reform and development, and the Public Procurement Act has been amended because even though contractors win bids through low bidding, construction is not being completed on time. He also spoke about the government's ongoing work. Meanwhile, while the Honorable Minister was speaking, Hon. Guru Prasad Baral raised a point of order, questioning the minister's statements that appeared accusatory and misleading to the MPs, and asking how the funds to be sent to the market over three years would be disbursed. Similarly, after the Honorable Minister finished speaking, Hon. Kavindra Burlakoti raised a point of order, drawing attention to the fact that according to Rule 20(j) of the Regulations, ministers should not be interrupted with questions while speaking, which was violated by the minister.

Thereafter, on behalf of the Right Honorable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, the Honorable Minister for Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the "Annual Report of the Judicial Council Fiscal Year 2081/082," "Annual Report of the Judicial Service Commission Fiscal Year 2081/082," and "Sixty-eighth Annual Report of the Public Service Commission (2081/82)" to the House, respectively.
Subsequently, the Honorable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "Bill to Amend and Repeal Some Nepal Laws Related to Finance, 2083." Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker informed the House that since no objection notice was received from any Honorable member regarding the proposal seeking permission to introduce the bill, in accordance with Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, permission had been granted to introduce the "Bill to Amend and Repeal Some Nepal Laws Related to Finance, 2083." Subsequently, the Honorable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented the "Bill to Amend and Repeal Some Nepal Laws Related to Finance, 2083." Immediately thereafter, on behalf of the Right Honorable Prime Minister Balen Shah, the Honorable Minister for Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented a proposal that "the Bill to Revive the Previous Provisions of the Nepal Special Service Act, 2042, 2083, be sent to the relevant committee for clause-wise discussion." The proposal was presented for decision and was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker presented for decision a proposal moved by the Honorable Minister for Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honorable Prime Minister Balen Shah, that "the National Service Corps Bill, 2083, be sent to the relevant committee for clause-wise discussion." The proposal was presented for decision and was unanimously approved.
The House was then adjourned until the next meeting, which will be held on Wednesday, Ashar 31, 2083, at 11:00 AM. The adjournment took place at 3:30 PM.
