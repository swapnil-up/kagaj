Federal Parliament, House of Representatives, 2082 Second Session Meeting Number - 38 Federal Parliament of Nepal, Notice - 2 (Brief Minutes of the Meeting) The meeting of the House of Representatives today, Wednesday, Ashad 31, 2083, commenced at 1:24 PM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, honorable members from the Nepali Congress, Communist Party of Nepal (UML), Communist Party of Nepal, Labor Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood before their seats and requested time to speak. The Honorable Speaker granted time to the following honorable members who expressed that the escalating events in the country are causing more despair than hope; the style of covering up issues with stunts is now outdated; the statement made by the Honorable Prime Minister needs to be retracted from the floor of the house and removed from parliamentary records; the government has remained silent even as border pillars are being replaced, altering citizens' nationality; the army had to be mobilized for border defense; the government must seriously address the landless squatters' problem; the government is excessively focused on cheap popularity and publicity-driven activities; these actions are leading the country to ruin; the rule of law must be implemented; bank loans are a significant factor in the youth's self-immolation; the arrest of Majid Ansari needs investigation, among other issues. Honorable Ambika Devi Sangrila, Honorable Gita Gurung, Honorable Ganga Devi Shrestha, Honorable Tahir Ali Bhat, Honorable Mahendra Bahadur Shahi. Subsequently, during the impromptu session, the following honorable members stated that the situation on the Mechi Highway in Ilam is dire, and the condition of roads without planning and alternative routes is deplorable, requiring the government's attention.
The removal of landless squatters has created problems, and these issues need to be resolved. The immediate reconsideration of hiring contract and daily wage employees through contractors is necessary. In Baglung district, 2-year-old Ashish Bhandari died on the spot after being trapped in a press; heartfelt condolences to the deceased and deep sympathy to the family. Mina Karki of Gulmi has gone missing while attempting to cross the Resa River in Baglung; efforts must be intensified in the search. The self-immolation of Gautam Kumar Sahani in Rautahat expresses despair towards the government. Due to a server problem at the Central Transport Office, transport services in Itahari have been disrupted, and the government must resolve this issue. In Sindhuli's Goljore Rural Municipality, a landslide on Ashad 28 buried local Hem Bahadur Dura Magar in his house, and due to road blockages, rescue and timely treatment were delayed, indicating that most roads in hilly areas are similar. Therefore, the government must relocate residents from disaster-prone areas and carry out all activities from disaster risk preparedness to disaster response and management in a timely manner. Due to a shortage of medicines, services at Nepal's Bir Hospital, Teaching Hospital, and Gangalal Hospital are affected. Health service beneficiaries are unable to receive payments due to medical mafias. Heartfelt condolences are offered to Bishnu Thapa of Baglung, who lost his life in a gas cylinder explosion in the Baudha Jorpati area, and Hemkala Bhandari, who lost her life due to electrocution, and condolences to the bereaved families of both incidents.

Deep sympathy is expressed to the families of the victims, and appropriate relief and compensation should be provided. A Mental Health Council must be formed immediately to address the growing mental health problems in the country. Furthermore, as mental health services are limited, unequal, and concentrated in the capital, the old policy should be revised and a new policy formulated. The settlements in Rautahat are at risk of flooding due to the rising Lal Bakaiya and Bagmati rivers. The Prime Minister has a moral obligation to answer the questions raised in Parliament. While the government talks about digital governance, problems with the servers of the Department of Transport Management and the Department of Land Management and Records have halted services nationwide, necessitating the government to build reliable digital infrastructure. An internal revenue office should also be established in West Nawalparasi district, among other issues.

Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker stated that during the Second Session of the House of Representatives, 2082, important bills including the Annual Policy and Program of the Government of Nepal, the Appropriation Bill, 2083, the Finance Bill, 2083, and the Loan-Raising Bill, 2083, were passed.
It is the primary responsibility of the members of the House of Representatives to listen to and understand the public's reactions, concerns, and suggestions regarding the state's policies and programs implemented, laws formulated, and services delivered by the end of the current fiscal year.
Embracing the constitutional principle and spirit that the relationship between the federal, provincial, and local levels in Nepal's Constitution is based on cooperation, coexistence, and coordination, members of the House of Representatives must also take a leading role in harmonizing development, lawmaking, and public service delivery among all three levels and connect the experiences, complexities, and expectations of provincial governments and local levels with the federal level. Since Parliament is the center of democracy, the vibrancy of Parliament is determined by how close its members are to the people, and representatives are democratic bridges connecting the people and Parliament with trust, dialogue, and accountability. The essence of representation is the dual responsibility of bringing the voices, aspirations, and expectations of the people into the parliamentary decision-making process and re-establishing parliamentary decisions among the people. Continuous interaction among the people, dialogue with the people, and reflecting public opinion in the parliamentary process are the basic parliamentary responsibilities of every honorable member.
Recognizing the need for honorable members to reach out to their constituents and citizens to strengthen the relationship among all three tiers, connect the people with Parliament, and prepare the background for extensive debate on bills, the more robust the relationship between Parliament and the people, the more accountable, participatory, and trustworthy democracy becomes.
The government has declared the upcoming fiscal year as the "Year of Lawmaking and Reform." Since Parliament has a significant role in this campaign of reviewing, amending, integrating existing laws, and enacting new ones, the members' role in upcoming meetings will be active. With this assurance, and wishing that the parliamentary break be transformed into a discourse with the people, the House of Representatives was adjourned at 6:35 PM, with the next meeting scheduled for Thursday, Ashad 14, 2083, at 11:00 AM.
