(Brief Record of Meeting Proceedings)
Thursday, Shrawan 14, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 11:05 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the commencement of the meeting, Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, Nepal Communist Party (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Labor Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood up in front of their seats and sought time to speak. The Right Honourable Speaker granted them time, during which they raised issues such as the government's silence despite one death and dozens injured in the violent incident in Captainganj, Sunsari, the government's responsibility for the incident, the need for a factual investigation and action against the guilty and compensation for the victims, the former Prime Minister and Home Minister being imprisoned and resigning following a shooting incident in Kathmandu, and whether the current Prime Minister and Home Minister should resign following the shooting in Sunsari. They also stated that the government should take moral responsibility for the incident, all parties should work together to maintain religious tolerance, the Right Honourable Prime Minister should retract his statement from the floor of the House and it should be removed from the parliamentary record, and if the Prime Minister cannot run the country, he should pave the way. They further pointed out contradictory statements from the police regarding the firing incident during the Bolbam religious procession in Sunsari, and that religious intolerance and communal tension have increased since Nepal became a secular state. Honourable Members Hon. Aaren Rai, Hon. Basana Thapa, Hon. Padama Kumari Aryal, Hon. Khusbu Oli, Hon. Gopal Sharma, and Hon. [Name missing] expressed their views on these matters.
Subsequently, during the impromptu session, Honourable Members stated that steps to incite conflict among religions and ethnic groups for political gain should cease. They drew the attention of the House to the incident of assault on doctors following the death of a patient at Narayani Hospital in Birgunj on Shrawan 9, and the subsequent nationwide strike by health workers, demanding a strict investigation and action against the guilty. They emphasized that the Prime Minister's responsibility is to prevent incidents and ensure security, not just to write a status on Facebook. They also demanded free treatment for the injured in the violent incident in Kasaganaj, Sunsari, and strict action against the perpetrators after an investigation. They called for immediate relief, temporary housing, food, and health services in the affected areas of Yadabark Rural Municipality, Panchthar, which suffered significant damage due to incessant rainfall. They urged the government to promptly address the demands of Deepa Nepali, who has been on a hunger strike at Maitighar Mandala for 103 days. They stated that the government is working to prevent communal violence and maintain religious tolerance in Sunsari, and that opposition parties also have a responsibility. They highlighted the death of Kamal Giri and Ghanshyam Giri inside a water tank of a building under construction in Salyan, emphasizing the need for attention to worker safety in construction and enclosed spaces. They demanded that all genuine landless, landless Dalits, and unorganized settlers nationwide be provided with land ownership certificates, similar to the 82 landless families in Bardiya's Badhaiyatal Rural Municipality. They suggested prioritizing positive news in the media, as negative discussions can sometimes incite people. They noted that the bridge on the Chyathapu and Falaincha section in Panchthar was washed away by floods, necessitating the construction of a Bailey bridge. They pointed out that 42 families in Ludmadhen, Panchthar, are at high risk due to a landslide, and their safe resettlement needs to be managed. They urged the government to take seriously the case of Kopila Dulal from Okhaldhunga, who was murdered in Malaysia, as the accused Hari Baraili has verbally confessed to the police, but the investigation is hampered by the delay in receiving the Malaysian police's findings, post-mortem report, CCTV footage, and other evidence. They also called for efforts to bring the deceased's body back to Nepal. They requested the government's attention to the treatment of passengers seriously injured in a vehicle accident on the Achham-Gairitaad road section on Shrawan 11, where Devi Bhandari tragically died during treatment, and in another accident involving a force vehicle traveling from Surkhet to Achham on the Karnali Highway in Dailekh on Shrawan 13. They called for immediate reconstruction efforts in Sankhuwasabha's Chainpur, where incessant rainfall damaged farmers' cardamom plantations, washed away roads and water pipelines due to landslides, leaving 220 households without water and disrupting essential services due to dilapidated roads. They extended condolences for the deaths of Dharmalal Buda from Bajura and Amar Singh Buda from Humla in a jeep accident on Asar 4 while traveling to Himli Rural Municipality, Bajura, and wished speedy recovery to the injured. They demanded a thorough investigation and action against the perpetrators in the incidents of rape of a seven-year-old girl in Baglung, the murder of Lajin Thapa by his friends in Baglung, and the death of Rashmi Rizal by burning inside a car in Tarakeswor, Kathmandu. They reported the tragic deaths of Tika Bahadur BK and his daughter Binita BK when their house collapsed due to a landslide in Lamjung, with the deceased's wife Sukamaya BK and elder daughter Asmita BK injured. They called for an impartial investigation into the incident where Samir Thapa allegedly insulted, beat, and made a woman lick his feet while performing a ritual at the Pathibhara Temple in Surkhet, broadcasting it live on Facebook, and demanded an end to violence against women. Meanwhile, after Honourable Gurupasad Baral finished speaking, Honourable Gajala Shameem Mikrani raised a point of order, demanding that the statement made regarding the investigation into the Sunsari incident, for which a committee has already been formed, and which disparaged a particular community, be expunged from the parliamentary record. Furthermore, after Honourable Rahbar Ansari finished speaking, Honourable Rekha Kumari Yadav raised a point of order, demanding that the words used by Hindu followers, "Har Har Mahadev" and "Om Namah Shivay," were uttered while consuming cannabis, be expunged from the parliamentary record.
Similarly, Honourable Chandramohan Yadav, taking impromptu time, stated that Janakpur has also become tense due to the Sunsari incident and that the government should pay attention to it, and that Nepal is a place of all religions, castes, and cultures, thus religious tolerance must be maintained.
Following this, the proposal put forth by the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle, "Let the Bill to Amend and Repeal Some Nepal Laws Related to Finance, 2083, be sent to the relevant committee for clause-wise discussion," was presented by the Right Honourable Speaker for decision and was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the condolence motion for the demise of a former member of the House of Representatives and Constituent Assembly member for decision, which was unanimously passed.
Condolence Motion
"The House of Representatives expresses sorrow over the untimely demise of Gopalman Shrestha, former Deputy Prime Minister, who served as a member of the House of Representatives from Syangja Constituency No. 1 in the year 2048 BS and from Syangja Constituency No. 2 in the year 2056 BS, a member elected from Syangja Constituency No. 2 in the Constituent Assembly election of 2064 BS and from the proportional representation category in the Constituent Assembly election of 2070 BS, who played a leading role in various movements and for the democratic system, and who also served as Minister of Education, Physical Planning and Construction, Forest and Environment, and Commerce, at the age of 79 on Shrawan 12, 2083 BS during treatment. This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses condolences on his demise and offers tribute, extending sympathy to the bereaved family."
Thereafter, the House stood for one minute of silence in memory of the late Gopalman Shrestha.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives will be held on Tuesday, Shrawan 19, 2083, at 1:00 PM, and the House adjourned at 3:30 PM.
