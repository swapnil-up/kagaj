(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Tuesday, Shrawan 19, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:05 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, as Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, Nepal Communist Party (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Shram Sanskriti Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood up in front of their seats and requested time to speak, the Right Honourable Speaker granted time to the following Honourable Members, who expressed their views on various issues including the need for action against those who disrupt social and religious harmony, the need to establish national consensus through agreement and dialogue to create a peaceful environment, that although the incident in Captain, Sunsari was moving towards resolution through political dialogue, the injured and locals were still in fear, the need for a judicial inquiry into the incident and for the Prime Minister and Home Minister to take responsibility for the incident, expressing heartfelt condolences to Nirmal Purja, a world record holder, and other mountaineers who lost their lives in an accident during the K2 expedition in Pakistan, the Prime Minister's need to respond to the House regarding national problems and border encroachment, and for the Prime Minister's statement to be removed from the record, the need to uphold the rule of law, and for Honourable Members to be able to travel freely throughout the country, the Home Minister's commendable role in controlling the incident in Madhesh, the need for everyone's initiative to respect all religions and maintain religious tolerance.

Following this, during the impromptu session, the following Honourable Members spoke, demanding an investigation into the Sunsari incident and for the government to inform the House about the incident, and for strict action against the culprits.
- The government's tendency to use batons and tear gas and then fire bullets during protests in Madhesh should end.
- The Madhesh has become peaceful due to the initiatives of the party president, Prime Minister, and Home Minister.
- The government should arrange for medicines for diseases like cancer and anti-rabies vaccine in government hospitals.
- Services provided by the state should be easily accessible to the public.
- On Shrawan 17, Ahan Khatri died in a flood in Mairongmai Rural Municipality, Ilam, and unable to bear the grief, his mother Amrita Khatri committed suicide. Therefore, risk-prone areas should be identified and attention should be paid to accident prevention.
- On Vaishakh 28, Urmila Bhatta and Nilam Bhatta were seriously injured in a bus accident while traveling from Kathmandu to Dhangadhi. The injured should receive free treatment, the insurance company should immediately provide treatment expenses, and the culprits should be punished.
- The police should be rewarded for controlling drug trafficking in Tilathi Koiladi Rural Municipality and arresting the main traffickers.
- Gajendra Narayan Hospital should be provided with necessary physical infrastructure and doctors.
- Due to the delay in the construction of bridges in the Bheri corridor, citizens of Jajarkot, Dolpa, and Rukum West are suffering. Patients are not receiving treatment, and travel is problematic. The government should pay attention to this.
- In Panchthar, more than 50 animals have shown symptoms of rabies, more than 15 animals have died, and there is a dangerous situation where rabies could spread from animals to humans. Therefore, the government should arrange for anti-rabies vaccine in hospitals and health institutions across the country.
- The Medical Education Commission is without officials. Officials, including for MBBS, should be appointed immediately.
- Those who engage in politics in the name of religion are creating regional and communal conflict, and such incidents are increasing. The state should take initiative to reduce conflict.
- On Shrawan 18, a car fell into the Sunkoshi River after being hit by a bus in Nawalpur, Sindhuli, on the BP Highway, resulting in 4 people missing. The search for the missing citizens should be intensified.
- Large vehicles should only be allowed to operate according to the road's capacity.
- Religious and ethnic conflicts are not beneficial for the country and society.
- Those who post inflammatory videos and writings on social media should be investigated and strictly punished.

Meanwhile, Honourable Prem Bahadur Byak took impromptu time and drew the government's attention to the need for the government to establish national unity and solve national problems by taking along the contributions and experiences of political parties in the democratic movement, to implement the agreement reached with the families of the deceased, to take action against those responsible for the incidents, and to prevent activities that spread communal violence in the future in Sunsari and Siraha.
Subsequently, Honourable Minister of Health and Population, Nisha Mehta, stated that there is no shortage of "Anti-Snake Venom" for snakebite treatment, with 1,269 vials of anti-venom currently in stock, and that the necessary budget and legislation for making health insurance more effective are in the drafting stage. She also spoke about the work being done by the ministry.
Thereafter, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balen Shah, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam presented the "Sixth Annual Report of the National Dalit Commission Fiscal Year 2081/082" to the House.
Following this, Honourable Bhishma Raj Aangdembe, the proposer of the motion of urgent public importance, presented a motion stating, "It is necessary for the government to make necessary arrangements for the identification, protection, and resettlement of genuine landless, squatters, and unorganized residents, ensuring safe housing, basic services, food, health, education, and livelihood continuity, providing appropriate compensation, guaranteeing permanent resettlement, and distributing land ownership certificates to genuine landless and squatters as soon as possible."
