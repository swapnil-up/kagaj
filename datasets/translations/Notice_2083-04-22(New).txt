(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Friday, Shrawan 22, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:15 PM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, as Honorable Members from the Nepali Congress, Nepal Communist Party (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Labor Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and sought time to speak, the Honorable Speaker granted time to the following Honorable Members to express condolences for the tragic accident during the Broad Peak expedition in Pakistan that claimed the lives of world record holder Nirmal Purja and other mountaineers, to demand that the Prime Minister respond to the House regarding national issues and border encroachment, and to remove the Prime Minister's statement from the record, to build a trauma center in Dhalkebar, Dhanusha, to resolve issues such as the gas shortage and black market in the market and ensure easy availability of gas to the public, to hold direct question-and-answer sessions with the Prime Minister as per Rule 46 of the House of Representatives Regulations, to address the inability of people's representatives to work according to public expectations, to make the ambassador appointment process transparent, to resolve the problems of intern doctors at Bir Hospital, to address the problems of meter- ब्याज victims, and other issues, they expressed their views: Hon. Chandra Mohan Yadav, Hon. Dhuravraj Rai, Hon. Khushbu Oli, Hon. Kripa Ram Rana, Hon. Yuvraj Dulal. Meanwhile, after Hon. Chandra Mohan Yadav finished speaking, Hon. Manish Khanal raised a point of order, drawing attention to Rule 21 (d) and Rule 15 of the House of Representatives Regulations, and demanded that the discussion be confined only to the subject for which time was sought and that a ruling be made by the Speaker. Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker urged Hon. Chandra Mohan Yadav to take his seat and directed the government to respond to the issues raised by the Honorable Member. Subsequently, during the impromptu session that began, the following Honorable Members raised issues: the need for immediate construction of a bridge due to the Mayu River diversion being washed away by continuous rainfall in Maijogmai Rural Municipality-1, Nayabazar, Ilam; the state should provide free funeral arrangements for the deceased poor and destitute citizens; the need to intensify the search for Kedar Nath Parajuli of Kashemandandevpur and cancer patient Til Kumari Parajuli, who went missing after jumping into the Sun Koshi River on Shrawan 20; expressing heartfelt condolences for the demise of 10 mountaineers including 6 Nepali mountaineers including world record holder Nirmal Purja during the Broad Peak expedition in Pakistan; ensuring timely supply of fertilizer to farmers; the death of a child from drowning in the Sun Koshi River in Yamunamai Rural Municipality-5, Rautahat due to floods; the increasing loss of life and property across the country, including Madhesh, due to floods and landslides, necessitating timely disaster preparedness, relief, and rehabilitation by the government; the impact on daily life due to the inability to hold the 20-day China-Nepal border trade fair, requiring government attention; drawing the government's attention to the allegation of posting hateful content on social media against former secretary Bhim Upadhyay, and questioning the situation of ordinary citizens when people's representatives are not safe; the need for the government to arrange for monkey caretakers and use technology for monkey control due to significant damage to orange orchards by monkeys in Baglung; the displacement of people due to houses and shelters being buried by floods and landslides in various places in Parbat, difficulties in transportation, and the submersion of Setibeni bazaar; the death of Kalpana K.C. in a vehicle accident while managing waste in Tulsipur, Dang; the increasing number of vehicle accidents annually, requiring necessary steps for accident reduction; the death of Kartik Sada of Rajpur Municipality, Sarlahi, on Shrawan 19, due to an electric shock while watching TV at a neighbor's house; the state should provide free education and health services to the disaster-affected and helpless; the need for necessary initiatives for the repatriation of Hom Bahadur Nepali from Bardiya's Bansgadi Municipality, who is imprisoned in Dubai after being defrauded while on foreign employment; the resignation of the Prime Minister and Home Minister due to the government's complete failure to maintain peace and security and address public problems; the publication in various media of thousands of citizens queuing for fertilizer in Belka, Triyuga, and Chaudandigadhi municipalities of Udaypur, necessitating timely fertilizer supply by the government; and other issues, they expressed their views: Hon. Prem Lal Chaudhary, Hon. Sagar Bhusal, Hon. Tika Sanggoula, Hon. Kamal Subedi, Hon. Khwopindra Karna, Hon. Prem Bahadur BK, Hon. Dhan Bahadur Buda, Hon. Sushil Khadka, Hon. Yashoda Kumari Baral, Hon. Gajala Shamim Mikrani, Hon. Bishwaraj Pokharel, Hon. Bhim Kumari Budhamagar, Hon. Thakur Singh Tharu, Hon. Parvati BK. Subsequently, during the impromptu session that began, the following Honorable Members expressed their views on contemporary issues: Hon. Mohan Lal Acharya, Hon. Basana Thapa, Hon. Bidushi Rana, Hon. Mo. Firdosh Alam, Hon. Bina Gurung, Hon. Yashodha Kumari, Hon. Buddhi Ratna Maharjan, Hon. Bharat Bahadur Khadka, Hon. Bharat Kumar Swar, Hon. Bharat Giri, Hon. Bhumika Limbu Subba, Hon. Bhumika Shrestha, Hon. Madan Krishna Shrestha, Hon. Manmaya BK, Hon. Madhav Bahadur Thapa, Hon. Mohan Acharya, Hon. Ramesh Kumar Malla, Hon. Ramesh Kumar Sapkota, Hon. Ramesh Prasai, Hon. Raviendra Patel, Hon. Raj Kishor Mahato, Hon. Rajan Gautam, Hon. Rajiv Khatri, Hon. Radhika Ramtel, Hon. Ram Lama, Hon. Ringla Yadav, Hon. Rima Bishwakarma, Hon. Rukesh Ranjit, Hon. Rubina Acharya, Hon. Rekha Kumari Yadav, Hon. Renuka Kaucha. Thereafter, the Honorable Minister of Industry, Commerce, and Supplies, Gauri Kumari, stated that the import of LP gas has not decreased, supply is meeting regular demand, there is no gas shortage but only a problem created by changes in the distribution process, and addressed the ministry's ongoing work. Subsequently, the leader of the Labor Culture Party parliamentary party, Hon. Harkaraj Rai, expressed his views on contemporary issues. Thereafter, during the special session that began, the following Honorable Members expressed their views on contemporary issues: Hon. Arvind Sah, Hon. Komal Jawali, Hon. Khadak Bahadur Buda, Hon. Mohammad Istiyak Rai, Hon. Mahendra Bahadur Shahi, Hon. Chandan Kumar Singh, Hon. Dhuravraj Rai, Hon. Shambhu Prasad Dhakal, Hon. Khushbu Oli, Hon. Taufik Ahmed Khan, Hon. Manish Jha, Hon. Surendra Chaudhary, Hon. Sandip Rana, Hon. Smriti Century, Hon. Sunil KC, Hon. Guru Prasad Baral, Hon. Pradip Bisht, Hon. Gopal Sharma, Hon. Ankita Thakur, Hon. Deepak Kumar Sah (Sunsari), Hon. Dhananjay Regmi, Hon. Rekha Kumari Yadav. Meanwhile, after Hon. Surendra Chaudhary finished speaking, Hon. Nishkal Rai raised a point of order, demanding that the "anti-national statement" made by the Acting Prime Minister be removed from the House record and that an apology be made from the rostrum. Similarly, after Hon. Smriti Century finished speaking, Hon. Parvati BK raised a point of order, objecting to the previous speaker's statement that border-related issues should be raised in India's parliament, and demanded that the Prime Minister's statement be removed from the parliamentary record. Similarly, Hon. Sajida Khatun Siddiki raised a point of order, stating that the allegation made by Hon. Surendra Chaudhary during his speech that "MPs from the CPN-UML have eaten India's salt" should be proven. Thereafter, the Honorable Chairperson of the State Affairs and Good Governance Committee, Hari Dhakal, presented the "Committee Report on the Bill Related to Reviving the Previous Provisions of the Nepal Special Service Act, 2042, 2083" to the House. Subsequently, on behalf of the Honorable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, the Honorable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "Constitutional Bodies (Remuneration, Service Conditions, and Facilities) (First Amendment) Bill, 2083". As no notice of objection from any Honorable Member was received as per Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honorable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Honorable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, introduced the "Constitutional Bodies (Remuneration, Service Conditions, and Facilities) (First Amendment) Bill, 2083". Thereafter, the Honorable Minister of Foreign Affairs, Shishir Khanal, presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "Passport (First Amendment) Bill, 2083". As no notice of objection from any Honorable Member was received as per Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honorable Minister of Foreign Affairs, Shishir Khanal, introduced the "Passport (First Amendment) Bill, 2083". Subsequently, the Honorable Minister of Women, Children, and Senior Citizens, Sita Wadi, presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "Human Trafficking and Transportation (Control) (First Amendment) Bill, 2083". As no notice of objection from any Honorable Member was received as per Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honorable Minister of Women, Children, and Senior Citizens, Sita Wadi, introduced the "Human Trafficking and Transportation (Control) (First Amendment) Bill, 2083". Thereafter, the Honorable Minister of Industry, Commerce, and Supplies, Gauri Kumari, presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "Mines and Minerals Bill, 2083". As no notice of objection from any Honorable Member was received as per Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honorable Minister of Industry, Commerce, and Supplies, Gauri Kumari, introduced the "Mines and Minerals Bill, 2083". Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled for Monday, Shrawan 25, 2083, at 1:00 PM, and the House was adjourned at 5:54 PM. Padmasundar Pandey, Secretary-General
