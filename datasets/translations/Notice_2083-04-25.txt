(Brief Record of Meeting Proceedings)
Monday, Shrawan 24, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:20 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, after the Honourable Members from Nepali Congress, Nepal Communist Party (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Shram Sanskriti Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and sought time to speak, the Right Honourable Speaker granted time to the following Honourable Members, urging the Prime Minister not to feel alone, demanding that the Prime Minister respond in the House regarding national issues and border encroachment, and that the Prime Minister's statement be removed from the record; ensuring timely supply of fertilizer to farmers; the health insurance program being unsuccessful; gas shortage and inflation in the market; the sight of citizens still having to queue for passports, driver's licenses, and at hospitals mocking the government's online slogan; the declaration of a Decade of Dalit Rights for the upliftment of the Dalit community; representatives being unable to work according to the expectations of the people; violence occurring under any pretext, religious or otherwise, causing damage to the country, and urging the government to adopt security, coordination, and long-term measures to stop such incidents; and other issues, they expressed their views: Hon. Renuka Kaucha, Hon. Dhuravraj Rai, Hon. Sarita Lama, Hon. Neeta Ghatani, Hon. Ganesh Bahadur Bishwakarma, Hon. Rajendra Kumar Rai, Hon. Samiksha Baskota, Hon. Kamini Kumari, Hon. Ashika Tamang, Hon. Ashish Gajurel, Hon. Ramesh Kumar Malla, Hon. Bodhnarayan Shrestha, Hon. Indira Rana Magar, Hon. Parvati BK, Hon. Tahir Ali Bhat, Hon. Prakash Singh Karki, Hon. Prakash Pathak.
Thereafter, during the impromptu session that commenced, the following Honourable Members raised issues: the need for the government to take initiative to clear the road in the Maikhola Rajduwali section of the Mechi Highway, which has been blocked due to landslides for the past 9 months; the need to declare the long-drought-stricken Bangad in Western Salyan and Simta in Eastern Surkhet as drought-affected areas and provide relief to farmers, as well as arrange for seeds and fertilizers; the need for the concerned bodies to bring smugglers into the purview of the law who are illegally extracting river boulders and sand in Dhading and other districts; the need for periodic health check-ups for drivers, following the death of the driver of an EV car carrying 18 lawyers to Dhading on Shrawan 23 due to a heart attack; drawing the government's attention to the need to immediately ascertain the status of Narendra Hemrad of Birtamod-10, Jhapa, who has been missing in the United Arab Emirates for 7 months during foreign employment, and Prem Phuyal of Bahadashi Municipality-5, who is out of contact in Saudi Arabia; the need for the police administration to intensify the search for Nabaraj Tamang of Dhankuta, who went missing while studying at a monastery in Gokarneshwor Municipality-6; drawing attention to the dire condition of Mahottari Jaleswor Prison; the need to intensify the search for missing citizens following a bus accident on the BP Highway in Nawalpur, Sindhuli on Shrawan 18, which caused a car to fall into the Sun Koshi River, resulting in 3 people going missing; the need for correctional centers to be operated according to law, otherwise they should be closed, following the death of Umesh Khadi at the Utsahit Sudhar Kendra in Suryabinayak Municipality-1, Biruwa, Bhaktapur; the need for the government to make timely arrangements for disaster preparedness, relief, and rehabilitation, as floods and landslides are causing increasing loss of life and property across the country, including Jajarkot; the need to immediately start the distribution of land ownership certificates, as the propaganda that village block land in Rohini Rural Municipality, Rupandehi, was distributed to squatters is false, and 1.2 million applications from genuine squatters are stuck in the ministry; and the need to immediately halt the decision made by the Council of Ministers of the Government of Nepal on Shrawan 20 to dissolve the Nepal Intermodal Transport Development Board (NITDB) and merge it with the Nepal Transit and Warehousing Company Limited (NTWCL), and instead further strengthen (NITDB); and other issues, they expressed their views: Hon. Lalita Kumari, Hon. Sujata Tamang, Hon. Sunil K.C., Hon. Laxmi Prasad Pokharel, Hon. Lima Adhikari (Acharya), Hon. Sumina Udhas, Hon. Suresh Kumar Chaudhary, Hon. Vikram Thapa, Hon. Vipin Kumar Acharya, Hon. Sulabh Kharel, Hon. Bishnumaya BK, Hon. Sushant Vaidya, Hon. Shahjahan Khatun, Hon. Sushil Kumar Kanu, Hon. Shiva Shankar Yadav, Hon. Sushil Khadka, Hon. Sushila Dhakal (Acharya), Hon. Shobha Khanal, Hon. Shraddha Kunwar Chhetri, Hon. Sushma Swarnakar (Dimple), Hon. Shridhar Pokhel, Hon. Surya Bahadur Tamang, Hon. Shriram Neupane, Hon. Srishti Bhattarai, Hon. Som Sharma, Hon. Smriti Century, Hon. Harina Devi Kami, Hon. Hari Mohan Bhandari, Hon. Kshitij Thebe, Hon. Gyanu Poudyal, Hon. Janendra Singh Mahata, Hon. Lal Bikram Thapa, Hon. Sarita Lama, Hon. Sarita Mahato, Hon. Sagar Bhusal, Hon. Sajida Khatun Siddiki, Hon. Sirjana Shrestha, Hon. Sita Thapaliya.
Thereafter, during the Zero Hour that commenced, the following Honourable Members expressed their views on contemporary issues: Hon. Lalita Kumari, Hon. Sujata Tamang, Hon. Sunil K.C., Hon. Laxmi Prasad Pokharel, Hon. Lima Adhikari (Acharya), Hon. Sumina Udhas, Hon. Suresh Kumar Chaudhary, Hon. Vikram Thapa, Hon. Vipin Kumar Acharya, Hon. Sulabh Kharel, Hon. Bishnumaya BK, Hon. Sushant Vaidya, Hon. Shahjahan Khatun, Hon. Sushil Kumar Kanu, Hon. Shiva Shankar Yadav, Hon. Sushil Khadka, Hon. Sushila Dhakal (Acharya), Hon. Shobha Khanal, Hon. Shraddha Kunwar Chhetri, Hon. Sushma Swarnakar (Dimple), Hon. Shridhar Pokhel, Hon. Surya Bahadur Tamang, Hon. Shriram Neupane, Hon. Srishti Bhattarai, Hon. Som Sharma, Hon. Smriti Century, Hon. Harina Devi Kami, Hon. Hari Mohan Bhandari, Hon. Kshitij Thebe, Hon. Gyanu Poudyal, Hon. Janendra Singh Mahata, Hon. Lal Bikram Thapa, Hon. Sarita Lama, Hon. Sarita Mahato, Hon. Sagar Bhusal, Hon. Sajida Khatun Siddiki, Hon. Sirjana Shrestha, Hon. Sita Thapaliya.
Thereafter, the Honourable Minister of Health and Population, Nisha Mehta, responded to the questions raised during the Zero Hour and Special Time, stating that the construction process for the proposed Trauma Center in Dhalkebar will not be halted, and a decision has been made to operate a trauma unit at the Lahan Provincial Hospital to provide immediate treatment services until the new structure is built, and that the government is advancing both long-term infrastructure development and immediate service expansion to end the compulsion for accident victims to travel to Kathmandu, and informed the House about other related matters.
Subsequently, the Honourable Leader of the Rastriya Prajatantra Party Parliamentary Party, Hon. Gyan Bahadur Shahi, expressed his views on contemporary issues.
Thereafter, under the Question and Answer session related to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs as per Chapter 8 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honourable Minister of Foreign Affairs, Shishir Khanal, responded to questions (Questions No. 1 and 2) asked by Hon. Nishchal Rai, and also responded to supplementary questions related to Question No. 1.
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister of Youth, Employment and Labour, Ramji Yadav, responded to the question (Question No. 3) asked by Hon. Nishchal Rai.
Thereafter, the Honourable Minister of Finance, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, presented a proposal seeking permission to present the "Employees Provident Fund (Tenth Amendment) Bill, 2083". As no objection notice was received from any Honourable Member as per Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honourable Minister of Finance, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, presented the "Employees Provident Fund (Tenth Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented a proposal seeking permission to present the "Mediation (Second Amendment) Bill, 2083". As no objection notice was received from any Honourable Member as per Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the "Mediation (Second Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, the Honourable Minister of Youth, Employment and Labour, Ramji Yadav, presented a proposal seeking permission to present the "Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Bill, 2083". As no objection notice was received from any Honourable Member as per Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honourable Minister of Youth, Employment and Labour, Ramji Yadav, presented the "Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the proposal "Consideration of the Constitutional Bodies' Remuneration, Service Conditions, and Facilities (First Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, responded to the questions raised by the following Honourable Members during the discussion on the bill: Hon. Pramila Kumari Gachchhadar, Hon. Rina Upreti, Hon. Lima Adhikari Acharya, Hon. Krishna Hari Budhathoki, Hon. Ganga Laxmi Awal, Hon. Janak Singh Dhami, Hon. Kali Bahadur Shrestha, Hon. Madhav Bahadur Thapa, Hon. Kamal Subedi, Hon. Parashmani Gelal, Hon. Parvati BK, Hon. Nirasha Chaudhary Dagaura, Hon. Yeshuda Kumari Baral, Hon. Sajida Khatun Siddiki.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal "Consideration of the Constitutional Bodies' Remuneration, Service Conditions, and Facilities (First Amendment) Bill, 2083" for decision, and it was approved by a majority.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to submit amendment notices to the "Constitutional Bodies' Remuneration, Service Conditions, and Facilities (First Amendment) Bill, 2083" as per Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, can submit their notices to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singhdarbar.
Subsequently, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the proposal "Consideration of the Bill to Revive the Previous Provisions of the Nepal Special Service Act, 2042, along with the Report of the State Affairs and Good Governance Committee, 2083". The Right Honourable Speaker presented this proposal for decision, and it was approved by a majority.
Thereafter, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, responded to the questions raised by the following Honourable Members during the discussion: Hon. Srishti Bhattarai, Hon. Ganesh Bahadur Bishwakarma, Hon. Deepak Kumar Shah (Sunsari), Hon. Shahjahan Khatun, Hon. Yeshuda Kumari Baral, Hon. Sajida Khatun Siddiki.
Subsequently, as there were no amendment proposals to the report of the State Affairs and Good Governance Committee regarding the "Bill to Revive the Previous Provisions of the Nepal Special Service Act, 2042, 2083", the Right Honourable Speaker presented Clause 2 "Bill to Revive the Previous Provisions of the Nepal Special Service Act, 2042, 2083" for decision, and it was approved by a majority.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker read out the preamble and title of the bill and presented the preamble and title "Bill to Revive the Previous Provisions of the Nepal Special Service Act, 2042, 2083" for decision, and it was approved by a majority.
Subsequently, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the proposal "The Bill to Revive the Previous Provisions of the Nepal Special Service Act, 2042, 2083" for passage. The Right Honourable Speaker presented this proposal for decision, and it was passed by a majority.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled to be held on Tuesday, Shrawan 26, 2083, at 1:00 PM, and the House was adjourned at 4:04 PM.
Padma Prasad Pandey
