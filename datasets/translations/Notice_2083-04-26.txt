(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Tuesday, Shrawan 26, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:15 PM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, when Honorable Members from Nepali Congress, Nepal Communist Party (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Labor Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and sought time to speak, the Honorable Speaker granted time to the following Honorable Members, who then expressed their objections to the government and the Prime Minister's working style, drew the government's attention to issues of border disputes, administrative delays, and corruption. The Honorable Prime Minister was urged to come to the House in national attire, to provide a response in the House regarding national problems and border encroachment, and to remove the Prime Minister's statement from the parliamentary record. They also spoke on issues such as the need to enact laws to provide strict punishment to criminals for the end of widespread irregularities, corruption, impunity, and injustice in the country, and the government and ruling coalition parties' inability to be responsible for national sovereignty, a clear foreign policy, and the resolution of citizens' basic problems.

She also expressed condolences on the passing of Radhika Gotame, Prashant Adhikari, and Dr. Nutan Sharma. She also expressed condolences on the passing of the late litterateur, poet, and novelist Rajendra Bimal, and extended sympathy to the bereaved family.

Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker presented the proposal "Mining and Minerals Bill, 2083" for decision. Honorable Nishkal Rai raised a point of order regarding the lack of quorum in the House. The Honorable Speaker, in accordance with Article 94 of the Constitution of Nepal, stated that one-fourth of the members of the House of Representatives must be present to present any question or proposal for decision, and directed the Secretariat to ascertain the quorum. After the ringing of the notice bell for 3 minutes and counting the number of present Honorable Members, it was found that the required quorum was 69, but only 61 Honorable Members were present. Therefore, the decision on the proposal to consider the bill will be made in the next meeting.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives will be held on Monday, Bhadra 1, 2083, at 1:00 PM, and the House adjourned at 5:05 PM.
