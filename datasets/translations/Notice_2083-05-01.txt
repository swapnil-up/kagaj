(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Monday, Bhadra 1, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 1:25 PM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, Honorable Members from Nepali Congress, Nepal Communist Party (UML), Nepal Communist Party, Labor Culture Party, and Rastriya Prajatantra Party stood in front of their seats and requested time to speak. The Honorable Speaker, noting the continuous protests by Honorable Members for a long time, demanding question-and-answer sessions with the Prime Minister or related to the Prime Minister's work as per Rule 56 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, directed the Honorable Prime Minister to respond to the questions raised by the MPs as per Rules 56, 47, 58, 59, and 60 of the Regulations.
Subsequently, during the impromptu session that began, the following Honorable Members drew the government's attention to issues such as: 18 people died in the landslide in Rolpa on July 27, 28, and 30; significant damage to structures including roads and bridges due to floods, landslides, and continuous rainfall in various parts of the country including Jajarkot; problems with transportation; disruption of students' education; the need for proper relief and compensation for the families of those who lost their lives in the disaster, and free treatment for the injured; the government's need to prepare for disasters; the distribution of land ownership certificates to 429 landless residents in Madhyapur Thimi-8 on July 28, during which Hiralal Paswan died at the Sukumbasi Holding Center in Bode, highlighting the need to focus on management; the problem of landless people, which has been unresolved for decades, needing to be solved; two people died at the scene and three were taken to the hospital for treatment following a Bolero jeep accident this morning in Syaulee Thali, Khandikhola Rural Municipality-1, and the injured need free treatment; the government's discriminatory behavior in various communal incidents, leading to a loss of public trust in the state; the significant increase in snakebite incidents during the monsoon season, necessitating the availability of antivenom in all hospitals; paying tribute to the late eminent litterateur and poet Durgalal Shrestha; the need for an impartial investigation into the discovery of the body of Anish Dev Shahi, the youngest son of Padambahadur Shahi of Kamal Bazar Municipality-6, Achham, found unclaimed on the bank of the Tinau River; the right to write and speak for all citizens in a democratic system, and the government's move towards authoritarianism; the need for the government to provide free treatment to all citizens; the existence of the Susta region being threatened by the erosion of the Narayani River, requiring diplomatic dialogue with India to solve the Susta problem and construct embankments in the border area; the serious situation where ministers are rescued by helicopter when ill, but the public cannot even get a stretcher in hospitals, necessitating equal treatment for all citizens by the government; paying tribute to the four youths who died in the religious and communal incidents in Sunsari and Siraha, ensuring free treatment for the injured, and the government's need to be vigilant in controlling epidemics in the Madhesh region; the tragic death of Anita Panth of Belauri Municipality-6 on July 28, due to electrocution from an unsafe electric wire while cutting grass; the need for regular monitoring and maintenance of electricity infrastructure required by farmers for irrigation; the need for government facilitation to bring back the body of Ramesh Bohora of Salyan, who died in a traffic accident in Malaysia on July 29 while working abroad for foreign employment; the government's responsible action in the earthquake-affected areas of Rukum, Jajarkot, and Salyan; the inability to implement the budget for corpse management due to the ministry not formulating a work procedure, in the case of Santosh Rai of Dhading; and other issues.

Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker presented the proposal "Consider the Human Trafficking and Transportation (Control) (First Amendment) Bill, 2083" for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker informed that Honorable Members wishing to give notice of amendment to the "Human Trafficking and Transportation (Control) (First Amendment) Bill, 2083" as per Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, can submit their notice to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singha Durbar.
After that, the Honorable Speaker informed that the program related to direct question-and-answer with the Prime Minister as per Chapter-9 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, has been scheduled for the meeting on Bhadra 10, 2083, and informed that for the direct question-and-answer with the Prime Minister, each party represented in the House of Representatives will have one question from at least one Honorable Member, and the remaining questions, up to a maximum of 15, will be allocated based on the representation in the House.
Similarly, the Honorable Speaker drew the attention of the Honorable Members that those who wish to submit questions for the direct question-and-answer with the Prime Minister as per Rule 56 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, should submit their questions to the Secretary of the House of Representatives by 1:00 PM on Friday, Bhadra 5, 2083, fulfilling the conditions related to questions as per Rule 49 of the same Regulations, and also urged the Honorable Members to ask concise and relevant questions rather than long and pointless ones.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled for Tuesday, Bhadra 2, 2083, at 11:00 AM, and the meeting was adjourned at 6:15 PM.
