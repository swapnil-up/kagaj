(Brief details of the meeting proceedings)
Tuesday, Bhadra 2, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, during the impromptu session, the following Honourable Members expressed their views on contemporary issues including the need for immediate operation of the Jajarkot-Dolpa road under the Bheri Corridor, which is the lifeline of the Dolpa residents, as it has been completely obstructed due to the flood on Shrawan 20; the need to identify the causes of road accidents and take necessary steps to reduce them; the need for timely repair of the electricity and communication services disrupted in the remote areas of Sankhuwasabha and Udaypur due to damage to the transmission lines and towers caused by continuous rainfall in the Bhotkhola Rural Municipality of Sankhuwasabha; the need for the concerned bodies to take timely initiatives to control elephant menace as the destructive activities of wild elephants that entered Nepal from India with a large herd have caused significant damage to the crops and physical structures of farmers in Jhapa; and the need for the government to remove the VAT imposed on electricity consumption and focus on increasing electricity consumption, as electricity is an alternative to traditional energy sources like gas, firewood, and dung cakes:-
Hon. Purna Prasad Limbu

Meanwhile, after Hon. Tika Sangraula finished speaking, Hon. Sajida Khatun Siddiki raised a point of order, stating that the opposition would never support the government's wrongdoings and that the prevailing lawlessness in the country was happening under the government's protection, and demanded that the government respond regarding the police firing in Siraha. Immediately thereafter, Hon. Tika Sangraula, raising a point of order and drawing attention to Rule 21(b) of the Regulations, requested that the word "your police" used by Hon. Sajida Khatun Siddiki during her speech be expunged from the parliamentary record. Similarly, Hon. Ringala Yadav objected to the use of the words "goons and anarchists" for the previous government and stated that the current government is perpetuating lawlessness in the country.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed the House about a minor amendment made to the agenda for today's proceedings.
Subsequently, under the Question and Answer program as per Chapter-8 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, in the Q&A program related to the Ministry of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation, Hon. Minister of Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation, Biraj Bhakta Shrestha, responded to the question asked by Hon. Basana Thapa (Question No. 6).
Meanwhile, when Hon. Harka Raj Rai questioned why a written question was asked when it was meant to be oral, the Right Honourable Speaker drew attention to the provision in Chapter-8 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, that questions asked orally by Honourable Members would be answered orally.
After that, the Honourable Minister of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation, Khadak Raj Poudel, answered the questions asked by Hon. Nishkal Rai (Questions No. 7 and 8) and also provided supplementary answers.
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister of Agriculture, Forest and Environment, Gita Chaudhary, answered the questions asked by Hon. Nishkal Rai (Questions No. 9 and 10) and also provided supplementary answers.
Thereafter, the Honourable Minister of Foreign Affairs, Shishir Khanal, answered the questions asked by Hon. Nishkal Rai (Questions No. 4 and 5).
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister of Foreign Affairs, Shishir Khanal, responding to questions raised during the impromptu, zero, and special hours, informed the House about issues including the construction of own buildings for Nepali missions abroad, management of local staff, rescue of Nepalis in distress, advancement of the process for constructing embassy buildings in Canberra, Australia, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and Bangkok, Thailand, the rescue of only 860 Nepalis from Cambodia, and the provision of a fund of 15 crore rupees for the rescue of those who went abroad illegally and are in trouble, and for bringing their bodies back to Nepal.
Thereafter, Hon. Asika Tamang sought time to ask a question, expressing dissatisfaction with the minister's response, but the Right Honourable Speaker informed that ministers would not be present to answer questions raised during impromptu, zero, and special hours.
Subsequently, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, Pushpa Kamal Dahal 'Prachanda', the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the "Seventh Annual Report of the Tharu Commission (Shrawan 2081 - Ashadh 2082)" to the House.
Thereafter, the Honourable Minister of Foreign Affairs, Shishir Khanal, presented the proposal "Consider the Passport (First Amendment) Bill, 2083".

Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker put the proposal "Consider the Passport (First Amendment) Bill, 2083" to a decision, and it was approved by a majority.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to give notice of amendments to the "Passport (First Amendment) Bill, 2083" as per Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, could submit their notice to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singh Durbar.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker informed the House that the programs listed as items 4 and 5 of today's agenda have been removed from the list.
Thereafter, the meeting was adjourned at 4:14 PM, with the next meeting of the House of Representatives scheduled to be held on Wednesday, Bhadra 3, 2083, at 1:00 PM.
