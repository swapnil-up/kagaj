(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Wednesday, Bhadra 3, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 2:55 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, taking impromptu time, the following Honourable Members raised concerns regarding the escalating problem of drug abuse in Kailali and urged the government to increase its activeness in controlling the smuggling and use of drugs; the serious negligence and incompetence of the police being responsible for the murder of 3-year-old Garima Chaudhary of Jitpur Simara Sub-Metropolis-1, Bara, and urged for a thorough investigation into the incident, action against the culprits, and reform in police procedures, drawing the attention of the Home Minister; numerous citizens being injured due to attacks by wild animals in Achham, and the government needing to be serious about controlling the damage to crops and livestock caused by wild boars; the government's neglect in the rescue and relief efforts for the flood victims of Jajarkot, with 824 people kept in a holding center, and the injured, children, lactating mothers, and pregnant women having to stay under plastic tarps, drawing the government's attention; the building of Adarsha Secondary School in Aathbiskot Municipality-14, Rukum Paschim, being completely destroyed due to continuous rain and landslides, and the immediate need for arrangements for the school building and educational materials to resume teaching and learning; two girls drowning and one still missing while trying to cross the Basudev River in Hetauda Sub-Metropolis-19 on their way home from school yesterday evening, and the need for an intensified search for the missing person; the sad demise of Bikram Bahadur Kayastha, a social worker, educationist, and fighter for democracy, aged 98, residing in Khau Tole, Bhaktapur Municipality-2, Bhaktapur, and expressing heartfelt condolences to the deceased; the health post in Letang Municipality, Morang, issuing a letter yesterday stating the inability to manage the salary of the Lab Assistant, leading to the closure of lab services, and urging the local government to take initiative for the operation of health posts; protesting the incident of secretly filming and making public a video of a police officer and a young woman, stating that secretly filming and making public a video without consent is a violation of the right to privacy and a punishable offense; expressing heartfelt condolences on the tragic death of Gopal Basnet from Sotang Rural Municipality-4, Solukhumbu, who went for foreign employment and passed away in Korea on Bhadra 30, and urging the government to facilitate bringing his body back to Nepal, among other contemporary issues, Honourable Members expressed their views.

Meanwhile, after Hon. Sajida Khatun Siddiki finished speaking, Hon. Ashika Tamang raised a point of order, stating that the Honourable Member referred to the ruling party members as "coming from a circus," and objected, stating that they were elected by the invaluable votes of the people, and demanded that the word be removed from the parliamentary record as it was unparliamentary and insulting. Similarly, Hon. Vishnu Maya B.K. raised a point of order, drawing attention to the fact that when Honourable Members of the opposition raise the voice of the people in the house, Honourable Members of the ruling party stand up and raise points of order.
Subsequently, the Honourable Ram Bahadur Thapa, leader of the CPN (UML) parliamentary party, expressed his views on contemporary issues.
Meanwhile, after Hon. Ram Bahadur Thapa finished speaking, Hon. Samiksha Baskota raised a point of order, stating that the words used by the Honourable Member during his speech, such as "Valendra" and others, were insulting and objectionable, drawing attention to Rule 21 (d) of the Regulations, and demanded that the words be removed from the parliamentary record.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed the house that agenda item number 1 of today's agenda had been removed.

Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal "Consideration of the Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Bill, 2083" for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed the Honourable Members wishing to give notice of amendments to the "Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Bill, 2083" in accordance with Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, that they could submit their notices to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singh Durbar.
Subsequently, the Honourable Home Minister, Sudhan Gurung, responding to the questions raised during the impromptu, zero, and special times of the session, informed the house that the government is sensitive to resolving the legal and technical complexities seen in the construction of private housing for the earthquake victims of Jajarkot and Rukum Paschim, that a task force has been formed under the chairmanship of the Executive Chief of the Disaster Management Authority, and informed the house about the ongoing work including the requirement for local governments to comply with the National Building Code as per the Local Government Operation Act, 2074, for grants.
Thereafter, the House of Representatives was adjourned at 6:35 PM, with the next meeting scheduled for Monday, Bhadra 8, 2083, at 1:00 PM.
