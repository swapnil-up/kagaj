(Brief Description of Meeting Proceedings)
Monday, Bhadra 8, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 1:15 PM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, taking impromptu time, the following Honorable Members drew the government's attention to contemporary issues, including: the death of Sinkki Bohara of Ward-6, Mattagaupalika, in Kailali on Bhadra 5, when a passenger bus hit and ran her over, demanding legal action against the culprits and appropriate compensation for the victim; the death of a young man in a hotel fire in Shankhamul, Kathmandu, and drawing the government's attention to the family's situation, urging the government to arrange for the security of those living in Kathmandu's hotels; the government's agreement and compensation style in cases of girl child rape and murder not being good, demanding the implementation of life imprisonment for proven rape and murder after rape, and the establishment of fast-track courts for the speedy resolution of rape and sexual violence cases; arranging for the repatriation of the bodies of Deepak Bohara of Achham, who tragically died in a road accident in Malaysia, and Laxmi Rajwanshi of Jhapa, who went to Japan for studies, to Nepal; not keeping pickpockets and rapists in the same prison; demanding the strictest legal action against the culprits in the rape and murder of 3-year-old Garima Chaudhary of Jitpur Simara Sub-Metropolitan City-1, Bara, and an end to the media trial in the name of investigation into the incident; the Prime Minister's personal visits highlighting public despair and state indifference amidst rising incidents of rape and murder across the country; urging the government to focus on relief, compensation, and resettlement for the significant loss of life and property due to floods and landslides in Jajarkot and Humla; the kidnapping of 16-year-old Rishav Gupta by a group of 5 Indian citizens from Narayangadh on Asar 1, demanding ransom of 2.2 million from the family, and demanding the arrest and legal action against the perpetrators of the attempted rape of a 14-year-old girl at Mission Success Pvt. Ltd. resort in Bharatpur on Saun 31; the commencement of the removal of structures built by encroaching on public land and ponds in Janakpurdham, demanding an end to the practice of only demolishing the settlements of the poor while leaving the lands of the wealthy untouched, and urging equal treatment for all; allocating necessary budget to Khaskhad Engineering Campus; urging immediate search for Rebika Bishwakarma of Haldibari-2, Jhapa, who has been missing for the past two days; immediate search and rescue of Anu Shahi and Prashant Rawal, who were swept away and disappeared by the Karnali River in Humla; urging the government to prioritize Nepali products due to foreign apples not finding a market because of their uncontrolled import; the government not paying attention to the management of those displaced by Koshi river erosion; urging the government to enact strict laws and regulate the increasing risk of infections like HIV and Hepatitis; drawing attention to the plight of 40 youths from Bardiya who went to work for a Chinese noodle company in Shahid Lakhan Rural Municipality, Gorkha district, and have been stranded for 3 days without wages and food, and were subjected to beating when demanding wages; and arranging for the dialysis machine purchased two years ago at the Provincial Hospital in Malangawa, Sarlahi, to be put into operation with necessary doctors and health workers to provide services, among other contemporary issues.

Meanwhile, after Honorable Harkaraj Rai finished speaking, Honorable Khushbu Oli, citing the rules, demanded that the words "miracle when minds meet, rape when they don't" used by the previous speaker on the sensitive issue of rape be removed from the records of the House as they were objectionable.
Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker directed the Secretariat to remove the inappropriate and unnecessary words 'Bhalu', 'Veshya', and 'Cyber Sena' used by Honorable Member Indira Rana Magar during her speech on the meeting of Bhadra 1, 2083, and the words 'Bali of citizens', 'Pakistani model', 'North Korean model', and 'America' used by Honorable Member Ram Bahadur Thapa during his speech on the meeting of Bhadra 3, 2083, from the records. The Speaker also drew attention to the fact that Honorable Members should not unnecessarily mention the governance practices of neighboring countries and friendly nations in a way that could be misinterpreted in the context of our governance practices in the dignified House.
After that, under the question-and-answer program related to the Ministry of Industry, Commerce and Supplies, in accordance with Chapter-8 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honorable Minister of Industry, Commerce and Supplies, Gauri Kumari, responded to the question asked by Honorable Harkaraj Rai (Question No. 11).
Subsequently, on behalf of the Honorable Prime Minister, the Honorable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle proposed that "the Bill to Amend the Remuneration, Service Conditions, and Facilities of Office Bearers of Constitutional Bodies (First Amendment) Bill, 2083, be sent to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion." The Honorable Speaker presented this proposal for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the Honorable Minister of Foreign Affairs, Shishir Khanal, proposed that "the Passport (First Amendment) Bill, 2083, be sent to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion." The Honorable Speaker presented this proposal for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Honorable Minister of Women, Children, and Senior Citizens and Social Security, Sita Kumari, proposed that "the Human Trafficking and Transportation (Control) (First Amendment) Bill, 2083, be sent to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion." The Honorable Speaker presented this proposal for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
After that, the Honorable Minister of Industry, Commerce and Supplies, Gauri Kumari, proposed that "the Mines and Minerals Bill, 2083, be sent to the relevant committee for clause-by-clause discussion." The Honorable Speaker presented this proposal for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled to be held on Tuesday, Bhadra 9, 2083, at 1:00 PM, and the meeting was adjourned at 5:05 PM.
