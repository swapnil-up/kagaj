Information Bulletin - 2
(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Wednesday, Bhadra 10, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 2:00 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, the following Honourable Members took urgent time and drew the government's attention to the severe flood in the Bhote Koshi (Trishuli) river in Rasuwa, which has caused significant human and physical damage, urging the declaration of a national disaster and the affected areas as disaster-stricken zones, and to immediately engage in rescue and relief efforts, and to establish a disaster early warning system, response, and preparedness. They also stated that the government should prioritize rescue and relief in this hour of disaster; citizens at risk in the flood-affected areas of Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha, Chitwan, and Nawalparasi West should be relocated to safe places, and essential humanitarian aid should be immediately provided to the affected areas with full capacity, including helicopter teams; missing citizens and security personnel should be immediately searched for and rescued; free food, temporary shelter, and immediate free treatment for the injured should be arranged for the displaced victims; the government should immediately provide relief, appropriate compensation, and resettlement for the citizens displaced by the flood and landslides in Jajarkot, who have not yet received any relief; although Nepal's contribution to climate change is negligible, it is bearing the brunt of its effects, therefore emphasis should be placed on disaster management and risk reduction, including monitoring of glacial lakes, development of an early warning system, construction of climate-resilient infrastructure, and effective climate diplomacy; and requested everyone to refrain from spreading unverified and misleading news on social media during times of disaster. They also highlighted the need for government and private hospitals and health institutions to keep skilled manpower, health equipment, and ambulances ready. Honourable Members Arjun Narsingh KC, Yuvraj Dulal, Khushbu Oli, Mohan Acharya, Amresh Kumar Singh, Basu Maya Tamang, Pushpa Raj Kandel, Ramesh Kumar Malla, Saraswati Lama, Samiksha Baskota, Tapeshwor Yadav, Sita Thapaliya, Ganga Devi Shrestha, Parvati BK, Kabindra Burla Koti, Smriti Century, Prakash Singh Karki, Harkaraj Rai, Toshima Karki, Basana Thapa, Ain Bahadur Mahar, and Kripa Ram Rana spoke on these contemporary issues and drew the government's attention.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that there was no Zero Hour or Special Time scheduled for today.
Subsequently, Honourable Rabi Lamichhane, on behalf of the Rastriya Swatantra Party Parliamentary Party, presented his views on the flood in the Bhote Koshi (Trishuli) river basin in Rasuwa and the disaster it caused, including the roles of civil society, political parties, and the government.
Following that, Honourable Minister Shishir Khanal informed the House about the flood that occurred in Rasuwa at approximately 8:40 AM today, the resulting situation, and the government's ongoing efforts in relief, search, and rescue.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker expressed deep sorrow over the loss of life and property due to the devastating flood in the Bhote Koshi river in Rasuwa this morning, drew the attention of the Government of Nepal to coordinate necessary efforts for the rescue and treatment of the injured, search for the missing, and relief for the victims, displaced, and affected in this hour of disaster, and heartily urged all private sectors, non-governmental organizations, and civil society to provide necessary support and coordination in this endeavor.
Stating that all state mechanisms need to be mobilized for rescue, relief, and assistance to the affected citizens in this hour of grief, he informed that all the programs scheduled for today's agenda have been removed from the list.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives will be held on Thursday, Bhadra 11, 2083, at 1:00 PM. The meeting was adjourned at 3:55 PM.
