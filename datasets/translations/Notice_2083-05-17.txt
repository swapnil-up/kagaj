(Concise Details of Meeting Proceedings)
Wednesday, Bhadra 17, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 1:15 PM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, the Honorable Prime Minister Balendra Shah informed the House about the government's ongoing rescue, relief, and rehabilitation efforts following the flood in the BhoteKoshi-Trishuli rivers on Bhadra 10, 2083. He stated that climate change is the primary cause of the BhoteKoshi disaster, and its mitigation and management is a shared responsibility of the global community. He mentioned that the government is making every effort to save its citizens after the disaster, and highlighted the impact on the tunnels of hydropower projects. He explained that rescue efforts are facing difficulties and delays due to differences compared to various international experiences, and emphasized that the army, armed police, and Nepal police deployed for rescue should be encouraged.
Following this, the following Honorable Members expressed their views on the flood in the BhoteKoshi-Trishuli rivers and related issues from their party's perspective:-
1. Hon. Ganesh Parajuli
2. Hon. Bhimraj Adhikari
3. Hon. Ram Bahadur Thapa
4. Hon. Pushpa Kamal Dahal
5. Hon. Dhuvraj Rai
6. Hon. Gyan Bahadur Shahi
Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker expressed sorrow over the loss of life and property due to the flood in the BhoteKoshi-Trishuli rivers, directed the government to expedite rescue and relief efforts, and shared his views on the work to be done by domestic and foreign organizations and civil society. The following special motion regarding the loss of life and property due to the flood in the BhoteKoshi-Trishuli rivers was presented to the House and unanimously passed:-
Special Motion
"This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses deep sorrow over the incident on Wednesday, Bhadra 10, 2083, where the flood in the BhoteKoshi-Trishuli rivers caused many citizens, both Nepali and foreign, to go missing and a large number of deaths in areas including Timure, Syafrubesi, Betrawati in Rasuwa, Trishuli Bazaar and Devighat in Nuwakot, and along the Trishuli river banks in Dhading, Chitwan, and Gorkha districts, resulting in extensive loss of life and property. Furthermore, it wishes for the management of those who have been displaced by the incident, the speedy recovery of the injured, and prays for the eternal peace of the souls of the deceased, expressing deep condolences to the bereaved families."
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives will be held on Thursday, Bhadra 18, 2083, at 1:00 PM. The meeting was adjourned at 3:20 PM.
