(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Thursday, Bhadra 18, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:44 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, taking impromptu time, the following Honourable Members drew the government's attention to the severe floods in the Bhote Koshi (Trishuli) River in Rasuwa, which caused significant human and physical damage, urging the declaration of the flood-affected areas as disaster zones, immediate rescue and relief efforts, and the establishment of an early warning system, response, and preparedness for disasters. They also emphasized that the government should prioritize rescue and relief, immediately relocate citizens at risk in flood-affected areas of Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha, and Chitwan to safe locations, and provide necessary humanitarian assistance. They also called for vigilance against potential black marketeering, full deployment of helicopter teams to promptly search and rescue missing citizens and security personnel, and arrangements for free food, temporary shelter for those who lost their homes, and free treatment for the injured. Concerns were raised about the government's delay in seeking foreign assistance for the rescue of those trapped in landslides, the public suffering from hunger due to the government's centralized relief distribution system, the need for the government to expedite relief, appropriate compensation, and resettlement for citizens displaced by floods and landslides in Jajarkot. Despite Nepal's negligible contribution to climate change, it is disproportionately affected, necessitating a focus on disaster management and risk reduction through monitoring glacial lakes, developing early warning systems, constructing climate-resilient infrastructure, and engaging in effective climate diplomacy. Attention was drawn to the need to prevent the dissemination of unverified and misleading news on social media during times of disaster. They also highlighted the importance of keeping skilled personnel, health equipment, and ambulances in government and private hospitals and health institutions ready. The Honourable Members expressed their views on these contemporary issues and drew the government's attention.

After Hon. Ganga Devi Shrestha finished speaking, Hon. Rata Kumari Thapa took impromptu time to express wishes for a speedy recovery for Ambika Saud, a resident of Ramaroshan Rural Municipality-6, Achham, who was seriously injured in a bear attack today, and called for her free treatment and for the relevant bodies to control the bear menace.
Similarly, after Hon. Dharmaraj KC finished speaking, Hon. Sajida Khatun Siddiki took impromptu time to offer condolences to those who died in the Bhote Koshi flood in Rasuwa, urged arrangements for free treatment for the injured, and called for intensifying the search for those missing in the flood.
Thereafter, the proposal by the Honourable Minister for Youth, Labour and Employment, Ramji Yadav, to "Send the Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Bill, 2083 for clause-wise discussion to the relevant committee" was presented by the Right Honourable Speaker for decision and was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister for Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation, Biraj Bhakta Shrestha, presented the "Agreement between Nepal and India on Long-Term Electricity Trade".
Thereafter, the Honourable Minister for Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the "Convention on the Conservation and Sustainable Use of Marine Biological Diversity of Areas Beyond National Jurisdiction under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea".
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 5:25 PM, to be held on Wednesday, Bhadra 24, 2083, at 1:00 PM.
