(Brief Minutes of the Meeting)
Wednesday, Bhadra 24, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:04 PM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, taking impromptu time, the following Honorable Members raised their voices and drew the government's attention to the following contemporary issues: the government must implement the agreements made with the families of the deceased and injured in the Janjati movement and immediately release the Janjati fighters in detention; rescue efforts have not gained momentum even two weeks after the flood in Rasuwa, so the government must immediately manage rescue, relief, safe housing, and resettlement; the government must be sensitive to the security risks faced by women and children in the holding center where flood victims are kept; a disaster early warning system must be established in the Chameliya and Mahakali riverine areas of Dadeldhura, and a retaining wall must be constructed immediately in the Gokulather market located on the banks of the Chameliya River; although the Janjati movement on Bhadra 23 was genuinely by Janjatis, the movement on Bhadra 24 was hijacked, and structures including Singha Durbar were destroyed; the government must take the incident of attempted self-immolation at Maitighar yesterday seriously; the government has not taken concrete steps to restore road connectivity in Dhading, which has been cut off for 15 days; low-level comments on social media during times of disaster must be controlled; the government must pay attention to the severe shortage of gas in the market and citizens having to wait in line for hours; the search for Dhan Bahadur Mall and Himmat Mall from Mugu, who went missing in the flood, must be intensified; the government must be timely aware of the potential risks posed by four glacial lakes, including the Lower Barun glacial lake, in the Bhotkhola and Makalu regions of Sankhuwasbhsa, and the potential damage from glacial lake outburst floods; and the government must be aware of the potential damage from glacial lake outburst floods.

Thereafter, Honorable Rabi Lamichhane, on behalf of the Rastriya Swatantra Party Parliamentary Party, expressed his views on issues such as the flood in Bhotekoshi, Rasuwa, being not just a natural disaster but a consequence of climate crisis, for which Nepal must demand climate justice in the international arena, and the government must be serious about the relief, rescue, and resettlement of flood and landslide victims.
After that, the Honorable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the 'Nepal Law Commission (First Amendment) Bill, 2083'.
Subsequently, as no notice of objection from any Honorable Member was received as per Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, regarding the proposal seeking permission to introduce the aforementioned bill, the Honorable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the 'Nepal Law Commission (First Amendment) Bill, 2083'.
Thereafter, the Honorable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented a proposal for consideration on the 'Mediation (Second Amendment) Bill, 2083'.

Thereafter, the Honorable Speaker presented the proposal 'Let the Mediation (Second Amendment) Bill, 2083 be considered' for decision, and it was approved by a majority.
Subsequently, the Honorable Speaker informed that Honorable Members wishing to give notice of amendments to the 'Mediation (Second Amendment) Bill, 2083' as per Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, can submit their notices to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singh Durbar.
After that, the Honorable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the 'Proposal Regarding Membership in the Council of Asian Legislative Institutions (CALI)' to the House.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 5:34 PM, scheduled to convene on Thursday, Bhadra 25, 2083, at 1:00 PM.
