Thursday, Bhadra 25, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:30 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, taking urgent time, the following Honourable Members drew the government's attention to various contemporary issues, including: the incident where a pregnant woman had to give birth outside the hospital on the street due to lack of money as she could not reach Gajendra Narayan Singh Hospital, calling it extreme negligence and demanding an investigation and action against the guilty; offering condolences to the deceased in the Rasuwa flood and arranging free treatment for the injured, as well as providing official information to the families of the missing persons; making relief and rescue efforts for flood victims more effective by mobilizing additional resources and manpower; immediately managing the displaced families due to landslides caused by continuous rainfall in Narharinath Rural Municipality, Kalikot, and searching for the missing; the problem in rescue operations at Rasuwagadhi due to lack of rescue materials and equipment, urging the government to provide them immediately; the incident of self-immolation attempt by activist Laxmi Devi Basnet at Maitighar Mandala and the case of a youth attempting suicide by jumping into the Dhobi Khola because he could not cook for his wife due to gas shortage, drawing the government's attention; the need to intensify the search for 34 individuals from Kailali working at Mailung Khola Hydropower who have been missing since the flood in Bhotekoshi; the need for impartial investigation and action against those involved in the violent incident in Arghakhanchi where Raju Gharti of Palpa had his limbs broken and four others were seriously injured; the need to intensify the search for Siddhanta Kumar Mahato who was abducted in Dhanusha; providing immediate safe housing and relief to approximately 300 to 500 households displaced by the 5.3 magnitude earthquake in Mustang on Bhadra 23, and commencing the urgent reconstruction of various historical and socio-cultural significant structures; the problem of general public not being able to get gas despite regular supply from India, urging the ministry to seriously address the issue and for the government to take initiatives to remove long-term dependence on gas by introducing schemes like providing electric stoves at special discounts.

The meeting of the House of Representatives, which was adjourned to reconvene in 10 minutes, commenced today at 4:54 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the very beginning of the meeting, the Honourable Minister of Finance, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "Citizen Investment Fund (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, as no Honourable Member submitted a notice of objection to the proposal seeking permission to introduce the said bill, in accordance with Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honourable Minister of Finance, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, introduced the "Citizen Investment Fund (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister of Finance, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "Payment and Settlement (First Amendment) Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, as no Honourable Member submitted a notice of objection to the proposal seeking permission to introduce the said bill, in accordance with Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honourable Minister of Finance, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, introduced the "Payment and Settlement (First Amendment) Bill, 2083".

Thereafter, Honourable Parvati BK took urgent time and stated that the government should immediately arrange for safe passage as Rat Bahadur Damai, a resident of Nalgaad Municipality-11, was swept away and went missing by the Taksughta Khola in the Bheri Corridor in Jajarkot.
Subsequently, the proposal "Let there be general discussion on the proposal to obtain membership of the Council of Asian Legislative Institutions (CALI)" presented by the Right Honourable Speaker was put to vote and unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that as the proposal "Council of Asian Legislative Institutions (CALI) Membership Acquisition Proposal" has been proposed for Nepal Government to clarify and declare its position, Honourable Members wishing to submit a notice of amendment in accordance with Rule 227 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, can submit their notice at the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singhdarbar.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the condolence motion on the demise of Shukraraj Shastri, former member of the National Assembly, which was unanimously passed:-
Condolence Motion
"This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses sorrow at the untimely demise of Shukraraj Shastri, who was elected as a member from the Nepali Congress party in the 2054 BS National Assembly election and played a leading role in the establishment of democracy and the democratic system, at the age of 89 on Magh 19, 2082 BS, and extends heartfelt condolences to the bereaved family."
Thereafter, the assembly observed one minute of silence for the eternal peace of the soul of the late Shukraraj Shastri.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 6:24 PM to reconvene on Friday, Bhadra 26, 2083, at 1:00 PM.
