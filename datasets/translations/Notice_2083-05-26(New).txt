(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Friday, Bhadra 26, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:10 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, taking impromptu time, the following Honourable Members expressed their views on contemporary issues, drawing the government's attention to the closure of the Electronic Government Procurement system, implemented since 2070, which has completely affected the tender process, stating that the system was hacked and a new system is being introduced under the pretext that it cannot run on the old code; that the Bhote Koshi flood has caused physical damage and the flood victims are suffering severe mental stress and psychosocial problems, and the government should prioritize rescue and relief along with mental health services; raising the issue of Babita Paswan of Rajbiraj Municipality-14, Saptari, who had to give birth at the hospital gate due to lack of money, stating that the government must be responsible and accountable for safe motherhood and end the government's irresponsibility towards safe motherhood; demanding an accelerated search for citizens including Anita Rawal, Janak Budha, Kabita Budha, Riyans Budha, and Dhanraj Jaisi from Humla, who are missing in the Bhote Koshi flood; urging the government to focus on service delivery as citizens of Humla are deprived of basic services; drawing attention to the fact that for the past few days, a pungent smell like gunpowder has been emanating from underground near the Dhauabadi iron mine located in Hupsekot Rural Municipality-4, Nawalpur, and the locals are terrified due to the continuous smoke, and drawing the attention of the concerned bodies; drawing the government's attention to the incident this morning near Basant Chowk, Bharatpur Metropolitan City-9, Chitwan, where a person died from burns when an EV caught fire while running, and demanding an impartial investigation into the incident and that the government should prioritize consumer safety by conducting technical inspections of imported EV vehicles before they are registered; and expressing their views on various contemporary issues.

Meanwhile, after Hon. Amrita BK finished speaking, Hon. Rekha Kumari Yadav took impromptu time and drew attention to the issue that less than a month after a three-point agreement was reached between the victim's family and the government in the rape and murder case of 3-year-old Garima Chaudhary of Jitpur Simara Sub-Metropolitan City-1, Bara, some of the perpetrators involved in the rape have been released on bail of Rs. 20,000 and others on general bail.
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister of Health and Population, Nisha Mehta, responded to the questions raised during the impromptu, zero, and special times, stating that the government takes the incident at Rajbiraj Hospital seriously and will investigate and take action against the guilty, and informed the House that a five-member ethical committee led by Dr. Suprabha Shrestha, Registrar of the Nepal Medical Council, has been formed to investigate the incident, and that the government prioritizes safe motherhood and health services for mothers and newborns.
Following that, Honourable Barman Pun, on behalf of the CPN Parliamentary Party, expressed his views on issues such as the current government's deviation from good governance, accountability, and citizen security; the need for immediate relief and rehabilitation plans for the flood victims of Bhote Koshi-Trishuli rivers as they have not received relief; the government suppressing the demands of the youth; and the government's neglect of the scarcity of goods in the market.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker read out the following letter received from the Office of the President:
I have the honour to request that, as per the recommendation of the Right Honourable Prime Minister dated 2083/05/25, the Right Honourable President, Mr. Ramchandra Paudel, has appointed Honourable Deepak Kumar Sah, Sunsari, as a minister in the current Council of Ministers formed under the leadership of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, Mr. Balendra Shah, on Chaitra 13, 2082, and assigned him the responsibility of the Ministry of Industry, Commerce, and Supplies.
Date: 2083/05/24
