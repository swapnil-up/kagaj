(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Tuesday, Bhadra 30, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:25 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, taking impromptu time, the following Honourable Members drew the government's attention to the following issues: immediate relief for families affected by floods, reconstruction of damaged schools, health institutions, and roads; government's responsibility for the protection and education of orphaned children by resuming school operations; free treatment for the injured; prioritizing patient health, privacy, and infection control in sensitive hospital areas like ICUs; legal action against the "Mama-Bhanja" company involved in caste discrimination during a search operation; search for two citizens missing due to floods and landslides in Takshugad, Jajarkot, and reconstruction of damaged physical structures; the need for constitutional review on fundamental constitutional issues such as power distribution between federal, provincial, and local levels, issues of exclusive and shared powers, form of governance, federalism, republic, secularism, and the effectiveness of constitutional commissions, for which a discussion paper has been prepared; the death of jeep driver Sundar Khadka Magar and student Premkala Pun Magar on the spot in a jeep accident in Kotgaun, Sani-Bheri Rural Municipality-6, Rukum Paschim on Bhadra 28, with 11 injured rescued by helicopter and undergoing treatment in Surkhet and Kohalpur, necessitating identification of accident causes and construction of safe road infrastructure; immediate handover of identified bodies from the Bhote Koshi flood to families, and the state's sensitivity as reports for DNA samples given 14 days ago at the police hospital have not yet arrived; government's immediate arrangement of hospitals and rehabilitation centers for the mental health treatment and psychosocial counseling of children affected by floods; prosecution of the perpetrator in the murder of 40-year-old Unishara BK from Nawalpur by her own husband on Teej; drawing the government's attention to the damage caused to riverside settlements in Kathmandu and Bhaktapur by the flood in the Manohara River due to incessant rainfall on Bhadra 29; impartial investigation and prosecution of the perpetrator in the murder of Vivek Sardar of Balwa Municipality, Mahottari, whose body was dismembered and hidden inside a septic tank; immediate management of displaced citizens as houses have been damaged and villages are at risk due to land subsidence in Byas Rural Municipality and Naugad Rural Municipality, Darchula, caused by incessant rainfall; government's search for Jitendra Khadka of Khadsechhatra Rural Municipality-7, Bajhang, who has been missing since going to collect wild mushrooms from his home on Bhadra 23; the death of Dal Kumar Khatri's niece in Gulmi's Madane Rural Municipality-4, who was electrocuted by current applied by a neighbor to protect crops from wild boars while at her aunt's house, and the death of Kalpana Pun of Musikot Municipality-4, Musikot, who was swept away by the Nisti River while going to her maternal home, among other contemporary issues, the Honourable Members expressed their views and drew the government's attention.

Meanwhile, after Hon. Yuvraj Dulal finished speaking, Hon. Ain Bahadur Mahar raised a point of order, stating that the Home Minister, Hon. Sudhan Gurung, had left the hall between the Speaker and the Honourable Member speaking, which was a violation of Rule 20, Sub-rule 6 of the House of Representatives Regulations. He demanded that the dignity of the house be maintained, as it is also the responsibility of the ruling party and the government. The Right Honourable Speaker then drew the attention of all Honourable Members to this matter.
Subsequently, in accordance with the Question and Answer program related to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs as per Chapter 8 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honourable Minister of Foreign Affairs, Shishir Khanal, responded to the question (Question No. 12) asked by Hon. Sita Thapaliya.
Thereafter, in the Question and Answer program related to the Ministry of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Federal Affairs, the Honourable Minister of Land Management, Cooperatives, and Federal Affairs, Pratibha Rawal, responded to the question (Question No. 13) asked by Hon. Yuvraj Dulal, including supplementary questions.
Following this, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, proposed, "Let the discussion be held in the House on the declaration made by the Government of Nepal regarding its reservations concerning the convention for obtaining membership in the Council of Asian Legislative Institutions (CALI)." The Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that no amendment notices from any Honourable Member were received regarding the declaration made by the Government of Nepal concerning the convention, as per Rule 227 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083.
Thereafter, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, responded to the questions raised by Honourable Members during the discussion on the convention:-

Meanwhile, after Hon. Sajida Khatun Siddiki finished speaking, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that according to Rule 227 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, amendments can only be submitted regarding reservations made in a treaty or interpretations declared by the Government of Nepal regarding any provision of such a treaty. However, the amendment submitted by the Honourable Member regarding Article 10 went beyond the scope of reservations concerning the convention for obtaining membership in the Council of Asian Legislative Institutions. Therefore, the amendment was deemed non-compliant with the House of Representatives Regulations and was rejected.
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, proposed, "Let the convention for obtaining membership in the Council of Asian Legislative Institutions (CALI) be ratified with the declaration made by the Government of Nepal regarding its reservations." The Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal for decision, and it was unanimously passed.
Following this, the Speaker directed the Secretariat to remove the vocabulary from the House records used by Hon. Khagendra Sunar during the Zero Hour in the meeting of Bhadra 26, 2083, between the phrase "ever" and the phrase "the accusation is serious."
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 5:50 PM, scheduled to convene on Wednesday, Bhadra 31, 2083, at 1:00 PM.
