(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Wednesday, Bhadra 31, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:20 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
In the beginning, the following Honourable Members took urgent time and drew the government's attention to various contemporary issues:
Expressing that the government's silence has poured salt on the wounds of the Bhote Koshi flood victims, they demanded immediate construction of a bailey bridge and acceleration of relief efforts. They also highlighted the increasing rate of accidents involving electric vehicles and urged the government to focus on road safety and driver competency enhancement, scientific license distribution process, EV safety education programs, clear safety guidelines, EV modules in driving license training, and expansion of training centers nationwide. They stated that Nepal should raise the issue of climate change strongly in the United Nations General Assembly based on scientific facts and data, as there is ample evidence of glaciers and ice melting due to climate change, which should be integrated and presented at the international forum. They wished for the speedy recovery of the three injured, including the driver, in the car accident that occurred yesterday evening when stones fell on a car traveling from Palpa to Butwal in the Siddhababa section, and thanked the government for bringing the Siddhababa tunnel construction to its final stage. They also urged the government to pay attention to the controversial incident related to transformer installation in Mahottari. They drew the government's attention to the need for immediate repair of the dam of Shera Khola in Itahari, which has put the settlements of Itahari and Duhabi at risk, and for the government to take effective steps for flood control by identifying high-risk areas.

Subsequently, the Chief Whip of the Nepali Congress Parliamentary Party, Honourable Bishwa Prakash Sharma, expressed his views on issues such as the government imposing suffering on citizens instead of wiping their tears during the crisis of the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli rivers, the government's authoritarian and threatening style of functioning, the government considering criticism and opposition as enemies and threatening those in power, the need for the government to provide a clear explanation regarding the incidents of Bhadra 23 and 24 and the escape of prisoners from jail, and the need to form a parliamentary committee regarding the Rasuwagadhi flood.
Thereafter, the Chief Whip of the Rastriya Swatantra Party Parliamentary Party, Honourable Aren Rai, demanded that the government effectively conduct search, rescue, and relief operations following the flood in Rasuwa, as the government had not performed effectively in these areas.
After that, the Honourable Minister for Women, Children, and Senior Citizens, Sita Kumari Gurung, presented a proposal to 'Consider the Children Act (First Amendment) Bill, 2083'.
Subsequently, Honourable Minister for Women, Children, and Senior Citizens, Sita Kumari Gurung, responded to the questions raised by the following Honourable Members during the discussion on the bill:

Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal 'Consider the Children Act (First Amendment) Bill, 2083' for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to give notice of amendments to the 'Children Act (First Amendment) Bill, 2083' under Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, can submit their notices to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singh Durbar.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled to be held on Thursday, Ashoj 1, 2083, at 1:00 PM, and the meeting was adjourned at 4:40 PM.
