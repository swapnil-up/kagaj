(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Thursday, Ashoj 01, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 1:05 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, taking impromptu time, the following Honourable Members drew the government's attention to issues including the situation where local residents are forced to lose their lives prematurely due to the lack of specialist doctors such as urologists and neurologists in hospitals in Karnali Province, the need for the government to immediately arrange specialist doctors and necessary equipment, the immediate resolution of obstacles and problems created in the Melamchi Drinking Water Project affected areas, intensifying the search for those missing in the Trishuli flood by maximizing the use of resources, providing immediate relief to flood-affected families, reconstructing damaged structures, the government taking responsibility for the protection of orphaned children by resuming school operations and implementing an alternative education system, and providing free treatment to the injured, and the alarming state of relief materials received from various countries and donor agencies being left unattended at Tribhuvan Airport.

Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal "Let there be a general discussion on the Agreement relating to the Conservation and Sustainable Use of Marine Biological Diversity of Areas Beyond National Jurisdiction under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea" for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that as there is no provision for any reservation by the Government of Nepal in the Agreement relating to the Conservation and Sustainable Use of Marine Biological Diversity of Areas Beyond National Jurisdiction under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea, there is no provision for amendment as per Rule 227 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083.
