(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
October 06, 2083, Tuesday
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:10 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
In the beginning of the meeting, taking the urgent time, the following Honourable Members raised their concerns regarding contemporary issues, including:
Whether the traffic police are under the Government of Nepal or not, the incident in Tundikhel has created disappointment among citizens towards the state and government, the need to conduct traffic actions through an online system, stating that Section 171 of the Transport and Vehicle Management Act is not practical, the self-immolation of driver Arjun Dodi inside a bus in Tundikhel has raised questions about the state's security mechanism and transport entrepreneurs, and the need to immediately address the police's policing style and the psychosocial health of drivers, drawing attention to the fact that such incidents can be repeated due to low salaries, disrespectful behavior, working hours, and neglect of mental health, 7 people have died in the hospital due to Scrub Typhus disease in one month in Darma Rural Municipality of Salyan, 116 out of 334 tested in Darma and Bagchaur within two months have been confirmed with Scrub Typhus, therefore the government should take necessary initiatives for its control, although it is known that a bill is being drafted regarding Non-Resident Nepalis, the government has not brought the bill even after 6 months of formation, drawing the government's attention to this, due to the increasing despair among the youth, self-immolation incidents have increased across the country, therefore the government should launch mental health-related activities in a timely manner, due to the severe spread of Dengue disease across the country, many people have died and a large number are being infected, therefore the government should pay attention before it becomes an epidemic.

Meanwhile, after Honourable Yagyamani Nyaupane finished speaking, Honourable Guru Prasad Baral, citing the rules, demanded that the words that interfere with the judiciary should be removed from the parliamentary record, stating that such opinions should not be expressed in the parliament and questions should not be raised about the work of the judiciary. Similarly, Honourable Yagyamani Nyaupane stated that the issue of the structure of the constitution should be a subject of parliamentary debate according to the rules, and that the judiciary should quickly resolve the issues of the Gorkha rebellion, and that there should be no interference with the judiciary.
Subsequently, the Secretary of the House of Representatives, Prakash Adhikari, tabled the message received from the National Assembly regarding the "Infectious Animal Disease (Control) Bill, 2083".
After that, Honourable Minister for Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the proposal "Let the House discuss the Agreement on the Conservation and Sustainable Use of Marine Biological Diversity of Areas Beyond National Jurisdiction under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea" for consideration, and the Right Honourable Speaker presented it for decision, and it was unanimously approved.
