The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, taking the floor during the impromptu session, Honourable Parbati BK demanded that the incident in Chaukune, Surkhet, where a husband murdered his wife and injured his daughters after consuming alcohol, be impartially investigated and the guilty be punished, and that the victim family be properly managed. She also demanded that the government launch a special campaign to prevent the growing addiction to alcohol and drugs in society.

Meanwhile, Honourable Parbati BK demanded that the phrase "communist ભાष्य" used by Honourable Ramesh Prasai during his speech be removed from the parliamentary record. Honourable Balawati Sharma stated that raising questions in the house that are related to matters under judicial consideration or court proceedings, as per Rule 105 of the Constitution and the House of Representatives Regulations, would be a violation of Article 105 of the Constitution and the House of Representatives Regulations, and therefore, such matters should not be expressed in the house.
Subsequently, Honourable Minister for Energy, Water Resources and Irrigation, Biraj Bhakta Shrestha, in response to questions raised during the impromptu, zero, and special times, informed the house that the devastating flood in Rasuwa caused a loss of NPR 390 billion, 620 million, and 30 million rupees in the hydropower sector. He also stated that over 800 individuals working in various hydropower projects were missing due to the flood, and the national grid experienced a deficit of 410 megawatts due to the closure of 11 power generation centers. He further informed that studies are underway regarding providing relief to customers in the flood-affected areas by waiving bills, replacing damaged meters free of charge, and providing concessions on tariffs.
After that, the Right Honourable Speaker read out the following letter received from the Office of the President:-
I would like to inform you that as the Right Honourable President, Mr. Ram Chandra Paudel, is scheduled to depart for the United States of America on Ashoj 6, 2083, Tuesday, to participate in the 81st General Assembly of the United Nations, it has been decided, upon the recommendation of the Right Honourable Prime Minister, in accordance with the Constitution of Nepal, that the Honourable Minister for Finance, Dr. Swarnim Wagle, will handle the daily administrative tasks of the Prime Minister during his absence.
(Mahadev Panth)
