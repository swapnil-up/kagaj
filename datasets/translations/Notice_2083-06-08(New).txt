The meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, taking urgent time, the following Honourable Members drew the government's attention to removing the legal complexities seen in rescuing patients suffering from sudden health problems, prioritizing emergency health services, helicopters, and infrastructure preparation for citizens in remote areas; to impartially investigate the incident where a customs officer in Kanchanpur used threatening language towards the Armed Police Superintendent (SP) in Kailali and take action against the guilty officer, and to establish coordination between the security bodies and revenue administration; and to exercise caution regarding the risk of floods and landslides due to the increased possibility of heavy rainfall from the low-pressure system, urging the government to timely alert citizens and provide advance information about floods and landslides through FM, television, mobile apps, call-back tones, and social media, among other issues, and expressed their views, drawing the government's attention:-

Meanwhile, after Hon'ble Guru Prasad Baral finished speaking, Hon'ble Yajmani Neupane raised a point of order, stating that the syndicate in the court should be removed and the court empowered, and that he spoke in accordance with the constitution. Similarly, Hon'ble Guru Prasad Baral, raising a point of order, stated that the Judicial Council is responsible for studying court judgments and that attacking the court and disrespecting it is unacceptable.
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister for Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented a proposal seeking permission to introduce the "Free Legal Aid Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, as no notice of objection was received from any Honourable Member regarding the proposal to seek permission to introduce the said bill, in accordance with Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honourable Minister for Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, introduced the "Free Legal Aid Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned to be held 15 minutes later today at 2:05 PM.

Meanwhile, after Hon'khushbu Oli finished speaking, Hon'ble Midma Gyabu Sherpa requested urgent time and reported that 7 houses were completely destroyed by fire due to an electrical short circuit in Dobhan Bazaar, Myagde Rural Municipality-3, Taplejung, and that the Nepali Army, Nepal Police, Armed Police, and locals were making efforts with fire engines to control the fire.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the proposal "Consideration of the Solid Waste Management Bill, 2083" for decision, which was unanimously approved.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to give notice of amendment to the "Solid Waste Management Bill, 2083" in accordance with Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, can submit their notice to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singhdarbar.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed the Honourable Members that while raising points of order is natural during discussions, it is not appropriate to deviate from the subject matter for which the point of order was raised and to make points of order solely for the purpose of speaking, and that it would be relevant to make points of order with clear basis and justification in accordance with the provisions of the regulations.
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister for Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented a proposal for consideration of the "Free Legal Aid Bill, 2083".
