(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Monday, October 12, 2083
The first meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 11:20 AM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, when the Honorable Members from the Labor Culture Party stood up in front of their seats and sought time to speak, the Honorable Speaker gave time to Honorable Dhuvraj Rai. He raised the issue that the program of direct question and answer with the Prime Minister, which was scheduled, has not been held even after one month and two days have passed. He also raised the issue of the arrest and release of former Chief Justice Cholendra Shumsher Rana in the House, stating that a serious and factual investigation should be conducted when the state makes an arrest.
Thereafter, in the impromptu session that followed, the following Honorable Members drew the government's attention to the damage caused by recent floods, landslides, and inundation, stating that the government's presence during disasters was weak, that laws for disaster management should be enacted immediately, that relief, safe housing, and food arrangements should be made, and that the reconstruction of damaged structures should be initiated. They also described the arrest and release of former Chief Justice Cholendra Shumsher Rana as an illegal practice, stating that the government's action of arresting and releasing someone in the middle of the night without reason was a mockery of democracy and the justice system, that the process adopted by the government was eroding democratic culture, that the government was engaging in politics of revenge under the guise of law, and that transparency was not maintained in the use of force. They also mentioned that due to continuous rain, more than half a dozen houses in the headquarters of the Chame region of Manang were washed away by floods, life was disrupted, and rescue operations were difficult due to disruptions in electricity and communication services, and tourists were stranded. The high flow in the Narayani River has disrupted life in Nawalpur, and in Nawalparasi, including the Susta rural municipality, more than 1100 citizens have been displaced due to inundation in various areas, and various settlements in Bharatpur Metropolitan City, Ichakamana Rural Municipality, and Madi Municipality of Chitwan have been inundated, so the government needs to expedite rescue and relief efforts. They also stated that the flood in the Kaligandaki River due to continuous rain has caused significant damage to life, property, bridges, roads, and other structures in Baglung, Myagdi, Parbat, Syangja, Gulmi, Tanahu, and Nawalparasi. Furthermore, landslides in various places along the Mid-Hilly Highway in Baglung have disconnected the headquarters of all ten local levels, so the government must immediately start the reconstruction of damaged structures. They also stated that containers coming from China to Nepal after the Bhote Koshi flood have been coming through the Kerung border crossing, but the road from that crossing has been damaged by floods, so diplomatic efforts should be made to open the Tatopani border crossing immediately. They also mentioned that the government should provide immediate relief and rehabilitation to the families displaced by the fire in Dobhan Bazaar, Taplejung. They also stated that due to continuous rain, Kamala Shrestha died when her house was buried by a landslide in Dhanusha. They also mentioned that the address given by the Honorable Prime Minister at the 81st General Assembly of the United Nations has made Nepal and Nepalis proud in the world. They also stated that five people died due to floods and landslides in Palpa due to continuous rain. They also mentioned that the flood in the Tinau River in Butwal washed away 5 houses in the Majuwa area of Rupandehi, and some houses are at risk. Due to continuous rain, 24 houses in Gulariya Municipality of Bardiya were inundated, and paddy crops planted on 50,000 hectares of land have been damaged, so the government should provide relief to the farmers. They also stated that due to continuous rain, most roads in southern Lalitpur have been destroyed and there are problems with transportation, so the government and concerned bodies should pay attention to this matter.
