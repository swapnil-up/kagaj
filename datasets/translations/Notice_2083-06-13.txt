(Brief Minutes of the Meeting Proceedings)
Tuesday, October 13, 2083
The first meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 10:10 AM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, Honourable Dhuraj Rai took an impromptu time and stated that the Right Honourable Prime Minister should be present in the parliament to answer the Honourable members' questions and that the government should take the matter seriously, drawing attention to the fact that the question-and-answer program with the Prime Minister, scheduled for August 10, has not yet taken place.

Subsequently, Honourable Chairperson of the Education, Health, and Information Technology Committee, Ojaswi Sherchan, presented the "Report of the Committee on the National Forensic Science Laboratory (Establishment and Operation) Bill, 2083" which originated in the National Assembly.
After that, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister and Minister of Defence, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam proposed a motion seeking permission to present the "National Defence University Bill, 2083".
Thereafter, as no notice of objection was received from any Honourable member in accordance with Rule 105 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2083, on the motion seeking permission to present the said bill, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice, and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister and Minister of Defence, presented the "National Defence University Bill, 2083".
Subsequently, Honourable Minister of Physical Infrastructure and Transport Sunil Lamsal proposed a motion seeking permission to present the "Diploma in Engineering Council Bill, 2083".
After that, as no notice of objection was received from any Honourable member in accordance with Rule 104 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2083, on the motion seeking permission to present the said bill, Honourable Minister of Physical Infrastructure and Transport Sunil Lamsal presented the "Diploma in Engineering Council Bill, 2083".
