Thereafter, the Honourable Leader of the Parliamentary Party of the main opposition party, Nepali Congress, Bhishma Raj Aadembe, expressed his views on issues such as the democratic system of governance and the arrest and release of former Chief Justice Cholendra Shumsher Rana, and offered suggestions to the government.
Subsequently, the Honourable Deputy Leader of the Rastriya Swatantra Party Parliamentary Party, Ganesh Parajuli, stated that constructive cooperation is needed in disaster management, rising above accusations; seasonal events like floods, landslides, and inundation should be viewed as impacts of climate change, and Nepal should take the initiative to establish shared international responsibility for the conservation of the Himalayan region; administrative systems for good governance should be brought under law and impartiality, and steps like adopting a digital system for employee transfers are on the right track, and public cooperation is indispensable for ending corruption.
Thereafter, the Chief Whip of the Labour Culture Party, Aren Rai, drew the government's attention to the need for the government to deliver results on pressing public issues, stating that the economy is sustained by remittances, youth migration, rising inflation, and the government's neglect of issues like corruption, and that the commitment to good governance is limited to hollow slogans. He also pointed out that the government has failed to open files related to the Giribandhu Tea Estate and the Wide Body aircraft scandal, and has suppressed the Gen Z movement.

Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the item listed at serial number 1 in today's agenda has been removed from the order of business.
Subsequently, the Honourable Chairperson of the State Affairs and Good Governance Committee, Hari Thakal, presented the "Report of the Committee on the Bill Relating to the Remuneration, Service Conditions, and Facilities of Office Bearers of Constitutional Bodies (First Amendment), 2083".
Thereafter, the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle's proposals to send the "Employees Provident Fund (Tenth Amendment) Bill, 2083, Citizen Investment Fund (Third Amendment) Bill, 2083, and Payment and Settlement (First Amendment) Bill, 2083" to the respective committees for clause-wise discussion were presented by the Right Honourable Speaker for decision and were unanimously approved.
After that, Honourable Asha Jha requested urgent time and drew the government's attention to the negligence at the airport, stating that on a domestic flight from Kathmandu to Biratnagar on 2083/06/14, a foreign national became unconscious, and although there was a help desk with a cabin at the airport, a sensitive location, there was no arrangement for necessary health facilities and manpower. She urged the Civil Aviation Authority to monitor and supervise basic standards and arrange for health facilities to be provided equally to Nepali and foreign citizens.
Unanimously Passed:
Condolence Resolution
"This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses deep sorrow over the untimely demise of Ghanendra Basnet, who was elected Member of Parliament from Sankhuwasabha Constituency No. 2 in the 2048 BS House of Representatives election and from Bhojpur Constituency No. 1 in the 2056 BS House of Representatives election, and who played a leading role in the establishment of democracy and the democratic system, at the age of 74 on October 1, 2083. This meeting expresses heartfelt condolences and sympathy to the bereaved family."
Thereafter, the House stood for 1 minute of silence in memory of the soul of the late Ghanendra Basnet.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 3:20 PM, to be held on Friday, October 3, 2083, at 11:00 AM.
