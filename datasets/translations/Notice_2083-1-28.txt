(Brief Description of Meeting Proceedings)
Monday, Baishakh 28, 2083
The first meeting of the second session of the House of Representatives, 2082 commenced at 2:05 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
As soon as the meeting began, Honourable members from the Nepali Congress, Communist Party of Nepal (UML), Communist Party of Nepal (Maoist Centre), Rastriya Prajatantra Party, and Janamat Party stood up in front of their seats and requested time. The Right Honourable Speaker then informed that after paying tribute to the martyrs, impromptu time would be provided to the Honourable members on a party basis, after which the Honourable members took their seats.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker read out the following letter from the Right Honourable President regarding the summoning of the session:-
"In accordance with Article 93, Sub-section (1) of the Constitution of Nepal, on the recommendation of the Government of Nepal, Council of Ministers, dated Baishakh 22, 2083, I have summoned the session of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2083, at 14:00 hours at the Federal Parliament Building, Singhdarbar."
Date: Baishakh 22, 2083
(Ram Chandra Poudel)
Subsequently, the House observed a minute of silence standing to pay tribute to all the unknown martyrs who sacrificed their lives in various movements for national interest, democracy, and progressive change, as well as in the Jana Andolan.
Immediately thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker welcomed all the Honourable members present at the first meeting of the second session of the House.
Following this, the following Honourable members expressed their views on contemporary issues such as the special arrangements made applicable to the members of the House in the draft rules of procedure of the House of Representatives, the government's removal of informal settlements without a management plan, leading to loss of life, the need to caution the Kantipur Daily newspaper through the Press Council for severely impacting the image of MPs and parties by linking them to the Smart Telecom scam, objection to the ordinance to abolish trade unions, the government's attempt to rule by ordinance despite advocating for the rule of law, the government's inability to present a clear stance on the Lipulek pass issue, and the government's increase in foreign debt without a plan:-
1. Honourable Nishkal Rai
2. Honourable Padra Kumari Aryal
3. Honourable Jagdish Kharel (Lalitpur)
4. Honourable Yubaraj Dulal
5. Honourable Harkaraj Rai
6. Honourable Khushbu Oli

Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed the House that an order to remove obstacles was issued on Thursday, Baishakh 3, 2083, by exercising the authority under Rules 260 and 261 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079, to facilitate the immediate reshuffling of names of some Honourable members in the Federal Parliament's joint committees and thematic committees of the House of Representatives when the session of the House of Representatives is not in session, and to remove obstacles in reshuffling names of members in some committees. The proposal to approve the said order to remove obstacles was presented to the House in accordance with Rule 260 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079, and the order to remove obstacles was approved by a majority.
Thereafter, Honourable Ganesh Parajuli, Chairperson of the Draft Rules Committee of the House of Representatives, presented the "Report of the Draft Rules Committee of the House of Representatives, 2083".
Subsequently, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Sher Bahadur Deuba, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam presented the "Public Procurement (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2083", the "Special Provisions Ordinance Regarding Dismissal from Office, 2083", and the "Constitutional Council (Functions, Duties, Authority, and Procedures) (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2083" respectively.
Following this, Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle presented the "Anti-Money Laundering (Third Amendment) Ordinance, 2083".
Subsequently, Honourable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam presented the "Ordinance to Amend Some Nepal Laws, 2083".
Thereafter, Honourable Minister of Land Management, Cooperatives and Poverty Alleviation Pratibha Rawal presented the "Cooperatives (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2083".
Subsequently, Honourable Minister of Health and Population Nisha Mehta presented the "Ordinance to Amend Some Nepal Laws Regarding Health Science Academies, 2083".
Subsequently, Honourable Minister of Education, Science and Technology Sasmit Pokharel presented the "Ordinance to Amend Some Nepal Laws Regarding Universities, 2083".
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker requested all Honourable members to take their seats by 3:45 PM as the Right Honourable President would be addressing a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament at 4:00 PM today.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 3:00 PM to be held on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2082, at 5:00 PM.

The second meeting of the House of Representatives today commenced at 6:00 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
As soon as the meeting began, Honourable Guru Prasad Baral stood up in front of his seat and requested time. When granted time, he raised issues regarding the Prime Minister's attire on the day of the President's address, questioning whether it was appropriate for a person holding the responsibility of Prime Minister to disregard national dignity and to turn away from the President while the President was presenting the government's policy and program, in accordance with Rule 20 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079. The Right Honourable Speaker acknowledged the issues raised by the Honourable member and directed the Secretariat to investigate the matter.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Prime Minister Sher Bahadur Deuba tabled the copy of the address made by the Right Honourable President in the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker presented the following motion of thanks for the address made by the Right Honourable President, in accordance with Sub-rule (2) of Rule 37 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079, for decision, which was unanimously passed:-
Motion of Thanks
"This meeting of the House of Representatives expresses its gratitude to the Right Honourable President for the address delivered to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2083, in accordance with Article 95 of the Constitution of Nepal."
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that the discussion on the Government of Nepal's Annual Policy and Program for the fiscal year 2083/084, mentioned in the address by the Right Honourable President, and the Prime Minister's response would be held on Baishakh 30, 2083, in accordance with Sub-rule (1) of Rule 38 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079. He further informed that for the discussion on the Annual Policy and Program for the fiscal year 2083/084, the office bearers of the respective parties should submit the names of the speakers to the Business Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singhdarbar, one hour before the meeting begins. He also informed that Honourable members wishing to submit amendments to the Government of Nepal's Annual Policy and Program for the fiscal year 2083/084 can submit their notice to the Business Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singhdarbar, between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM on Baishakh 29, 2083, and that the amendment proposal forms would also be available from the same section.
Subsequently, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 6:10 PM to be held on Tuesday, Baishakh 29, 2083, at 1:00 PM.
