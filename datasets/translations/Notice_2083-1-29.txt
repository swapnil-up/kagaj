(Brief Record of Meeting Proceedings)
Tuesday, Baisakh 29, 2083
The meeting of the House of Representatives commenced today at 1:05 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
As soon as the meeting began, the Honourable Members of the Nepali Congress stood up in front of their seats. Although the Right Honourable Speaker requested to commence the Zero Hour, as the Zero Hour and the program for top party leaders to speak were scheduled, the Honourable Members remained standing. The Right Honourable Speaker then gave the floor to Honourable Basana Thapa, who stated that the Prime Minister leaving the meeting during the presentation of the government's policy and program in yesterday's meeting was contrary to parliamentary tradition and state responsibility. She demanded a response from the government on this matter and drew attention to ensure such incidents do not recur.

Subsequently, Honourable Sushant Baidya raised a point of order, stating that in the course of criticizing the government, an Honourable Member used inappropriate and unparliamentary language such as 'Hitler' and 'gas chamber'. He requested that respectful, responsible, and fact-based language be used in parliamentary speeches, that such words be expunged from the record, and that the concerned Honourable Member be directed to retract their statement. The Right Honourable Speaker acknowledged the point of order.
After that, the Leader of the Opposition in the House of Representatives, Honourable Bhimraj Aadem, and former Prime Minister Honourable Pushpa Kamal Dahal 'Prachanda' presented their views.

Following Honourable Ram Bahadur Thapa's speech, Honourable Manish Khanal raised a point of order, stating that the previous speaker's remarks were against parliamentary decorum, that the elected House was accused of being a chaotic mob, and that expressions bringing the Nepali Army into controversy were made, including accusations against Rastriya Swatantra Party's 'blue revolution'. He demanded that these remarks be expunged from the record. Furthermore, Honourable Khushbu Oli also raised a point of order, stating that Honourable Ram Bahadur Thapa repeatedly made expressions that brought the Nepali Army into controversy by levelling various accusations against it, and demanded that these remarks be expunged from the record. He also stated that the phrase 'even shame was ashamed' used by Honourable Ganesh Parajuli while speaking in the House was against parliamentary decorum and requested that the Honourable Member be cautioned.
Meanwhile, after Honourable Jan Bahadur Shahi finished speaking, Honourable Parvati BK and Honourable Ganesh Karki attempted to raise points of order. The Right Honourable Speaker urged the Honourable Members to raise points of order only at the appropriate time as per the rules and to maintain parliamentary decorum.
Subsequently, the Honourable Minister for Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, presented the "Agreement between the Government of Nepal and the Government of the Republic of India on Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters".

Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker put the motion 'Consideration of the Alternative Development Finance Mobilization Bill, 2082' to a vote, and it was approved unanimously.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that Honourable Members wishing to present amendments to the "Alternative Development Finance Mobilization Bill, 2082" in accordance with Rule 112 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079, may submit them to the Bill Section (House of Representatives), Federal Parliament Secretariat, Singha Durbar.
Thereafter, the next meeting of the House of Representatives was scheduled to convene on Wednesday, Baisakh 30, 2083, at 11:00 AM, and the meeting was adjourned at 5:20 PM.
