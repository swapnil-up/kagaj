Subsequently, Honourable Manish Khanal raised a point of order, stating that the unparliamentary words spoken by Honourable Ram Bahadur Thapa in yesterday's meeting should be removed from the record. In response to his query as to whether the unparliamentary words had been removed or not, the Right Honourable Speaker directed the Secretariat to remove the said unparliamentary words spoken by Honourable Ram Bahadur Thapa, which were contrary to Rule 21(d) of the Regulations.
Thereafter, the Right Honourable Speaker, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balendra Shah, granted permission to the Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle to present the motion: "Let the discussion on the Annual Policy and Program of the Government of Nepal for the fiscal year 2083/084, as mentioned in the address by the Right Honourable President to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2083, commence." However, as all Honourable Members of the main opposition and other opposition parties stood up in front of their seats, demanding that the Prime Minister present the proposal, and engaged in sloganeering and commotion, the Right Honourable Speaker urged the Honourable Members to maintain decorum and listen calmly when presenting issues. Despite this, the Honourable Members continued to stand and engage in sloganeering and commotion.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker, reminding the Honourable Members of Rule 38 of the Regulations, gave time to the following Honourable Members, who stated that while there is a tradition of the Prime Minister responding to discussions on the policy and program, Rule 38 of the Regulations allows the Prime Minister or another minister designated by him to respond. They argued that the Prime Minister's presence is mandatory for discussions on an important government document, that the Prime Minister should not insult the House, that it is objectionable that the Finance Minister attempted to present it when the agenda stated the Prime Minister himself would present it, that the ruling party has misinterpreted Rule 38, that the reason for the Prime Minister's absence from the House was not disclosed, and that the House proceedings should be taken seriously, among other points:-
1. Honourable Yuvraj Dulal
2. Honourable Gyan Bahadur Shahi
3. Honourable Bhimraj Aadembe
4. Honourable Kavindra Bullakoti
5. Honourable Arjun Narsingh K.C.
6. Honourable Guru Prasad Baral
7. Honourable Ain Bahadur Mahar
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker, stating that he had noted the issues clearly raised by the Honourable Members of the opposition parties and that there is a clear provision in the regulations, and that the Right Honourable Prime Minister had sent a letter as per his schedule, requested them to take their seats. However, as all Honourable Members of the opposition parties remained standing, the Right Honourable Speaker adjourned the meeting for fifteen minutes.
Thereafter, the meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 1:15 PM, to reconvene fifteen minutes later today.
The meeting of the House of Representatives, adjourned at [time not specified] PM to reconvene fifteen minutes later today, commenced at 2:40 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
At the beginning of the meeting, as all Honourable Members of the main opposition and other opposition parties stood up in front of their seats, engaging in sloganeering and commotion, the Right Honourable Speaker provided time to Honourable Ain Bahadur Mahar, who stated that he needed to know which specific issues spoken by the leader of the CPN (UML) parliamentary party the Right Honourable Speaker had directed to be removed from the record as per Rule 21(d) of the Regulations. Furthermore, Honourable Ganesh Parajuli of the ruling party drew attention to the need to remove from the parliamentary record the expressions made against the judge during his speech, which were contrary to Rule 21(d) of the Regulations and Article 105 of the Constitution.

Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker directed the Secretariat to remove the words "mob of anarchists" and "those who burn the country wearing the national flag, the Nepali army is a mute spectator" used by Honourable Ram Bahadur Thapa during his speech on Baishakh 29, 2083, from the House records.
Thereafter, Honourable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle, on behalf of the Right Honourable Prime Minister Balen Shah, presented the motion: "Let the discussion on the Annual Policy and Program of the Government of Nepal for the fiscal year 2083/084, as mentioned in the address by the Right Honourable President to a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2083, commence."
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that 35 notices of amendment to the Annual Policy and Program of the Government of Nepal for the fiscal year 2083/084 had been received. As all Honourable Members of the main opposition party stood up in front of their seats, the Right Honourable Speaker, stating that he had conducted the session in accordance with the discussions among the parties, urged them to take their seats. He then gave time to Honourable Bhimraj Aadembe, who pointed out that the Annual Policy and Program of the Government of Nepal could be tabled by the Honourable Finance Minister, but the Prime Minister must be present and provide the response. The Right Honourable Speaker informed that this issue had been noted.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker repeatedly urged the House to cooperate in advancing the day's agenda, stating that he would discuss with the parties after communicating with the Right Honourable Prime Minister and then set tomorrow's agenda, and urged them to cooperate in advancing the discussion on the policy and program for the sake of the public and the country. However, as the opposition parties maintained their demands, Honourable Kavindra Bullakoti of the ruling party stated that the program should proceed as per Rule 38 of the Regulations, which allows the Prime Minister or another minister designated by him to respond.
Thereafter, as the opposition parties remained standing, maintaining their demands, and despite the Right Honourable Speaker repeatedly requesting cooperation to advance the day's agenda, the Right Honourable Speaker adjourned the meeting for fifteen minutes.
Thereafter, the meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 3:30 PM, to reconvene fifteen minutes later today.
The meeting of the House of Representatives, adjourned at [time not specified] PM to reconvene fifteen minutes later today, commenced at 5:20 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
As soon as the meeting commenced, all Honourable Members of the main opposition and other opposition parties stood up in front of their seats, engaging in sloganeering and commotion. The Right Honourable Speaker stated that he would provide time for issues that would be beneficial to the citizens and that he had noted the issues raised by the Honourable Members. However, the opposition parties remained standing, maintaining their demands.
Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker informed that all programs from serial number 2 to 5 on today's agenda had been removed from the list. He also informed that as per Rule 38(1) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, the date of Baishakh 31, 2083, had been set for presenting amendments to the Annual Policy and Program of the Government of Nepal for the fiscal year 2083/084, as mentioned in the Right Honourable President's address, for participation in the discussion, and for the Right Honourable Prime Minister to provide a response.

Subsequently, although the Right Honourable Speaker stated that he had noted the issues raised by the Honourable Members, the opposition parties remained standing, maintaining their demands. Therefore, the Right Honourable Speaker adjourned the meeting until Thursday, Baishakh 31, 2083, at 11:00 AM.
Thereafter, the meeting of the House of Representatives was adjourned at 5:30 PM, to reconvene on Thursday, Baishakh 31, 2083, at 11:00 AM.
