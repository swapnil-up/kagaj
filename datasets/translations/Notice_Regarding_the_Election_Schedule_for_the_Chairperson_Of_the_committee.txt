House of Representatives Secretariat Notice Regarding Election Program for Committee Chairperson In accordance with Rule 173 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, the Honorable Speaker has scheduled the election of the Chairperson of the committee formed under the said rule for Friday, April 4, 2083 (BS). The election program is published as follows:

S.N. Program Date Location Time
1. A member of the relevant committee of the House of Representatives proposes that a member of the same committee be elected as the Chairperson, and another member of the same committee seconds the proposal, and the proposal is registered in accordance with Rule 176, Sub-rule (2) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079. April 3, 2083 Thursday 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM Secretariat of the House of Representatives, Singha Durbar
2. A list of proposals registered within the stipulated time will be published in order of registration. April 3, 2083 Thursday 4:30 PM Secretariat Notice Party
3. The proposer presents the proposal in the meeting of the relevant committee, the seconder supports the relevant proposal, and the proposals will be decided in accordance with Rule 176, Sub-rules (4), (5), and (6) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079. April 4, 2083 Friday 1:00 PM Meeting Room of the Relevant Committee

Furthermore: If Honorable Ministers who are ex-officio members of more than one committee are to vote in the election of the Chairperson, they must inform the relevant committee in writing 24 hours prior to the election as to which committee they will vote in.

(Harka Raj Rai)
Secretary, House of Representatives
Date: March 18, 2082 Monday
Note: A recent photograph and a copy of Nepali citizenship of the proposed candidate, who is an Honorable Member, must be attached.
