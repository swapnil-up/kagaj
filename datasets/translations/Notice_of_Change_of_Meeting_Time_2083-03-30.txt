House of Representatives, Federal Parliament of Nepal
Notice No. 9, Nepal Parliament, June 15, 2025, Tuesday
Information regarding the change of meeting time
The meeting scheduled for 11:00 AM on Wednesday, June 16, 2025, has been postponed. Due to the inability of the designated representatives to attend the meeting at the scheduled time, the Speaker has decided, in accordance with Rule 6, Sub-rule (3) of the Rules of Procedure of the House of Representatives, 2083, to convene the meeting of the House of Representatives at 9:00 AM on the same day.
This notice is published for information.
Prasad Pandey
Secretary-General
