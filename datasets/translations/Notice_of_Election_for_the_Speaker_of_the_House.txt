Notice Regarding the Election Program for the Speaker Position
In accordance with Rule 7 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079, the election for the Speaker of the House of Representatives will be held during the meeting of the House of Representatives scheduled for Sunday, Chaitra 22, 2082, at 11:00 AM. The Honorable Senior Member has decided the date and time for the election. Accordingly, for the purpose of Sub-rule (1) of Rule 7 of the same Regulations, the election program is published as follows: -
