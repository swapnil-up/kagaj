House of Representatives Secretariat, Parliament of Nepal, Kathmandu, Singhdarbar. Urgent Notice Regarding the Convening of the Session. It is hereby notified to all honorable members that His Excellency the Right Honorable President of Nepal, Ram Chandra Paudel, in accordance with Article 93, Sub-article (1) of the Constitution of Nepal, has convened the session of the National Assembly and the House of Representatives on Thursday, Chaitra 9, 2081 BS, at 2:00 PM. The members are requested to be present at the National Assembly building under construction, located at Singhdarbar, Kathmandu. Secretary General (Padma Prasad Pandey).
