House of Representatives Secretariat, Parliament, Kathmandu, Singhdarbar. Urgent notice regarding the convening of the session of the Federal Parliament. His Excellency the President, Ram Chandra Paudel, in accordance with Article 93, Sub-section (1) of the Constitution of Nepal, has convened the session of the Federal Parliament on Thursday, Chaitra 9, 2081 BS, at 2:00 PM. I hereby request the honorable members to attend the session. Venue: National Assembly, Parliament Secretariat Building, Singhdarbar. Venue: Multi-purpose Hall, National Assembly, Singhdarbar. (Padma Prasad Pandey) Secretary-General.
