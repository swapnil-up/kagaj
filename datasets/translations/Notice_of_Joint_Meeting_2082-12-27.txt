(Brief Description of Meeting Proceedings)
Friday, Chaitra 27, 2082 BS
The joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament commenced at 3:15 PM under the chairmanship of the Right Honourable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal. First, the Right Honourable Prime Minister Pushpa Kamal Dahal 'Prachanda' presented the address of the Right Honourable President to the Parliament. Thereafter, the Right Honourable President Ram Chandra Paudel addressed the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament. Subsequently, the Right Honourable Speaker requested the Honourable Members to stand in their respective places and bid farewell to the Right Honourable President after the meeting was adjourned. Thereafter, the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament was adjourned at 3:30 PM.
