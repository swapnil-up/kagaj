(Brief details of the meeting proceedings)
Thursday, Jestha 15, 2083
The joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament commenced at 4:05 PM under the chairmanship of the Honorable Speaker Dol Prasad Aryal.
Thereafter, the Honorable Finance Minister Dr. Swarnim Wagle, presenting the Annual Budget Statement (Budget) of Revenue and Expenditure for the Fiscal Year 2083/084 in accordance with Rule 5 of the Federal Parliament's Joint Meeting and Joint Committee (Operation) Regulations, 2080, informed that a total appropriation of Rs. 2124.34 billion has been allocated, comprising Rs. 1270.58 billion for recurrent expenditure, which is 49.8 percent of the total appropriation, Rs. 431.10 billion for capital expenditure, which is 20.3 percent, and Rs. 422.64 billion for financial management, which is 19.9 percent.
Subsequently, the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament was adjourned at 6:00 PM.
