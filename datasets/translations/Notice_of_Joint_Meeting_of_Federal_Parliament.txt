Joint Meeting
Information Regarding Convening
As per Article 94 of the Constitution of Nepal, the Honorable Ram Chandra Poudel, President, will address the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Chaitra 27, 2082, at 3:00 PM. This notice is published for the information of the members of the Federal Parliament. Members of the Federal Parliament are requested to be present in their respective seats in the Joint Meeting Hall, Singha Durbar, by 2:45 PM on the said day and take their seats.
