Federal Parliament
Notice Paper - 4
Nepal
Wednesday, Chaitra 25, 2082
Joint Meeting
Information regarding the convening of a joint meeting
As per Article 94 of the Constitution of Nepal, the Right Honourable Ram Chandra Paudel, President, has convened a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Chaitra 27, 2082, at 3:00 PM for the purpose of addressing the Federal Parliament. This notice is published for the information of the members of the Federal Parliament.
Furthermore, all members of the House of Representatives and the National Assembly are requested to be present in their respective seats in the Parliament House, Singha Durbar, by 2:45 PM on the said day for the joint meeting.
Padma Prasad Pandey
Secretary-General
