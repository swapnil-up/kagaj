2083 Baisakh 25, Friday
Notice regarding the convening of a joint meeting
As per Article 94 of the Constitution of Nepal, the Right Honorable President Ramchandra Poudel will address the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Baisakh 28, 2083, Monday, at 16:00 hrs. This notice is published for the information of the members of the Federal Parliament. Furthermore, all members are requested to be present in the House of Representatives (Multipurpose Hall, Singhdarbar) by 15:45 hrs on the said day and take their respective seats for the joint meeting.
Padma Prasad Pandey
Secretary-General
