Notice-1
Date: 2083 Bhadra 9, Tuesday
Information regarding change in meeting date and time

The meeting of the House of Representatives, which was scheduled to be held on Tuesday, Bhadra 9, 2083, at 9:00 AM, could not be held on the scheduled date and time due to special reasons. Therefore, as per Rule 6, Sub-rule (3) of the Rules of Procedure of the House of Representatives, 2083, the Speaker has rescheduled the meeting to be held on Wednesday, Bhadra 10, 2083, at 9:00 AM. This notice is published for the information of all the Honourable Members of the House of Representatives.

Padma Prasad Pandey
Secretary-General
