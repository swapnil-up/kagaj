Federal Parliament

Notice-1
Date: 2083 Jestha 25, Monday
Regarding the change in the day and time of the meeting.
Today, Jestha 25, 2083, Monday, the meeting of the House of Representatives, which was adjourned for fifteen minutes at 1:30 PM, could not be held at the said time due to special reasons. Therefore, as per sub-rule (3) of rule 6 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honorable Speaker has scheduled the meeting to be held on Tuesday, Jestha 26, 2083, at 9:00 AM. This notice is published for the information of the Honorable Members.

- P. P. Pandey
Secretary-General
