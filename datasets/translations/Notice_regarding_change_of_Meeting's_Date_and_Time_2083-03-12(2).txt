Notice-9
2083 Ashar 12, Friday
Notice regarding change in the day and time of the meeting.
Today, Ashar 12, 2083, Notice-1 was published, and the meeting of the House of Representatives scheduled to be held on Saturday, Ashar 13, 2083, at 9:00 AM could not be held at the said time due to special reasons. Therefore, in accordance with Rule 6, Sub-rule (3) of the House of Representatives Regulations 2083, the Honorable Speaker has determined that the meeting of the House of Representatives will be held on Sunday, Ashar 14, 2083, at 11:00 AM. This notice has been published for the information of the Honorable Members.
Padma Prasad Pandey
Secretary-General
