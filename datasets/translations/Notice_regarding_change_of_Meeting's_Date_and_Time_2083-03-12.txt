Notice-1
Date: 2083 Ashad 12, Friday
Notice Regarding Change in Day and Time of Meeting
Today, the meeting of the House of Representatives scheduled to be held on Friday, Ashad 12, 2083 at 1:00 PM could not be held at the specified time due to special reasons. Therefore, in accordance with Rule 6, Sub-rule (3) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honorable Speaker has decided to convene the meeting of the House on Saturday, Ashad 13, 2083 at 11:00 AM. This notice is published for the information of the Honorable Members.
Padma Prasad Pandey
Secretary-General
