Notice No. 9
Date: Asar 10, 2083
Wednesday
Information Regarding Meeting
Change of date and time
Time: 11:00 AM
Date: Asar 11, 2083, Thursday
Morning
It has been decided that the meeting of the House of Representatives will be held on Thursday, Asar 11, 2083, in the morning at 11:00 AM.
However, due to a change in the schedule, the meeting cannot be held at the scheduled time.
Therefore, as per Rule 6, Sub-rule (3) of the Rules of Procedure of the House of Representatives, 2083, the Honorable Speaker has rescheduled the meeting to be held on Friday, Asar 12, 2083, at 11:00 AM.
This notice is published for the information of the Honorable Members of the House of Representatives.
Padma Prasad Pandey
Secretary-General
