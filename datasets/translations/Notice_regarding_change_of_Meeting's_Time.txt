Parliament of Nepal House of Representatives Notice Regarding Change in Meeting Time Date: Jeth 27, 2083 Wednesday The meeting of the House of Representatives scheduled to be held today, Jeth 27, 2083, Wednesday at 11:00 AM has been postponed due to special reasons. As per the proviso to sub-rule (2) of rule 6 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, the Honorable Speaker has rescheduled the meeting of the House of Representatives to be held today at 2:00 PM. This notice is published for the information of the Honorable Members. Padm Prasad Pandey Secretary General
