Notice Regarding the Meeting
Regarding the meeting of the House of Representatives which was postponed until further notice by Notice Paper-1 published on 2083 Bhadra 11, the Honorable Speaker has scheduled the meeting of the House of Representatives to be held on Wednesday, 2083 Bhadra 17, at 1:00 PM, in accordance with Rule 6, Sub-rule (3) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083. This notice is published for the information of the Honorable Members.
