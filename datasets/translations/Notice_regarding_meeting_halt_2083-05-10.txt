Notice Regarding Adjournment of Meeting
Due to the fact that the members of the House of Representatives need to be involved in relief and rescue operations, along with the government, in the current situation caused by recent floods and landslides, and therefore cannot attend the meeting on the determined date and time, the meeting of the House of Representatives has been adjourned until further notice, in accordance with Rule 6, Sub-rule (3) of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2083, as directed by the Honorable Speaker. This notice is published for the information of the Honorable Members.
