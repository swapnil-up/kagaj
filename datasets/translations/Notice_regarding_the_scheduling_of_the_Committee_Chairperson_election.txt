Notice regarding the determination of the date for the election of the Committee Chairperson

In accordance with the Federal Parliament Joint Meeting and Joint Committee (Operation) Regulations, 2080, the election of the Chairperson for the Parliamentary Hearing Committee and the Committee for Monitoring and Evaluating the Implementation of State Directors' Principles, Policies, and Responsibilities has been scheduled by the Right Honorable Speaker for Friday, 2083/01/04, at 1:00 PM, in accordance with Rule 44 of the Federal Parliament Joint Meeting and Joint Committee (Operation) Regulations, 2080, and Sub-rule (1) of Rule 176 of the House of Representatives Regulations, 2079. This notice is published for the information of all Honorable Members.

Harkaraj Rai
