Note:
1. An emergency time may be allocated at the beginning of each meeting depending on the seriousness of the subject matter.
2. Even if not included in the agenda, any urgent matter (presentation of new bills, presentation of committee reports on bills, discussion on proposals, etc.) can be included in or removed from the agenda if it is deemed necessary to discuss/consider.
3. If a Member of Parliament requests time for a statement of public importance from the Hon'ble Minister, it can be granted at the beginning of the meeting, and if time is requested for a response during Zero/Special/Emergency Time, it can be granted after Zero Hour.
4. Time will be made available upon request to the Leader of the Opposition, former Prime Minister, former Speaker, etc., in accordance with parliamentary practice.
5. Days when the House does not meet are allocated for committee meetings and other purposes. Committee meetings can also be held before or after the House meeting on days when the House meets.
6. Articles 24, 27, and 30 of the Regulations, as well as other matters, may be included in the agenda as needed.
