Presentation of Annual Report
1. Honorable Minister for Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs, Sobita Gautam, on behalf of the Right Honorable Prime Minister, will present the "Sixth Annual Report of the National Foundation for the Development of Indigenous Nationalities, Fiscal Year 2081/082".
Proposal of Urgent Public Importance
2. Honorable Bhim Raj Aidembe will present a proposal of urgent public importance stating that "It is necessary for the government to identify landless, squatters, and unorganized residents, and to make necessary arrangements for their protection and resettlement, ensuring safe housing, basic services, food, health, education, and livelihood continuity, providing appropriate compensation, guaranteeing permanent resettlement, and distributing land ownership certificates to genuine landless and squatters as soon as possible."
Honorable Ain Bahadur Mahat, Honorable Yuvraj Dulal, and Honorable Karen Rai will support the said proposal.
