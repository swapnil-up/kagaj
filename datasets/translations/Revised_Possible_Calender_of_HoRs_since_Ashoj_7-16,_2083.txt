Note:
1. At the beginning of each meeting, depending on the gravity of the subject matter, there may be an 'Accidental Time'.
2. Matters not included in the schedule but urgently required (e.g., presentation of new bills, submission of committee reports on bills, discussion on motions) may be included in the agenda or removed.
3. If the Honorable Minister requests time for a statement of public importance, it may be provided at the beginning of the meeting, and for a response to Zero Hour/Special Time/Accidental Time, it may be provided after Zero Hour.
4. Time may be provided to the Leader of the Opposition, former Prime Ministers, former Speakers, etc., as per parliamentary practice.
5. Days when the House does not meet are reserved for committee meetings etc. Committee meetings may also be held before or after the House meeting on days when the House meets.
6. Provisions of Chapter 24, 27, and 30 of the Regulations, and other matters as needed, may be included in the agenda.
