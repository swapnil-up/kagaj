Federal Parliament
House of Representatives, 2082
First Session
Brief Information on Work Done
Honorable Members, the First Session of the House of Representatives commenced on Thursday, Chaitra 19, 2082 BS and continued for a total of 9 days until Friday, Chaitra 27, 2082 BS. During this period, meetings were held 6 times on 6 days, with a total of 10 hours and 50 minutes spent on parliamentary proceedings. Additionally, on Friday, Chaitra 27, 2082 BS, a joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament was held, spending a total of 15 minutes.

On Chaitra 27, 2082 BS, the Right Honorable President addressed the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament, as per Article 95 of the Constitution of Nepal, following the first session after the election of the House of Representatives. The House unanimously passed a motion of thanks for the address made by the Right Honorable President.

As per Article 104 of the Constitution of Nepal, to conduct its proceedings, maintain order in meetings, and make other proceedings regular and effective, the House of Representatives, until it formulates its own rules, will conduct its proceedings in accordance with the "House of Representatives Rules, 2079 BS". This proposal was unanimously approved by the House in its meeting on Chaitra 19, 2082 BS.

The "Voter Name List (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2082 BS", "House of Representatives Member Election (First Amendment) Ordinance, 2082 BS", and "Nepal Special Service (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2082 BS", which were presented in the meeting on Chaitra 19, 2082 BS, were approved by the meeting held on Chaitra 23, 2082 BS.

In the election for the Speaker held on Chaitra 22, 2082 BS, Honorable Rabi Lamichhane as proposer, and Honorable Minister of Law, Justice and Parliamentary Affairs Sobita Gautam, Honorable Minister of Finance Swarnim Wagle, and Honorable Minister of Physical Infrastructure and Transport and Minister of Urban Development Sunil Lamshal as supporters, the sole proposal "That Honorable Member Dol Prasad Aryal be elected as the Speaker of the House of Representatives" was received, and the House unanimously elected Honorable Dol Prasad Aryal as Speaker.

The "Alternative Development Fund Operation Bill, 2082 BS", registered in the House of Representatives during this session, was presented.

Similarly, messages passed by the National Assembly regarding the "Forensic Science Laboratory (Establishment and Operation) Bill, 2081 BS", "Tourism Bill, 2081 BS", and "Film Bill, 2081 BS", which originated in the National Assembly, have been presented in the House.

In the meeting held on Chaitra 23, 2082 BS, in accordance with Rule 13 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079 BS, 29 honorable members were nominated to the House of Representatives Business Advisory Committee. Furthermore, on the same day, Honorable Kavinra Bulety as proposer, and Honorable Sita Thapaliya, Honorable Ganesh Singh Thagunna, Honorable Yuvraj Dulal, Honorable Dhubraj Rai, Honorable Bharat Giri, and Honorable Pushpa Kumari Chaudhary as supporters, presented a proposal for the formation of a 15-member "House of Representatives Rules Drafting Committee", which was unanimously approved by the House.

In the election for the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives held on Chaitra 27, 2082 BS, out of two proposals, one stating "That Honorable Member Sarita Lama be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives" and another stating "That Honorable Member Ruby Kumari be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives", the proposal "That Honorable Member Ruby Kumari be elected as the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives" received two hundred and twenty-nine (229) votes and was passed by a majority of the total members present in the House of Representatives.

The "Seventh Annual Report of the National Natural Resources and Fiscal Commission, 2082 BS" was presented in this session.

On Chaitra 27, 2082 BS, in accordance with Rules 25 and 28 of the Federal Parliament Joint Meeting and Joint Committee (Operation) Rules, 2080 BS, the formation of the Parliamentary Hearing Committee and the Committee for Monitoring and Evaluating the Implementation of State Directors' Principles, Policies, and Responsibilities was completed. Additionally, in accordance with Rule 175 of the House of Representatives Rules, 2079 BS, the formation of 10 different thematic committees under the House of Representatives was completed.

During this period, condolence motions were passed by the House on the demise of former members of the Constituent Assembly and former members: Rishikesh Gautam, Mohammad Aftab Alam, Krishna Kumari Shrestha, Amod Prasad Upadhyay, Homnath Dahal, Baldev Bohara, and Ramchandra Bhattarai.

During this session, various honorable members sought Zero Hour, Special Time, and Urgent Time to draw the government's attention to contemporary issues.
